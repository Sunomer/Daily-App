Europe's leading distributor of sterile single use medical equipment for hospitals, clinics and health centers, as well as the pharmaceutical industry, is currently looking for a Sales Operations Specialist. The company is uniquely focused on improving the Healthcare Professionals safety at work, by providing the latest safety medical device innovations, as well as investing in research and development.
Role Description:
As a Logistic Specialist, you will manage the coordination, execution, and administration of logistics activities, working closely with logistics operators, the sales team, and internal clients to ensure the efficient and timely delivery of products to our customers.
Key Responsibilities:
Daily cooperation with logistics operator
Issuing documents according to the delivery plan to customers
Coordinating and ordering transport (road, rail, sea, air)
Pricing, organization and management of shipments to customers
Maintain and administer company inventory
Updating information about shipment status in the system
Daily cooperation with sales team and internal clients
Resolve problems that are related to transportation and logistics systems
Required Qualifications:
Experience in import & distribution operations
Experience in warehousing processes, custom clearance process as well as the customer support
Fluent English
Fluent Spanish/French – in plus
Good communication skills
Adaptability and flexibility to work in a global environment
What we offer:
Opportunity for career development in an international environment.
Private medical care.
Multisport card subsidy
Active participation in creating a very dynamic organization.
