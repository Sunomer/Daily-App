## Treść ogłoszenia
Auditor (Field Auditor / Kerja Kumpul Data)
Full-time
Career Site Team: Operations
Job Description
Key Responsibilities
Conduct in-store data collection and retail audits.
Recruit and maintain panel stores, distribute incentives, and build strong retailer relationships.
Support auditor hiring, training, supervision, and performance monitoring.
Handle ad-hoc requests, UC processes, and administrative tasks (cash advances, liquidation, etc.).
Ensure data quality by revisiting stores, troubleshooting issues, and supporting backend systems.
Collaborate with teams (MDQC, Data Ops, Data Sci, CSM) to uphold reporting integrity.
Contribute to team learning, share best practices, and support continuous improvement initiatives.
Data Quality Focus
Maintain accuracy and integrity of collected data.
Explain and validate data movements store-by-store.
Reinforce quality KPIs and resolve issues (coding errors, unclear manuals, category definitions).
Qualifications
Education:
SPM, STPM, Certificate, Diploma, or higher qualifications are welcome to apply.
2+ years’ experience in data collection or retail environment.
Strong communication skills in Bahasa Melayu and English required
High integrity, attention to detail, and ability to work under pressure.
Proficiency with smartphones, gadgets, and collaboration tools (Google, MS Office, Skype, etc.).
Willingness to travel, conduct fieldwork in varying conditions, and engage with shopkeepers.
Problem-solving mindset, coaching ability, and professional maturity.
Additional Qualities
Adaptability: Comfortable working in diverse environments, weather conditions, and store settings.
Resilience: Ability to handle repetitive tasks, long hours, and dynamic challenges in the field.
Analytical thinking: Skilled at identifying root causes of data issues and proposing improvements.
Collaboration: Strong team spirit, proactive in sharing knowledge and supporting colleagues.
Customer engagement: Capable of building trust and rapport with shopkeepers to ensure cooperation.
Initiative: Seeks opportunities for process improvement and efficiency gains.
Professional maturity: Maintains composure and effectiveness under pressure in a fast-paced environment.
