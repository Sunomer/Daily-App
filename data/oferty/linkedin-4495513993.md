The Impact You'll Make
As a
Scrum Master at Danfoss
, you will help globally distributed software teams work better together and deliver solutions that create real value for customers. You will support development, test automation, and DevOps teams working across different time zones in a
LeSS scaled Agile environment
.
This is more than a Scrum facilitation role. You will have the opportunity to influence
how software is developed and delivered
, helping teams remove obstacles, strengthen collaboration, and continuously improve their ways of working.
Working closely with another Scrum Master, the Product Owner, developers, and stakeholders, you will connect people across teams and help create an environment built on
ownership, transparency, and continuous improvement
.
The job shall be hybrid in one of our locations: Slovenia, Croatia, Bulgaria, Poland, Romania.
What You’ll Be Doing
Enable effective Agile delivery across distributed teams, helping development, test automation, and DevOps colleagues use Scrum, Kanban, and LeSS practices to deliver customer value.
Coach and support teams in becoming more self-organized and cross-functional, facilitating Sprint Planning, Daily Scrums, Sprint Reviews, and Retrospectives while helping remove obstacles that slow progress.
Strengthen collaboration across teams, working closely with another Scrum Master to manage dependencies, improve coordination, and share effective Agile practices.
Partner with the Product Owner and development teams to keep the Product Backlog clear and prioritized, while supporting the software development lifecycle from requirements through development and testing to production.
Drive transparency and continuous improvement through Azure DevOps, making progress and challenges visible and encouraging teams to challenge existing practices and turn ideas into practical improvements.
What We're Looking For
3+ years of experience as a Scrum Master in a software development environment, including experience supporting multiple, global teams. Scrum Master certification (e.g., CSM, PSM I/II) would be highly desirable.
Strong hands-on knowledge of Scrum and Kanban and experience working in a scaled Agile environment such as LeSS, SAFe, or Nexus.
A good understanding of the software development lifecycle, from experience or educational background, to collaborate effectively with developers, DevOps engineers, test automation specialists, Product Owners, and business stakeholders.
Experience using Azure DevOps, Jira, or a similar Agile delivery tool to support backlog management, delivery tracking, and team transparency.
Strong facilitation, coaching, communication, and problem-solving skills, with a collaborative approach and the confidence to challenge existing ways of working constructively.
Professional-level English and the ability to collaborate effectively with colleagues working across cultures and time zones.
