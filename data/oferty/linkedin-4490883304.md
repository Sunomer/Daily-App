What You’ll Do
The Supplier Development Engineer is responsible for developing supplier manufacturing capability, capacity, scalability, and operational readiness to support current and future business requirements. Leads structured supplier development activities to close manufacturing, process, capacity, and operational gaps and support business growth, new product launches, supplier transfers, and capacity expansion. Partners with Supplier Quality, Supply Chain, Engineering, Operations, COE, and suppliers to drive measurable and sustainable improvements in supplier performance.
Main Responsibilities
Supplier Development & Capability Improvement
Lead supplier development activities to identify and close manufacturing, process, capacity, and operational gaps. Establish action plans with clear objectives, ownership, timelines, and measurable outcomes.
Manufacturing & Process Capability
Assess supplier manufacturing processes and drive improvements using Lean methodologies, process capability analysis, structured problem solving, process controls, and advanced quality tools. Evaluate welding and joining processes, including qualifications, material controls, critical parameters, equipment capability, inspection/NDT, traceability, validation, and change management.
Capacity & Scalability Readiness
Assess supplier capacity and production-rate readiness against current and future requirements. Identify constraints and drive mitigation, recovery, or expansion plans.
Growth, Launch & Transfer Readiness
Develop supplier readiness for new product introductions, supplier transfers, localization, volume increases, and capacity expansion. Identify risks early and drive mitigation before operational impact.
Technical Supplier Recovery
Lead supplier development activities where quality, delivery, or operational issues result from manufacturing, process, capacity, or systemic capability gaps. Partner with Supplier Quality and cross-functional teams in line with RASIC responsibilities.
Supplier Assessment & Risk Identification
Participate in Supplier Site Assessments and risk-based reviews. Evaluate manufacturing capability, process controls, capacity, and operational readiness and drive actions to close identified gaps.
APQP & Supplier Readiness
Support APQP and supplier readiness activities by evaluating manufacturing capability, capacity, scalability, and operational risks and driving required development actions.
Continuous Improvement & Problem Solving
Coach suppliers on Lean, 8D, root-cause analysis, process capability, material management, auditing, and load and capacity planning to achieve sustainable performance improvement.
Cross-Functional Execution
Partner with Supplier Quality, Supply Chain, Engineering, Operations, COE, and other stakeholders to address critical supplier situations based on business risk and technical requirements.
Verification & Sustainability
Define measurable success criteria and verify that improvement actions are effective, objectives are achieved, and supplier capability is sustained before closing development activities.
