The role
In this role, you will
design, build, and lead the future end-to-end Order to Cash (O2C) organization and capabilities for TMICC
, covering the full journey from Customer Master Data and order capture through billing, collections, and cash.
You will own global O2C process and service performance across GBS, with a clear focus on
protecting revenue, accelerating cash, optimizing working capital, and reducing cost to serve
. A critical part of the role will be leading the relevant demerger and TSA exit activities, ensuring business continuity throughout transition and cutover.
As a senior transformation leader, you will combine deep commercial and finance operations expertise with digital capabilities, data-driven insights, and a customer-first mindset to build a standardized, scalable, well-controlled, and digitally enabled O2C organization.
What you'll be responsible for
Define and design the end-to-end global Order to Cash operating model, covering Customer Master Data, Credit Management, Order Management & Customer Service, Pricing & Billing, Trade Claims & Deductions, Cash Application, Collections & Dispute Management, and AR Accounting & Reconciliation.
Partner with Sales/Customer Development, Finance, Supply Chain, IT, ERP, Master Data Management, Treasury, and other functions to design and implement an integrated O2C model.
Lead the O2C Global Process Owners and Experts, driving process standardization, automation, effective controls, and continuous improvement through a single global O2C process model.
Act as the key O2C point of contact for business and functional stakeholders, particularly Sales/Customer Development, Finance, Treasury, and Supply Chain.
Lead the successful exit of relevant Transition Service Agreements (TSAs) associated with the TMICC demerger, delivering against critical deadlines while protecting order intake, invoicing, and cash collection.
Own O2C cash and working capital outcomes, partnering with Finance and Treasury to improve DSO, reduce overdue balances and bad debt, strengthen cash forecasting, and manage customer credit risk.
Partner with Customer Development and Finance on customer trade terms, pricing accuracy, trade promotion claims, and deductions, minimizing revenue leakage and unresolved deductions.
Establish and maintain a robust internal control environment across O2C, including financial reporting controls, segregation of duties, revenue recognition, AR reconciliation, statutory invoicing, e-invoicing, and tax requirements.
Develop and execute the global O2C strategy to deliver customer experience excellence, business agility, simplicity, and operational efficiency.
Design, build, recruit, and lead high-performing O2C teams across GBS global and regional hubs, fostering a strong customer-first and continuous-improvement culture.
Lead digital transformation across O2C, driving the deployment and adoption of technologies such as SAP S/4HANA and FSCM, AR automation, EDI and e-invoicing, customer self-service capabilities, and AI-enabled cash application and collections.
Oversee global service delivery, including third-party providers, ensuring consistent delivery against agreed SLAs and KPIs.
Drive performance across critical O2C measures such as DSO, overdue balances, cash application auto-match rates, invoice accuracy, deduction resolution cycle time, and cost to serve.
Leverage data, advanced analytics, and customer feedback to generate actionable insights, enable proactive decision-making, and identify service innovation opportunities.
Build strong relationships with markets and business functions, acting as a strategic liaison between GBS and the wider organization.
Contribute to the GBS Leadership Team and transformation roadmap, influencing global operating models, process harmonization, new capability deployment, and continuous improvement.
Own relevant documentation, process assets, tools, governance, and controls.
Manage the function's budget and costs, ensuring resources are effectively deployed against strategic priorities.
