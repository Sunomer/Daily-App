What You’ll Do
Define tools, processes & layout in the warehouse
Propose investment and participate to the budget construction
Identify suppliers for equipment requirements (with support of the sourcing team)
Create the warehouse instructions and train the team (new process/tools)
Lead the qualification for the new process/equipment
Apply HSE rules and instructions when defining processes
Support the warehouse team for the daily operations, identify the gaps and propose
corrective actions (suppliers claim, inventory discrepancy)

procurement / Logistics Specialist /Production
