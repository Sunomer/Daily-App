Job Description
We are seeking a talented Mobile App Developer with Kotlin expertise to join our growing mobile development team. As mobile-first solutions become increasingly critical to our client delivery, we need a skilled developer who can design, build, and maintain high-quality Android applications and contribute to cross-platform development initiatives. This is an excellent opportunity to work on diverse projects across consulting environments, leveraging Kotlin Multiplatform to expand reach and efficiency.
Key Responsibilities
Design and develop robust Android applications using Kotlin, following best practices and architectural patterns
Implement and maintain Firebase services
Develop and maintain cross-platform solutions using Kotlin Multiplatform, maximizing code reusability across platforms
Enhance and optimize existing mobile solutions, identifying improvement opportunities and implementing technical enhancements
Collaborate with product managers, designers, and backend developers to translate requirements into scalable mobile solutions
Write clean, maintainable code with comprehensive testing and documentation
Participate in code reviews and contribute to continuous improvement of our development processes
Support clients in understanding mobile best practices and GDS service standards where applicable
Troubleshoot and resolve technical issues, optimizing app performance and user experience
Contribute to architectural decisions and evaluate emerging technologies
Qualifications
Essential:
Proven experience developing production Android applications with Kotlin
Hands-on experience with Kotlin Multiplatform development
Hands-on experience with Google Firebase
Experience enhancing and optimizing existing mobile solutions
Strong understanding of mobile development principles and UI/UX considerations
Desirable:
Familiarity with GDS Service Standard and government digital services
Background working in consultancy or client-facing environments
