Data Integrity Specialist I
What You’ll Do At Elekta
As a Data Integrity Specialist I, you will help keep Elekta's supply chain data accurate, synchronized and reliable. You will investigate and resolve data discrepancies, work with colleagues across functions to find root causes, and improve the reports, tools and processes that support our daily operations. This role is a strong fit for someone who enjoys solving problems, takes ownership and is curious about how systems and data work.
Responsibilities
Investigate and resolve data discrepancies and synchronization errors across inventory, orders, item master data and system integrations.
Take ownership of issues from initial analysis through resolution, collaborating with Supply Chain, Operations, Data Operations, IT and other partners when needed.
Monitor alerts, recurring errors and key data checks to identify risks early and maintain stable operations.
Run and interpret regular reports, dashboards and analytical checks, and clearly communicate findings and exceptions.
Correct data and apply approved system updates in line with defined controls, procedures and access rights.
Document solutions and keep working instructions, process flows and team knowledge up to date.
Improve and automate reports, tools and routines to make daily work simpler, faster and more reliable.
Contribute to cross-functional projects, process improvements and knowledge sharing within the team.
What You’ll Bring
Strong written and spoken English, with confidence working in an international environment
University degree or equivalent experience in data, supply chain, business, technology or a related field
Minimum 1 year of professional experience, preferably in an international company
Advanced Excel skills (formulas, lookups, pivot tables, filters, large datasets) and good knowledge of Microsoft 365; basic SQL knowledge
Strong analytical skills, attention to detail and a structured approach to investigating discrepancies, identifying root causes and solving problems
Ownership, initiative and curiosity, with a genuine willingness to learn and strong communication and collaboration skills across teams and countries
