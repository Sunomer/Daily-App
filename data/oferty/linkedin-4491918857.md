With over 200 years of tradition and excellence, we are a proud Japanese family-owned company and one of the most innovative brands in the global pet food industry. Our premium products set standards in quality, taste, and animal well-being. As part of our continued growth across Europe, we are looking for an experienced and motivated Customer Operations Assistant to support our operations and help us deliver an outstanding customer experience. If you enjoy managing customer relationships, coordinating and improving processes, and working in an international environment where no two days are the same, we'd love to hear from you.
Your mission:
As our Customer Operations Assistant, you will ensure smooth order management and delivery processes, support excellent customer service, and coordinate effectively with internal teams to drive operational efficiency in Poland.
Manage customer orders throughout the entire order-to-delivery process, ensuring accurate processing, timely fulfilment, delivery coordination, and follow-up on outstanding orders and customer requests.
Handle customer inquiries and provide professional, timely updates while coordinating customer requirements, order changes, product availability, and delivery information with Sales and relevant internal departments.
Provide operational support to the Sales team and maintain accurate customer master data, order information, and related documentation.
Monitor and manage 3PL performance through KPIs, ensuring cost transparency, operational efficiency, and effective collaboration with the local warehouse.
Identify and resolve operational issues within the scope of responsibility, escalating complex matters where necessary, and proactively identify opportunities to improve customer service, streamline processes, and optimize costs.
Contribute to the continuous improvement of operational processes and Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), including supporting digitalization and process automation initiatives.
Support operational reporting and KPI tracking, ensure accurate records and timely follow-up, and maintain compliance with company policies, procedures, and quality standards.
Your profile:
You are proactive, customer-focused, and hands-on, with a strong attention to detail and a focus on clear communication and operational efficiency.
Bachelor's degree or equivalent qualification in Business Administration, Supply Chain Management, Logistics, or a related field.
2–3 years of experience in Customer Service, Customer Operations, Sales Support, Order Management, or Supply Chain.
Experience with ERP systems and Microsoft Office; familiarity with KPI reporting and data management.
Good understanding of order fulfilment, logistics, and customer service processes.
Experience in an international business environment; experience in the food or FMCG industry is an advantage.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with a customer-focused and collaborative mindset.
Excellent organizational skills, attention to detail, and the ability to manage multiple priorities.
Proactive, hands-on, and solution-oriented approach, with a strong sense of ownership and accountability.
Ability to work effectively across different departments and cultures.
Fluent in English and Polish. Knowledge of other European languages is a plus
Based in Poland, preferably in Warsaw.
Willingness to travel regularly to Wrocław
