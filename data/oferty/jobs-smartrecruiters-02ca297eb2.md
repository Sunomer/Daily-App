Job Description
About the role
Join Indirect Procurement and help shape how Sobi sources the services that keep our business running efficiently, compliantly and with strong commercial discipline. As our Strategic Sourcing & Operational Excellence Manager, you will own the vendor relationship from a procurement perspective, working closely with business owners to align on needs, priorities and outcomes. You will manage the vendor relationship end to end for Global
outsourced services within the Life Science area, from sourcing and contracting through performance, risk and continuous improvement, covering significant annual spend and real strategic impact.
You will build category strategies, multi-year roadmaps and supplier partnerships that deliver measurable value, cost efficiency and lower risk. You will also drive operational excellence and transformation across the procurement process, using data, digital tools and best-practice ways of working to raise the maturity and impact of the whole function.
This is a Hybrid role, joining a team located at our Headquarter in Stockholm. You will report to our Head of Indirect Procurement.
What you will do
You will be accountable for your outsourced services, from strategy and supplier performance to stakeholder alignment, risk and continuous improvement. In practice, you will:
Develop and execute partner strategies with senior stakeholders that support business objectives, optimize cost and create long-term value.
Run complex, high-value initiatives from market analysis and supplier selection through negotiation, contracting and performance management.
Drive operational excellence by standardizing ways of working, strengthening governance and compliance, and increasing transparency and efficiency, from Source-to-Pay to automation and digital enablement.
Use analytics and tools such as ServiceNow and Power BI to track performance and value, and own savings and KPI reporting.
Lead Supplier Relationship Management (SRM) programs for strategic suppliers, covering governance, executive engagement, performance reviews, risk, compliance and sustainability.
Be a trusted advisor to senior stakeholders across functions and geographies, offering commercial insight and pragmatic recommendations.
Mentor and coach colleagues, sharing best practices and building a culture of accountability, collaboration and continuous improvement.
Qualifications
About you
To succeed in this role, you combine strategic thinking with strong execution and a commercial, value-focused mindset. You are proactive, structured and resilient, comfortable with ambiguity and keen to challenge existing ways of working. You build trust quickly, communicate with clarity and influence senior stakeholders through fact-based, pragmatic recommendations. Curious and adaptable, you are motivated by creating scalable, efficient and sustainable ways of working.
What you bring
8+ years of experience in strategic procurement, vendor and category management, with responsibility for the full sourcing lifecycle and measurable business value.
A strong track record of leading complex sourcing projects, supplier negotiations and high-value contracts in a global or matrixed environment.
Operational excellence experience: driving process improvement, transformation or change management within procurement, supply chain or a related function.
A data-driven approach: you use KPIs, spend data and digital tools to spot opportunities, make recommendations and track progress.
Business acumen - strategic and practical thinking, with a solid understanding of commercial and legal contract terms, supplier governance and risk management.
Strong communication, influencing and project management skills, with the ability to engage senior leadership and lead cross-functional initiatives.
Experience with AI tools, Excel and PowerPoint, fluent English, and a relevant university degree or equivalent experience.
A plus
A background in pharma or management consulting
Experience with ServiceNow, Power BI or similar analytics and workflow tools
