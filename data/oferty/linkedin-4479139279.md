About The Role
DXC Technology is seeking an experienced Procurement Workstream Lead to drive the SAP MM Procurement stream within a large-scale Supply Chain Management (SCM) deployment. This role is central to ensuring robust SAP MM Procurement configuration, high delivery quality, and seamless integration with EWM, Finance, and Planning workstreams across Central and EMEA deployments.
As the Procurement Workstream Lead, you will own the end-to-end delivery of procurement processes, from design and configuration through testing and cutover, ensuring alignment with the global SCM template. You will work closely with business stakeholders, consultants, and cross-functional workstreams to deliver a high-quality, integrated procurement solution that supports the broader transformation programme.
This is an excellent opportunity for a seasoned SAP MM professional to take ownership of a critical workstream in a major transformation initiative, leveraging deep functional expertise and strong stakeholder management skills to drive successful outcomes across multiple regions.
Essential Job Functions
Lead the Procurement workstream delivery covering SAP MM purchasing, inventory management, and invoice verification for the SCM deployment
Design and configure S/4HANA Procurement processes aligned to the global SCM template
Facilitate fit-gap workshops with procurement and operations business stakeholders
Manage S4 MM Consultant deliverables and conduct quality reviews
Coordinate procurement integration testing with EWM, Finance, and PP workstreams
Drive procurement testing strategy, cutover activities, and vendor master data migration
Ensure delivery quality and integration across Central and EMEA deployment regions
Qualifications & Experience
SAP MM Procurement implementation leadership experience with SCM or manufacturing deployment context
Expert knowledge of S/4HANA Purchasing, Inventory Management, and Invoice Verification
Experience leading procurement workstreams in large SCM transformation programmes
Integration knowledge with EWM, PP, FI, and MDG for procurement flows
Strong stakeholder management and cross-functional coordination skills
Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Business, or IT
Nice To Have
SAP S/4HANA Procurement certification
Manufacturing or distribution procurement implementation experience
