About The Job
As Product Analyst for Talent & Skills, you are accountable for the delivery momentum and operational coordination of the product team. You secure campaign execution, coordinate consultants and Chapter members, animate Agile delivery rituals and maintain the backlog management tool as the reliable source of delivery status.
You work closely with the Product Owner and Senior Product Analyst to turn validated and technically ready stories into well-tracked delivery activities. Your main contribution is to ensure that the team knows what needs to be done, by whom, by when, with which blockers, and with the right level of testing and documentation.
Key responsibilities
Delivery Coordination and Campaign Execution
Coordinate the operational delivery of Talent Management & Skills initiatives, enhancements and campaign related activities
Manage day-to-day follow-up of configuration work performed by consultants and Chapter members
Monitor workload, progress, risks, blockers, dependencies and quality of deliverables
Ensure that campaign preparation, configuration, testing, release and post-go-live activities are properly followed up
Escalate risks, delays or arbitration needs to the Senior Product Analyst or Product Owner when required Agile Rituals and Backlog Tool Management
Own the preparation, animation and follow-up of the team Daily
Maintain the backlog management tool, ensuring that stories, tasks, owners, statuses, priorities, dependencies and blockers are up to date
Ensure that the Kanban board or backlog management tool is used as the team’s single source of truth for delivery follow-up
Facilitate operational
Agile rituals such as Daily meetings, delivery follow-ups, retrospectives, sprint reviews and preparation sessions
Produce delivery visibility through progress updates, dashboards, action lists and risk trackers
Configuration Follow-up and Consultant Coordination
Coordinate consultants and Chapter members involved in SAP SuccessFactors configuration activities
Contribute to Talent screens configuration when needed
Track configuration progress and confirm alignment with validated requirements and technical guidance provided by the Senior Product Analyst
Support issue tracking, defect resolution and root-cause analysis by gathering facts, evidence and context
Ensure configuration outputs are documented and ready for testing, release and future maintenance
Testing, Documentation and Release Support
Prepare, coordinate and execute functional test scenarios where relevant
Support User Acceptance Testing by ensuring clear test scope, test evidence, defect follow-up and stakeholder communication
Maintain business requirements traceability, test evidence, release notes and operational documentation
Contribute to demos and delivery sessions for new features, enhancements and campaign readiness
Operational Communication
Ensure smooth operational communication between consultants, Chapter members, the Product Owner, the Senior Product Analyst and HR stakeholders
Provide clear updates on delivery status, next steps, blockers and required decisions
Help maintain a structured and transparent delivery cadence across the product team
About You
We expect you to have
Experience
Minimum 2 to 3 years of experience in HRIS, HR Technology, Talent Management or a Product Analyst role
Experience coordinating consultants, technical contributors or configuration activities
Experience supporting HR campaigns, recurring HR processes or SAP SuccessFactors delivery is a strong asset

Good knowledge of SAP SuccessFactors Talent modules or strong willingness to build advanced configuration understanding
Ability to configure Talent screens, at least standard features
Strong coordination, follow-up, documentation and delivery tracking skills
Experience with Agile rituals, Daily meetings, Kanban boards and backlog management tools.
Ability to work with consultants, technical teams and business stakeholders in a structured and reliable way
Attention to detail, operational rigor and problem-solving mindset
