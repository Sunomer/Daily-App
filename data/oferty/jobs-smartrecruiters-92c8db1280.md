Job Description
Take ownership of complex software projects spanning Sports, Martech and Player Account Management as a Project Manager in Denver.
The Project and Programme Delivery function manages software development projects across multiple business and platform areas. This Project Manager will own delivery from initiation to closure, with an initial focus on Sports and scope across Martech and Player Account Management.
Working with development, testing and leadership stakeholders in Denver, the UK and Europe, the role may coordinate concurrent work across approximately 5 to 15 teams. Projects can run for 6 to 24 months or longer, requiring clear planning, controlled change and confident stakeholder management.
This role is eligible for inclusion in our hybrid work from home policy. The working arrangement is 3 days per week in the Denver office.
