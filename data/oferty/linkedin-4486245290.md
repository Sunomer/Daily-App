Company Description
Turka Invest Sp z o.o. is a leading European producer of tortillas, operating five modern factories in Poland and Spain with a total footprint of 20,000 m².
Another production facility will be launched in Egypt in the near future.
The company focuses on innovation, continually investing in new projects, machinery, and production lines to support its growth. Turka Tortilla regularly updates its production technologies to maintain high quality and efficiency. The organization is committed to meeting global demand for tortillas while offering a dynamic, forward-looking work environment for its team members. Currently we are looking for a Senior Planning Specialist.
Role Description
Manage inventory policies for critical materials to ensure availability for production while keeping inventory and working capital under control.
Set and regularly review planning parameters, including safety stock, order quantities, lead times and replenishment rules.
Identify material shortage risks and agree on actions with Purchasing and Production Planning.
Implement and configure MRP in Asprova APS together with your manager, with support from IT and the system provider. This includes defining requirements, preparing data, testing and go-live.
Maintain data quality and consistent planning parameters across ERP and APS.
Guide and coach the planner responsible for category B and C materials — help set priorities, solve problems and support their development.
Meet DIO targets while ensuring material availability for production.
Actively prevent excess and obsolete inventory (E&O). Find and implement solutions to use or reduce existing surplus stock and limit losses.
Qualifications
Experience in independently managing material planning in a manufacturing company.
Practical knowledge of MRP and inventory management, including ABC/XYZ analysis, safety stock and replenishment parameters.
Experience in implementing or improving planning processes in ERP or APS systems.
Ability to analyse data and turn findings into practical decisions.
Ability to share knowledge, give feedback and support other planners.
Good Excel skills and the ability to communicate effectively in English.
Optional
Knowledge of Asprova APS, Microsoft Dynamics 365 Business Central or Power BI.
Experience with VMI (Vendor Managed Inventory) or consignment stock.
