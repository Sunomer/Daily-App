As DHL, we are a world leader in supply chain management and support our customers in developing their business. DHL employs almost 600 000 people. It works in more than 220 countries and regions around the world, connecting people and businesses safely and reliably, enabling a global trade flow. Our employees daily deal with the delivery of the best logistics solutions and service the largest warehouses in Poland and around the world for various industries (including e-commerce, technology, life sciences and healthcare, energy, automotive and retail).
Within LLP business (Lead Logistics Partner) we deliver 4PL (Fourth-Party Logistics) services for our European and global customers. Within this framework, we are represented by approx. 940 employees at fifteen locations in 10 countries in Europe. Are you motivated to make our LLP division become even better? Then we should get to know each other. Become part of an international network and benefit from a variety of advantages!

Responsibilities:
Define and execute the regional Business Development strategy for Transport and 4PL/LLP, with Europe as the core market,
Lead major, high-value opportunities, from commercial strategy and solution shaping through negotiation and contracting,
Build trusted relationships with senior customer stakeholders, driving growth across strategic accounts,
Identify new growth opportunities through market, sector and competitor analysis,
Own the commercial performance of the Business Development and Account Management portfolio, with a strong focus on revenue growth, profitability and sustainable financial performance,
Lead and develop an international team across Business Development, Account Management and Transport,
Drive collaboration across commercial and operational teams to align customer opportunities with business capabilities,
Partner with regional and global Transport, LLP/4PL and functional leaders to deliver commercially viable solutions,
Represent DHL Supply Chain externally, bringing customer and market insights into the future development of the business.
Expectations:
Minimum 7 years of senior Business Development, Sales or Commercial experience, including significant hands-on experience within the 4PL/LLP environment,
Significant leadership experience, including leading, coaching and developing geographically dispersed and multicultural teams,
Strong commercial acumen, with experience shaping complex commercial models, value propositions and large-scale customer solutions,
Proven success in delivering revenue growth, profitability and securing high-value, complex customer contracts,
Strong understanding of complex contracting, commercial risk and legal considerations within large-scale customer agreements.
Proven ability to operate effectively within a complex regional and global matrix and influence senior stakeholders across multiple functions,
Experience managing strategic global accounts and building trusted relationships with C-level customer stakeholders,
Strong strategic mindset, with the ability to identify new market opportunities, assess industry trends and contribute to future product and service development,
Willingness to travel frequently for business, approximately 70%+ of the year, depending on the region,
Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field and fluency in English; additional European languages would be an advantage.
