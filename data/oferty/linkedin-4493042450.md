Job Description
Expect more. Connect more. Be more at Diebold Nixdorf.
Join a global leader transforming how people bank and shop. As a partner to top financial institutions and retailers, we connect millions of consumers to commerce every day.
Location: Warsaw (hybrid mode with 3 days working at the office).
Responsibilities
As Field Service Dispatcher, you’ll be the go-to link between customers, field teams, and internal operations. You’ll coordinate appointments, monitor schedules, and keep everything running smoothly. From managing job pools and optimizing routes to dispatching technicians and ensuring SLAs, you’ll make sure every commitment is met efficiently. Your role is all about smart scheduling, quick problem-solving, and delivering exceptional customer experience.
Responsibilities:
Support dispatch operations for your assigned region
Monitor technician status and schedules to keep jobs on track
Manage job pools, fill gaps, and reassign work to meet customer commitments
Optimize routes for efficiency and on-time performance
Handle appointment confirmations, rescheduling, and customer inquiries
Update calendars and adjust assignments based on skills and priorities
Anticipate obstacles and find prompt solutions
Collaborate with your team and share feedback for continuous improvement.
Qualifications
Education or equivalent practical experience
2–4 years of experience in Field Service Dispatch or a similar role. Candidates with a background in customer service or administrative support are also encouraged to apply. Dispatching experience is preferred but not mandatory.
Strong communication skills in German language, plus solid English.
