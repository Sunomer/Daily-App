Your Responsibilities
Managing and developing the warehouse team to ensure smooth and efficient execution of daily operations.
Supervising material flow, storage processes, inventory accuracy, and on-time delivery performance.
Initiating and implementing optimization activities in the areas of safety, quality, productivity, and cost efficiency.
Closely cooperating with Production, Logistics, and Quality departments to ensure operational excellence.
Ensuring warehouse processes comply with internal company standards and customer requirements.
Actively supporting digitalization initiatives and continuous improvement projects within warehouse operations.
Who We Are Looking For
We encourage applications from candidates who
Demonstrate strong leadership and communication skills.
Are motivated by challenges and eager to take on new responsibilities that support professional growth.
Have a continuous improvement mindset and actively seek opportunities to optimize processes.
Show a high level of accountability, engagement, and a hands-on approach.
Are ready to take a leadership role and make a tangible impact in a dynamic automotive environment.
Requirements
Bachelor's degree in Logistics, Economics, Supply Chain Management, or a related field.
Minimum 5 years of experience in warehouse management.
Previous experience in the automotive industry is required.
Ability to work effectively in an international environment.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
Proven experience in team leadership and people management.
Practical knowledge of modern warehouse management techniques and tools, preferably in the automotive sector.
Results-driven with a strong business orientation.
Good knowledge of SAP.
Very good command of English (mandatory).
