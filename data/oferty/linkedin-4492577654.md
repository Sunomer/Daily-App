Job Summary
The Control, Process & Governance Specialist strengthens the COO function’s control environment by designing, governing, and continuously improving end-to-end processes, operational controls, and risk governance routines.
The role ensures key COO activities are resilient, well-controlled, audit-ready, and aligned with regulatory expectations, while enabling process efficiency through standardisation, automation, and data-driven monitoring. Acting as a connector across COO, Risk, Compliance, Technology, Audit and business stakeholders, this role drives control effectiveness, issue remediation, and sustainable operating discipline.
Key Responsibilities
Design, implement and maintain fit-for-purpose processes and control frameworks that support safe, efficient, and compliant delivery of business activities
Translate policy, regulatory, and governance requirements into practical operational processes embedded within business workflows
Ensure controls are proportionate, clearly defined, and aligned to business activities, risk exposure and control standards
Establish and maintain governance frameworks, standards, and operating procedures that promote consistency, transparency, and accountability
Support the effective application of governance standards across functions, including documentation, approvals, and decision-making forums
Enable clear ownership and escalation pathways for process, control and governance matters
Implement continuous control monitoring approaches and automate evidence capture where possible
Develop dashboards / MI for control effectiveness, issue ageing, incident trends, and operational performance
Support oversight and assurance activities by providing clear evidence, reporting, and analysis on control design and operation
Partner with stakeholders to remediate control issues and strengthen process effectiveness where required
Drive continuous improvement in processes, controls, and governance practices to enhance efficiency, effectiveness, and resilience
Leverage insights from incidents, reviews, and operational feedback to strengthen frameworks and prevent recurrence
Promote disciplined execution and consistent application of controls across the organisation
Act as a trusted partner to COO leaders and operational teams, translating governance expectations into day-to-day execution
Drive communications, training and adoption plans to embed new standards, controls and process changes
Influence cross-functional stakeholders (Risk/Compliance/Technology/Audit) and major change programmes to align on risk outcomes and practical implementation
Qualifications
Experience in designing and embedding effective processes, controls and governance to support safe, compliant business delivery
Experience in translating regulatory and policy requirements into practical, auditable ways of working
Being able to to improve control and process effectiveness through data, insight and cross‑functional collaboration
Experience in Financial Risk Management, Stakeholder Management, Cross Functional Collaboration, Change Management and Regulatory Compliance
