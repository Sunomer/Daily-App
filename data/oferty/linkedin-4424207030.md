We are seeking a dynamic and strategic leader to serve as the Director, Indirect Procurement & Facilities. This role is responsible for overseeing the global strategy, operations and performance of all company’s facilities and indirect procurement functions across the organization. The ideal candidate will bring a strong background in operational excellence, vendor management, sustainability and cost optimization, while also leading cross-functional strategic initiatives driving efficiency and innovation.
Responsibilities:
Indirect Procurement
Develop and execute a global indirect procurement strategy aligned with business goals
Manage sourcing, contract negotiation, supplier performance, and risk mitigation
Negotiate contracts and terms with vendors
Analyze procurement data to identify cost-saving opportunities
Optimize indirect procurement processes and systems to ensure cost-effectiveness and transparency
Define and govern compliance with the internal company’s policies
Build strong relationships with both key vendors and internal stakeholders
Facilities Management
Lead the global facilities strategy: ensuring safe, efficient, and sustainable workplaces across all company locations
Oversee office design, space planning, lease management, and workplace services
Implement global standards and best practices for facility operations and maintenance
Ensure compliance with health, safety, and environmental regulations
Requirements:
Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Supply Chain, Engineering or related field; MBA or equivalent - advantage
10+ years of consecutive leadership experience in indirect procurement, facilities’ management, and similar types of operations in a global environment
Global experience in regions like : EMEA, APAC & LATAM
Proven track record of managing large-scale, cross-functional strategic projects
Strong analytical, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills
Experience with procurement and facilities management systems and tools
Excellent communication and leadership capabilities
English at a mother-tongue level. Other languages – advantage
