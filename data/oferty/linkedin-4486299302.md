Associate Manager, Customer Supply Chain
Location: Poland (Warsaw) or Czech Republic (Prague)
Function: Supply Chain
About The Role
This role will drive the implementation of efficient supply chain services with key customers, to achieve preferred supplier status and deliver against SCJ growth and profit objectives. You’ll be the primary liaison between our customers and SCJ Product Supply functions in relation to supply chain opportunities. Overall, you’ll be expected to implement sustainable, cost effective, supply chain processes while ensuring our customers' compliance with SCJ's Customer Operations Metrics.
This role is primarily focused on the supply chain that is shared between SCJ and its customers, setting up optimal order to cash processes and reverse logistics optimization.
We do not offer international relocation support for this role
Responsibilities
Executes best route to market and cost-to serve supply chain processes for SCJ and our customers; accountable for the supply chain initiatives that drive improved effectiveness
Develops and agrees annual plan or contract negotiation in line with SCJ & customer strategy to create value and reduce cost. Owns & leads Supply Chain Customer Collaboration agenda with customers
Partners with Sales, Demand Planning, Customer Fulfilment and Logistics to execute against strategic customer development opportunities and deliver Advantage Survey results
Develops and deploys analysis and scorecards for communication to the customer and key cross-functional areas to improve supply chain compliance and effectiveness, applying course corrective actions as needed
Acts as the escalation point of contact in day-to-day business with each assigned customer related to Supply Chain efficiency
Supports joint forecasting (Promo, NPD, Displays), both internally and with customers as defined by cross-functionally (supply chain, commercial, finance) aligned process
Manage the Seasonal return and reverse logistics process with close coordination with BIM, Sales and Logistics Teams.
Experience You’ll Bring
Cost to Serve optimization mindset
Proven experience in Collaborative Supply Chain related fields
University Degree Educated or equivalent
Able to represent Supply Chain internally and externally in a professional manner, both strategically and tactically
Advanced skills in Excel required, SAP preferred and the ability to learn and utilize customer systems and data
There is an expectation of travel to Customers’ offices for meetings
English and Polish or Czech is required
Behaviours You’ll Need
Demonstrated analytical thinker, skilled in problem solving and project management, assertive yet collaborative and team-oriented leadership style being able to influence at all levels
The ability to build business partnerships and external collaboration opportunities wherever possible through excellent negotiation skills
Proactive, decisional, goal and results oriented, able to manage priorities and remove barriers
Have a passion for Customer Service and Joint Value Creation
