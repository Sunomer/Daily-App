Legal Counsel
Sogecap S.A. oddział w Polsce
We are the Polish branches of leading French insurance companies operating in both life and non-life insurance sectors. As part of an international financial group, we combine local expertise with global standards to deliver high-quality insurance solutions to our customers.
Our legal function plays a strategic role in supporting business growth, regulatory compliance and innovation. Joining our team means working closely with senior management, international stakeholders and experts across multiple disciplines in a dynamic and highly regulated environment.
We offer the opportunity to contribute to key business projects, gain exposure to international legal and regulatory matters, and make a tangible impact on the development of our organization.
Legal Counsel
Miejsce pracy: Wrocław
Tasks:
Providing day-to-day legal support to the Polish branches of international insurance companies operating in life and non-life business.
Advising on the development and implementation of new insurance products and business initiatives, ensuring compliance with applicable laws, regulations and internal procedures.
Drafting, reviewing and negotiating legal documentation, including insurance policies, general terms and conditions, distribution agreements, outsourcing agreements and other commercial contracts.
Assessing legal and regulatory risks related to new products, services and business projects.
Supporting claims and complaints functions through legal analysis, preventive advice and litigation management.
Advising stakeholders on Polish insurance law, civil law, commercial law, labour law and regulatory matters.
Monitoring legal and regulatory developments in Poland and the European Union, assessing their impact on the business.
Cooperating with external counsel, regulatory authorities, notaries and courts when required.
Developing and implementing legal policies, procedures and controls within the organization.
Contributing to governance, compliance and permanent supervision activities.
Acting as Data Protection Officer (DPO) for the Polish branches.
Advising on GDPR compliance, privacy risk assessments, outsourcing arrangements and data protection by design.
Serving as the main point of contact for data protection authorities and data subjects.
Our requirements:
Qualified Attorney-at-Law (Radca Prawny) or Advocate (Adwokat) authorized to practice in Poland.
Minimum 3 years of professional experience in a legal role, preferably within the insurance, financial services or regulated industry sector.
Strong knowledge of Polish insurance law, civil law, commercial law and regulatory frameworks applicable to financial institutions.
Practical experience in drafting and negotiating commercial and insurance-related agreements.
Solid understanding of GDPR and Polish data protection regulations.
Proven ability to provide pragmatic legal advice while balancing business objectives and regulatory requirements.
Excellent analytical, communication and stakeholder management skills.
Ability to work independently and effectively cooperate with international teams and external advisors.
Fluent Polish and English, both written and spoken.
Proficiency in Microsoft Office applications.
Optional:
Experience gained within an insurance company, insurance intermediary (broker or agent) or financial institution.
Experience working in an international environment and cooperating with global legal teams.
Recognized data protection certification, such as CIPP/E.
Experience acting as a Data Protection Officer or supporting DPO activities.
Knowledge of Solvency II, IDD and insurance distribution regulations.
Familiarity with corporate governance and compliance frameworks in regulated entities.
French language skills would be an asset.
We offer:
Opportunity to work in an international environment within leading French insurance companies.
A key role combining legal advisory responsibilities with Data Protection Officer duties.
Exposure to a broad range of legal matters, including insurance, commercial, corporate, labour and data protection law.
Close cooperation with senior management, business stakeholders and international legal teams.
Participation in strategic business projects and product development initiatives.
High level of autonomy and real impact on business decisions.
Professional development opportunities, including specialized legal and data protection training.
Hybrid working model. (1 day of remote working and 4 days working from office per week)
Competitive salary and comprehensive benefits package.
Friendly and collaborative working environment focused on knowledge sharing and continuous improvement.
Additional days off (after one year in the company – 1 additional day of holidays; after 3 years in the company – 3 additional days of holidays; after 5 years in the company – 4 additional days of holidays; after 10 years in the company – 5 additional days of holidays; additional 16 hours in a year for charity activities; in addition the company is closed on 2nd of May and 31st of December)
Allegro Vouchers (Easter, Xmas)
Lunch card (450 pln monthly)
We kindly inform we will contact only selected candidates.
If you are interested, please send your application.
