E-Commerce Operations & Marketplace Lead, Korea
Full-time
Job Description
As E-commerce Operations & Marketplace Lead, you are responsible for reliable operations and continuous improvement of our Korean online channels, delivery of digital development projects, and key account management of marketplace partners. You drive marketplace selling and partner performance through analysis, commercial proposals and cross-functional execution, ensuring a consistent brand and customer experience. With our upcoming marketplace expansion and brand.com re-platforming, you will have the opportunity to play a key role in shaping and growing our digital business in Korea.
Responsibilities:
Website Operations & Customer
E
xperience
Coordinate regular site updates, product and content accuracy, campaign execution and quality assurance with Merchandising, Visual and Marketing.
Manage website incidents and recurring errors, follow up resolutions with Hyundai and other vendors, and coordinate order, customer-service, inventory, returns and settlement processes with operational partners.
Improve operating routines and escalation procedures.
Platform Improvements & Re-platforming
Coordinate website development priorities and the roadmap with Hyundai, global e-commerce/IT and other technology partners.
Prepare requirements and business cases, track milestones and dependencies, coordinate testing and launch readiness, and resolve post-launch issues.
Act as project manager for the Korean brand.com re-platforming, delivering against agreed scope, resources and priorities.
Marketplace Account
M
anagement
& Launches
Take ownership of commercial performance and partner relationships for Hyundai.com, 29CM and new marketplace channels to be launched soon.
Build partner relationships and develop account plans, sales forecasts and proposals for profitable growth.
Monitor sales, margin, stock and customer performance; identify opportunities and recommend commercial terms, assortment and buying needs, promotions, marketing visibility and stock actions.
Coordinate partner negotiations, launches, integration, product listings, campaigns, operational flows and settlement follow-up, and drive execution of agreed plans with Merchandising, Marketing, Logistics and Finance.
Data, Testing & Performance follow-up
Maintain operational analytics and reporting tools, dashboards and A/B testing.
Track channel performance, marketplace KPIs, product/content quality and customer experience.
Translate data into commercial actions to improve conversion, channel selling and profitability; follow up results and escalate risks with recommendations.
Cross-functional Collaboration & Standards
Work closely with Merchandising, Marketing/CRM, Visual, Finance, Logistics, customer service, regional/global teams and external partners.
Contribute commercial and operational input to marketplace, campaign, ranking and site priorities; coordinate execution, improve processes and ensure clear handovers.
Qualifications
Previous or current experience working in an international environment, with the ability to navigate cross-functional and multicultural stakeholders.
Strong business acumen, commercial mindset and entrepreneurial drive, with a proven track record of driving e-commerce growth and improving business performance.
Experience in e-commerce operations and marketplace key account management.
Strong project management skills, with experience coordinating digital development, platform improvements or marketplace launches.
Proven analytical capabilities, with the ability to translate sales, customer and market data into actionable commercial strategies and business decisions.
Strong stakeholder and partner management skills, with the ability to influence, proactively identify and resolve issues, and drive cross-functional collaboration.
Hands-on approach, strong sense of ownership, attention to detail and ability to prioritize and execute independently while taking accountability for business results.
Excellent verbal and written communication skills in both Korean and English, with the ability to effectively engage and collaborate with local and global stakeholders in an international business environment.
