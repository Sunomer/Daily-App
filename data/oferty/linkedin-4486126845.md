As a recruitment company, DCG understands that every business is powered by experienced professionals. Our management style and partnership approach enable us to meet your needs and provide continuous support. Due to our ongoing growth and the large number of recruitment projects we undertake for our partners, we are currently looking for:
Business Analyst
Responsibilities:
Models and documents value chains/streams, business processes, roles, capabilities, and data
Elicits and analyses business needs, requirements, and user stories
Supports business experts and product owners during the definition of requirements and software development
Maintains and tracks requirements/user stories throughout the systems lifecycle
Translates business, user, and technical needs into business requirements specifications
Maps business goals, project benefits, priorities, components, and system features
Analyses available systems and evaluates potential solutions
Supports identification of options for possible solutions
Assists in the preparation of business cases and feasibility studies
Contributes to systems design, testing, quality assurance, and change management
Designs, validates, and tests business processes to achieve business process excellence
Takes a holistic approach in planning and managing programmes for continuous improvement of business processes
Coaches staff on upcoming changes and ensures plans are in place to teach new skills and/or implement process changes
Collaborates with owners of end-to-end processes to continually identify opportunities for improvement
Identifies opportunities for business process optimization, leading to improved customer service, reduced costs, or increased revenue (effectiveness and efficiency gains)
Communicates project objectives, business cases, requirements, and scope to stakeholders to ensure alignment and rapid response to business needs
Conducts quantitative analysis of business processes, including cost-benefit and what-if scenarios
Plans and manages organizational change processes
Implements performance monitoring systems for business processes
Supports business users in developing strategic and tactical plans (e.g., sector strategies, implementation plans, etc.)
Supports the definition of transition roadmaps for business architectures
Requirements:
Minimum 8 years of relevant IT experience
Minimum 5 years in similar position
Bachelor's degree
At least two internationally recognized certifications in business analysis, such as CBAP, Certified UML Professional (Foundation Level), BPM 2 Fundamental Level, or equivalent certifications accepted by the Contracting Authority
English level: C1 confirmed by a certificate or be willing to take the exam
Has gone at least 1 time through the complete lifecycle of the software solution
Knowledge in business processes modelling using BPMN notation
Knowledge in requirements/user stories elicitation and analysis
Knows analysis/modelling notations like UML (capability diagram, activity diagram, state diagram, logical data model etc.)
Is proficient in using modelling tools like Sparx Enterprise Architect, Aris Architect or others
Presents excellent analytical and communication skills
Holds ability to create and maintain analytical and project documentation effectively
Presents problem solving skills
Knows how to develop a Business Architecture and its elements
Has experience in the role of Business Analyst in a project(s) aimed at business processes improvement or redesign
Has experience in the role of Business Analyst in a project(s) project aimed at analysis, design, implementation and deployment of ICT Solution that enabled (1) content production or (2) reporting tools or (3) analytical tools
Has experience working for/at the public sector, preferably in EU Institutions or International organisations
Offer:
Private medical care
Co-financing for the sports card
Constant support of dedicated consultant
Employee referral program
