We are looking for a Business Readiness Expert to join a strategic transformation program in the insurance sector. As part of a dynamic team, you will play a key role in developing a modern ecosystem for claims and benefits management that leverages data, automation, and AI. Your expertise in Business Readiness Management and Change Management will be crucial in preparing the organization for successful IT system implementations. If you are passionate about driving transformation and enabling change, this opportunity may be the right fit for you.
