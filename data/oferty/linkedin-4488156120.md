About The Team
You’ll be joining the JINGDONG Logistics team, which values collaboration, ownership, and a commitment to delivering high-quality results in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
The role primarily provides customer service within Europe for Chinese clients expanding into the European market. Key responsibilities include: 1. Maintaining and strengthening relationships with clients' European operations teams to ensure customer satisfaction and long-term partnership; 2. Serving as the liaison between clients and internal teams (warehousing, transportation, delivery, systems, etc.), coordinating resources to ensure timely response and resolution of client needs; 3. Conducting regular business reviews with clients' European operations teams to jointly improve operational processes and continuously enhance efficiency in daily operations and exception handling; 4. Operating business systems on behalf of clients, resolving order exceptions, verifying logistics tracking information, and handling abnormal incidents such as lost or damaged goods.
