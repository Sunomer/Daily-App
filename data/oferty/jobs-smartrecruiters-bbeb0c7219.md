Company Description
InPost Group is an innovative European out of home deliveries company, revolutionizing the way parcels are delivered to customers. With operations across several countries, our network of intelligent lockers provides customers with a fast, convenient, and secure delivery option. InPost Group is a publicly traded company, with a market capitalization of about $5 billion as of March 2023. With over 10,000 people worldwide, InPost Group is one of the largest out of home delivery providers in Europe, committed to providing sustainable and efficient delivery solutions to meet the evolving needs of customers in today's rapidly changing landscape.
We are looking for an ambitious and proactive
Engineering Program Manager
to join our
CTO Office
and help drive alignment, communication, and organisational effectiveness across a technology organisation of nearly 1,000 people. This role is ideal for someone who thrives in complex environments, enjoys working with senior stakeholders, and can turn ideas into action. We're looking for a highly organised and independent individual who can drive cross-functional initiatives, build alignment across teams, and leverage AI and automation to maximise impact.
Job Description
Drive organisation-wide standards, processes, and initiatives that improve the effectiveness of the Technology organisation and strengthen its culture.
Lead cross-functional programs from concept and leadership alignment through rollout, adoption, and continuous improvement.
Facilitate collaboration across multiple domains, bringing together stakeholders to align on shared goals, priorities, and ways of working.
Coordinate and support Tech-wide initiatives, working groups, workshops, and recurring organisational processes.
Shape and continuously improve information flow across the Technology organisation, ensuring employees receive relevant information through the appropriate channels.
Coordinate internal communications, updates, and events to drive transparency, alignment, and engagement across Tech.
Ensure new standards, practices, and organisational changes are effectively communicated, understood, and adopted by teams.
Track leadership decisions, commitments, and strategic initiatives, following up on actions and driving accountability across stakeholders.
Translate complex or ambiguous topics into clear plans, actionable next steps, ownership, and measurable outcomes.
Leverage AI tools, automation, and data-driven insights to improve efficiency, streamline processes, and support decision-making across the organisation.
Qualifications
3+ years of experience in program management, project management, organisational development, internal communications, or a similar role within a technology or complex organisational environment.
Strong understanding of how technology organisations operate, including engineering teams, delivery processes, organisational structures, and leadership dynamics.
Proven experience driving cross-functional initiatives and organisational processes from design through successful adoption.
Experience facilitating workshops, working groups, and stakeholder discussions across different teams and seniority levels.
Ability to influence outcomes and drive change without formal authority.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to tailor messages for different audiences.
Strong organisational skills with a structured, execution-focused approach to problem-solving.
Comfortable working with data and using tools such as Excel or Power BI to track progress and generate insights.
Fluent English and Polish.
Hands-on experience using AI tools to improve productivity, automate workflows, and support communication, reporting, or knowledge-sharing activities.
