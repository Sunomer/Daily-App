At DSV, we keep global supply chains moving, and technology plays a key role in how we support our operations, customers, and people around the world.
We are looking for a
Senior AI Engineer
to join our
AI Language Systems team
and help us design, build, and scale production-ready AI solutions. In this role, you will combine AI engineering, system design, and software engineering to turn complex business problems into reliable solutions based on Generative AI, LLMs, and NLP.
Your responsibilities
Design and develop complex AI and GenAI solutions, including LLM applications, RAG, NLP/NLU pipelines, tool-calling and agentic workflows
Own technical solutions from problem definition and architecture through implementation, deployment, and production
Define and develop evaluation frameworks to measure AI system quality and performance
Optimize solutions across quality, latency, cost, reliability, and scalability
Work with prompt optimization, retrieval, re-ranking, embeddings, caching, and model selection
Lead technical discovery and translate complex business requirements into practical AI solutions
Identify when AI provides real business value and when another approach may be more effective
Support and mentor other AI engineers through design discussions, code reviews, and knowledge sharing
Stay up to date with developments in LLMs, RAG, agentic AI, evaluation, and fine-tuning
What we’re looking for
5+ years of experience in AI Engineering, Machine Learning, Applied Data Science, or a similar area
Hands-on experience developing and running LLM-based solutions in production
Strong Python and software engineering skills, including testing, CI/CD, version control, and observability
Experience with RAG, embeddings, vector databases, retrieval and re-ranking
Practical knowledge of frameworks such as LangChain, LangGraph or DSPy
Experience designing and implementing AI evaluation frameworks
Experience optimizing production AI solutions for performance, cost, and reliability
Experience with tools such as MLflow, Weights & Biases, or similar
Experience with cloud platforms such as Google Cloud
Ability to communicate complex technical topics clearly in English and work effectively in international, cross-functional teams
Experience mentoring engineers or leading technical work
Nice to have
Experience with Kedro, KServe and Kubernetes
Knowledge of AI security and governance in an enterprise environment
Experience in logistics, supply chain, or another complex operational environment
