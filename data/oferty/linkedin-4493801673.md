Supply Chain Specialist with French
About the role
About the role:
As a Supply Chain Specialist with French, you will coordinate parts availability, process and follow up on purchase orders, and work closely with suppliers and production teams to support timely deliveries. You will monitor inventory in SAP, follow up on delivery-related issues, and identify opportunities to improve process efficiency and quality.
Key responsibilities
Manage parts shortages by coordinating purchases and deliveries in line with agreed requirements
Place purchase orders manually.
Monitor and follow up on open orders through weekly reporting and supplier collaboration platforms.
Communicate directly with suppliers regarding open and overdue purchase orders.
Coordinate internal transfers of standard parts between plants.
Resolve goods-receipt discrepancies and technical issues identified after parts are received.
Maintain stock parameters in the system and implement corrective actions when required.
Keep production teams informed of delivery schedules and status.
Monitor inventory levels and maintain stock coverage in line with agreed parameters
Maintain effective communication and professional relationships with customers, suppliers, and colleagues.
Regularly review supply chain status, identify issues, and implement corrective actions.
Ensure efficient processes and high-quality execution.
Identify process gaps and opportunities for improvement.
Requirements
French language proficiency at C1-C2 level- essential for client communication purposes
Good command of English, at a minimum of B1 level - essential for client communication purposes
Higher education level
At least 2 years of experience in supply chain or logistics.
Experience with SAP (preferably within Logistics modules)
Strong analytical skills.
A customer-focused mindset and a proactive approach.
Excellent communication, interpersonal, and organizational skills.
Ability to manage multiple priorities and ad hoc tasks.
A bachelor’s degree.
Strong teamwork skills.
