ABOUT THE ROLE
Location: Poland (remote across Poland)
Contract: Contract of Mandate
Duration: 12-month paid internship
We are looking for a curious and motivated Reporting & Business Intelligence Intern to join our Supply Chain team for a 12-month paid internship. This is an excellent opportunity for students or early-career professionals who are passionate about data, reporting, business intelligence, and process automation.
As an intern, you will gain hands-on experience working with real business data and modern reporting tools while supporting projects that help improve decision-making across our supply chain organization. You will work closely with experienced colleagues from Supply Chain, Planning, Operations, and Analytics teams to learn how data is transformed into meaningful insights and automated reporting solutions.
WHAT YOU WILL DO
Support the extraction, preparation, and validation of data from ERP, EDP, and other business systems
Assist in creating reports and dashboards using Power BI and Excel
Help automate reporting processes using Power Automate, VBA, SQL, and other workflow tools
Support data cleansing, transformation, and maintenance of structured databases and data models
Participate in the development and maintenance of automated reporting solutions
Help document reporting processes, data sources, and automation workflows
Support testing, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement of reporting tools and dashboards
Collaborate with business stakeholders to understand reporting needs and translate them into practical solutions
WHAT WE WANT YOU TO BRING ALONG
Technical Skills
Student or recent graduate in Data Analytics, Business Informatics, Supply Chain, Engineering, Computer Science, Mathematics, Economics, or a related field
Good knowledge of Microsoft Excel, including formulas, pivot tables, and data analysis
Basic knowledge of Power BI and data visualization principles
Understanding of SQL and database concepts is an advantage
Interest in automation tools such as Power Automate, VBA, or similar technologies
Ability to work with large data sets and learn new analytical tools quickly
Familiarity with ERP or enterprise systems is a plus
Analytical & Business Skills
Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset
Attention to detail and commitment to data accuracy
Interest in business processes and supply chain operations
Ability to organize work effectively and manage multiple tasks
Strong communication skills and willingness to collaborate with diverse teams
Curiosity, eagerness to learn, and proactive attitude
