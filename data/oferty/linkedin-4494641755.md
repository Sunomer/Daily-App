Your mission
Cześć i witajcie! We are thrilled to welcome you to Sungrow Poland, where cutting-edge technology meets real-world manufacturing excellence. We are looking for a sharp and driven
Business Systems Consultant – SAP(PP/QM)/MOM/MES
to be the bridge between our production floors and our digital backbone.
Curious what this is all about? Let's dive right in:
You will capture and translate requirements from Production Planning, Manufacturing, and Supply Chain teams into smart ERP and MOM/MES solutions - configuring master recipes, BOMs, MRP, work centers, and production orders.
You will drive and support implementation projects including new plant rollouts, MES integrations, and S&OP topics, ensuring seamless go-lives from data migration through to final handover.
You will coordinate the integration between SAP PP/QM, MOM/MES, quality, and warehouse systems - making sure data flows smoothly from planning all the way to the shop floor.
You will serve as the go-to expert for 2nd-level support on PP/QM and MOM/MES applications, troubleshooting production orders, capacity planning issues, and shop-floor execution challenges.
You will identify and champion process improvements in production planning and execution, documenting configurations and integration flows with precision.
You will actively support continuous improvement and Industry 4.0 initiatives, helping shape the future of smart manufacturing at Sungrow.
Your profile
Role
All right, all right - that was quite the adventure, wasn't it? Now, in order to fit into this role and thrive here for a long time, your ideal background should look a lot like this:
You hold a Bachelor's degree in Information Systems, Business Informatics, Industrial Engineering, or a related field - and you bring at least 5 years of hands-on experience with SAP PP/QM or an equivalent ERP production planning module.
You are familiar with MOM/MES platforms such as SAP DMC/ME, Siemens Opcenter, or similar systems, and you understand plan-to-produce end-to-end processes inside out.
You have solid knowledge of shop-floor equipment integration and warehouse system connectivity, and you are comfortable navigating a real manufacturing environment.
You are analytically strong, process-oriented, and able to collaborate effectively with production planners, shop-floor supervisors, and IT teams alike.
You communicate confidently in Chinese and English (C1 level); German is a plus and always welcome.
You are structured, proactive, and service-oriented - equally at ease working independently or as part of a cross-functional, multinational team.
Nice to have:
Experience with SAP S/4HANA Manufacturing, knowledge of OEE or lean manufacturing concepts, and exposure to both discrete and process manufacturing scenarios.
