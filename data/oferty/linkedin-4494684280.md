Job description:
Create models to replicate current situation and run end-to-end optimisation scenarios in Supply Chain;
Collaborate with management and users to clarify business objectives and provide expected support;
Acquire knowledge of internal operational systems landscape, gather relevant data and transform it into input for models;
Prepare operational and managerial reports that clearly visualise challenges/opportunities to support users in understanding the effects of different scenarios.

Min. 3 years of experience in international corporate environment; preferably in a production company;
Knowledge of Supply Chain functional areas and flows (esp. Demand and Supply Planning);
Knowledge of data management / business intelligence tools;
Experience in data analysis and modelling;
Fluent Business English;
University degree;
Very good analytical skills;
Initiative, goal orientation;
Excellent communication skills;
Knowledge of OMP is a plus.
