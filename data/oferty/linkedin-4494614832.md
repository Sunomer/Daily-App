Location: ul. Flisa 4, Warsaw
Join our Solution Design team at GXO Logistics Poland and help us turn customer needs into efficient and practical logistics solutions. In this role, you will design warehousing concepts, develop cost models, and work closely with various teams to create competitive solutions for both new and existing GXO customers.
What you’ll do:
Design effective and efficient logistics and warehousing solutions, incorporating automation, innovation and sustainability concepts
Analyze customer needs and supply chain requirements and translate them into practical operational concepts
Develop complete cost models and P&L analyses for proposed solutions
Prepare warehouse layouts, investment plans, productivity calculations, direct and indirect labor requirements and implementation cost estimates
Collaborate with Business Development, Operations, Finance, IT, HR and other teams involved in developing customer solutions
Prepare presentation materials and solution visualizations, including layouts and 3D drawings
Present proposed solutions to customers and support the team throughout the tender and offering process
Participate in tenders and the development of proposals for new and existing customers
Support implementation projects and help ensure that the final operation remains aligned with the agreed solution design
What you need to succeed:
Experience in Solution Design, engineering or logistics solution development, preferably within a contract logistics or 3PL environment
Experience with Business Development tender and proposal processes
Ability to develop and analyze financial models and work with key business metrics
Strong knowledge of MS Excel and PowerPoint
Fluency in English (minimum C1 level ), allowing you to communicate confidently in an international business environment
Strong analytical and communication skills
A proactive attitude, structured approach to work and ability to work independently
A creative mindset and interest in innovation, automation and new logistics concepts
Ability to present ideas effectively, influence stakeholders and communicate solutions clearly
Nice to have:
Experience in projects within the automotive industry
Knowledge of CAD tools or experience in creating warehouse layouts and solution visualizations
Experience in warehouse process design and automation projects
Experience working in an international logistics organization
What we offer:
Employment in a large, international logistics organization
Opportunity to design solutions for new and existing GXO customers
Participation in projects involving automation, innovation and modern warehousing solutions
Collaboration with experienced Solution Design, Business Development, Operations, Finance and IT teams
Opportunities to develop your technical, analytical and business skills
A friendly and collaborative working environment
