Global Logistics Lead, office based flex, Warsaw Poland
ICON is a global healthcare intelligence and clinical research organisation united by a mission to bring new medicines and treatments to patients faster.
As a values-driven organisation, integrity, collaboration, agility, and inclusion are at the heart of how we work and interact with each other, customers, patients and suppliers.
As a Global Logistics Lead at ICON, you will oversee and optimizing the global logistics operations across our supply chain.
What You Will Do
You will oversee process improvement and operational excellence workstreams, ensuring deliverables meet quality and timeline expectations.
Key Responsibilities Include
Leading the planning and execution of global logistics strategies, ensuring seamless coordination of shipping, warehousing, and distribution activities.
Collaborating with cross-functional teams to develop and implement logistics solutions that support business objectives and client requirements.
Managing relationships with logistics providers, vendors, and internal stakeholders to ensure high service levels and compliance with regulatory requirements.
Overseeing the tracking and analysis of logistics metrics and performance data to identify opportunities for process improvements and cost savings.
Driving the implementation of best practices and continuous improvement initiatives across the global logistics network.
Your Profile
You will have solid process improvement and operational excellence experience, with the ability to manage competing priorities and develop your team.
Required Qualifications And Experience
Bachelor's degree in supply chain management, logistics, life sciences, or a related discipline
5+ years of experience in logistics, supply chain management, or a related field, with a proven track record in a leadership role.
Strong expertise in global logistics operations, including import/export regulations, freight management, and supply chain optimization.
Exceptional leadership and project management skills, with the ability to drive cross-functional collaboration and manage complex logistics challenges.
Excellent communication, negotiation, and stakeholder management skills, with a focus on delivering results in a dynamic environment.
