Location:

Full time, permanent
Your role:
We are seeking a Junior Supply Chain Business Partner to join our Supply Chain Management team. The core of this role is demand planning: you will own the demand forecast for one of our European markets and lead the S&OP process for it, working closely with sales and marketing. In addition, you will lead the market operationally as a replenishment planner, ensuring product availability while keeping stock levels and costs under control.
Your key responsibilities:
Demand Planning:
Own the demand forecast for the market, using sales history and business assumptions to build accurate forecasts and improve forecast accuracy.
S&OP:
Lead the S&OP process for the market and drive alignment between supply chain, sales, marketing and finance.
Stakeholder Partnership:
Act as the supply chain voice of the market; build trust, influence and align sales teams on plans, priorities and trade-offs.
Replenishment Planning:
Lead the market operationally: monitor stock levels, adjust safety stocks and ensure supply meets demand.
Production and Customer Service:
Collaborate with customer service and ensure production is delivered on time.
Risk Management:
Identify supply risks early and create mitigation plans where needed.
Data Analysis and Systems:
Analyse demand and stock trends to provide actionable insights; use planning tools, with Kinaxis Rapid Response training provided.
What we are looking for:
Advanced Microsoft Excel including Power Query (must have).
Knowledge of SAP (including S/4HANA) is an advantage.
Experience with planning software such as Kinaxis, Blue Yonder, SAP IBP or SAP APO is a plus.
Excellent communication and influencing skills - ability to convince and align sales teams and other stakeholders.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with a proactive, ownership-driven mindset.
Experience in demand planning, replenishment or supply planning, preferably in FMCG.
Fluency in English and Polish is a must.
Degree in Supply Chain Management, Business, Finance, or a related field.
