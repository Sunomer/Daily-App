Ajax Systems is an international tech company and the largest manufacturer of security systems in Europe, with over 4.5 million end users and 330K PRO users in more than 180 countries worldwide. The company offers end-to-end solutions for protecting homes and businesses оf any scale. To date, Ajax product portfolio includes 280 devices for intrusion protection, video surveillance, fire and life safety, and comfort and automation. The company employs 6,000 specialists.
We are looking for an Export Logistics Lead to take ownership of finished goods distribution across international markets, ensuring our products reach customers and partners efficiently, reliably, and on time.
This role combines hands-on logistics management, international trade expertise, cost optimization, and process improvement.
Key responsibilities:
Manage global distribution: Coordinate end-to-end shipments of finished products from manufacturing facilities and regional distribution centers to customers and distributors across Europe, the Americas, the Middle East, Africa, and Asia-Pacific
Oversee export and customs operations: Ensure smooth international shipments, manage customs documentation, and coordinate clearance procedures with brokers and relevant authorities
Manage logistics partners: Work with international freight forwarders, carriers, and 3PL/4PL providers, monitor service quality, negotiate transportation solutions, and resolve operational issues
Drive logistics efficiency: Optimize transportation costs, improve freight consolidation, and maintain high delivery performance through KPIs such as OTIF (On-Time In-Full) and order lead time
Coordinate warehouse and distribution operations: Work closely with warehouse teams to align shipment schedules, inventory availability, order processing, and dispatch planning
Improve processes through technology: Identify opportunities for automation, real-time shipment visibility, system integrations, and more efficient documentation workflows
Ensure supply chain resilience: Proactively manage transportation risks, develop alternative delivery routes, and maintain operational continuity during disruptions
Requirements:
3+ years of experience in international export, outbound logistics, or global distribution, preferably within consumer electronics, high-tech, IoT, automotive, or FMCG industries
Proven experience managing international shipments originating from Poland or other EU countries, including road freight, air cargo, and express deliveries
Strong understanding of Incoterms 2020, EU customs regulations, export documentation, and international trade procedures
Practical knowledge of Polish/EU export customs processes and customs documentation, including export declarations, EUR.1, and related documents
Familiarity with regulations governing the transportation of electronic devices containing lithium batteries (ADR / IATA DGR)
Experience working with international carriers, freight forwarders, and customs brokers
Strong analytical skills and advanced proficiency in Excel; experience with Power BI or similar analytical tools
Experience with ERP, WMS, and TMS systems, with an interest in process automation and continuous improvement
Strong ownership mindset, attention to detail, and the ability to make decisions independently in a dynamic international environment
English at B2+ level and fluent Ukrainian
