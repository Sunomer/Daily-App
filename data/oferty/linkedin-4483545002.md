Established in 2013, HONOR is a leading global provider of smart devices. We are committed to becoming a global iconic tech brand and enabling a smart life across all scenarios and all channels, for all people. With a strategic focus on innovation, quality and service, HONOR is dedicated to developing technology that empowers people around the globe to go beyond through its R&D capabilities and forward-looking technology, as well as creating a new intelligent world for everyone with its portfolio of innovative products.
We are seeking a proactive and result-oriented
Aftersales Service Operations Specialist
. In this role, you will gain excellent career growth and development opportunities by driving e-commerce service planning and operations, vendor management, and optimizing localized business workflows.
Please note that we require working fully on-site in our
Warsaw office (Konstruktorska Business Center).
Occasional short-term business travel within CEE&Nordic countries may occur.
Main Responsibilities:
•
Official E-Commerce Platform After-Sales Operations Management:
Strategy & Planning: Support after-sales service strategies and initiatives for the official platform across relevant countries.
Performance Monitoring: Monitor operational metrics and KPIs across target markets to guarantee high Customer Satisfaction (CSAT).
Process Optimization: Oversee and refine workflows to ensure they continuously align with evolving business needs.
Escalation Management: Act as the regional escalation point for complex consumer issues, coordinating cross-functional resources for rapid resolution.
Task Execution: Complete daily operational tasks and special projects related to e-commerce after-sales.
•
Poland Offline Service Operations Support:
Vendor Management: Assist in maintaining service vendor communication to ensure high-quality delivery.
Complaint Handling: Resolve local customer complaints by collaborating with internal and external resources.
Localization Support: Support for tasks requiring native Polish language.
Qualifications & Experience:
Education: Bachelor’s degree or equivalent.
Experience: 2–4 years of experience in after-sales service management, customer service operations, call centre management, or vendor operations (experience in the E-commerce or Consumer Electronics sector is highly preferred).
Language Skills: Native or bilingual proficiency in Polish, professional working proficiency in English, fluency in Chinese is a strong plus.
Key Skills & Competencies:
Communication & Collaboration:
Strong interpersonal skills to effectively coordinate cross-functional internal teams and external partners to resolve customer issues.
Problem-Solving & Empathy:
A customer-first mindset with strong conflict-resolution skills to handle escalations calmly and turn feedback into process improvements.
Vendor Management:
Proven ability to monitor supplier KPIs, negotiate effectively, and maintain healthy business partnerships.
Technical Aptitude:
Proficient in Microsoft Office with the agility and willingness to quickly master internal IT tools.
Adaptability:
Ability to thrive in a fast-paced, multicultural, and matrixed corporate environment.

Interesting and challenging international work environment.
