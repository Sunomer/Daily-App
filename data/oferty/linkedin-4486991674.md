Job Responsibilities / 岗位职责
1. Customer Relationship Management / 客户关系维护
Maintain strong relationships with clients' European operations teams, understand their business needs, and ensure customer satisfaction and long-term cooperation.
负责维护客户欧洲运营团队的合作关系，了解客户业务需求，提升客户满意度并促进长期合作。
2. Cross-functional Coordination / 跨部门协调
Act as the key contact between clients and internal departments, including warehousing, transportation, delivery, and IT, ensuring timely communication and resolution of customer requests.
作为客户与仓储、运输、配送及系统等内部部门之间的对接窗口，协调资源，确保客户需求得到及时响应和解决。
3. Business Review & Process Improvement / 业务回顾与流程优化
Conduct regular business reviews with clients, identify operational issues, and drive improvements in service quality and operational efficiency.
定期与客户开展业务回顾，识别运营问题，推动业务流程优化，持续提升服务质量及运营效率。
4. Daily Operations & Exception Handling / 日常运营与异常处理
Handle daily system operations, order management, logistics tracking, and operational exceptions, including shipment delays, lost or damaged goods, and delivery issues.
负责日常业务系统操作、订单管理、物流轨迹核实及异常处理，包括货物延误、丢失、破损及配送异常等。
