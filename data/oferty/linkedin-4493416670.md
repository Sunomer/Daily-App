Job Description

Responsibilities for the role of Stores Officer will include: Control and supervision of stores (tools store, aircraft parts store) in accordance with company procedures and policies Supporting the day-to-day operation, ensuring the correct tooling is in place and compliant Management of shipments of unserviceable pards Inspection and acceptance of incoming shipments Maintaining stores to meet policy standards and company procedures as well as upkeeping necessary records and reports in our IT systems Processing, picking and packing of Aircraft Parts in a timely manner Assisting with the loading and unloading of vehicles using manual handling techniques Moving and storing products in the correct warehouse area Consistently working to a good standard of health and safety
