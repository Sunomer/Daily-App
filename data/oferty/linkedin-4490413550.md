Maintenance Manager
Location: Nowy Modlin
Responsibilities:
Managing a team of maintenance technicians, automation specialists and engineers, including work planning, competency development and performance evaluation.
Ensuring the availability and reliability of ASRS systems, warehouse automation, refrigeration systems and the facility's technical infrastructure.
Supervising preventive maintenance, servicing, predictive maintenance and corrective repair activities.
Ensuring uninterrupted warehouse operations by minimising downtime and effectively managing equipment failures.
Managing the CMMS system, analysing data and monitoring key performance indicators (KPIs).
Overseeing spare parts and tools management, as well as service contracts.
Preparing and controlling the department's budget, identifying and implementing cost optimisation initiatives.
Ensuring compliance with occupational health and safety (OHS), technical safety and fire safety requirements, as well as NewCold standards.
Cooperating with Operations and Project teams, as well as technology suppliers, to continuously improve processes.
Participating in modernisation projects, implementation of new technologies and facility development.
Requirements
A university degree in a technical field (Automation, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Mechatronics or a related discipline).
Several years of experience as a Maintenance Manager or Maintenance Department Manager in a highly automated production or logistics environment.
Experience in managing technical teams and working with external service providers.
Knowledge of industrial automation systems, material handling equipment, ASRS systems and refrigeration installations will be an additional advantage.
Practical knowledge of TPM, RCA, CMMS systems and maintenance performance indicators.
Ability to manage budgets, costs and technical projects.
Very good command of English, both spoken and written.
Strong leadership, organisational and analytical skills.
