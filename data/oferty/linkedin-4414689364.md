About The Team
You’ll be joining the JINGDONG Logistics team, which values collaboration, ownership, and a commitment to delivering high-quality results in a fast-paced, dynamic environment.
We are looking for an experienced
Warehouse Supervisor
responsible for the Inventory area within a dynamic
3PL warehouse
environment. The successful candidate will oversee stock accuracy, coordinate inventory processes, and lead a team supporting multiple clients and diverse operational requirements. The role includes root cause analysis of discrepancies, process optimization, and cross-functional cooperation to ensure high operational standards and service level compliance.
Oversee inventory control processes and maintain high stock accuracy across multiple clients
Plan and execute cycle counts and full stock takes in a 3PL environment
Analyze inventory discrepancies and implement corrective and preventive actions
Lead and organize the work of the inventory team
Monitor system transactions and ensure WMS accuracy
Track and report inventory KPIs, client SLAs, and operational risks
Cooperate with inbound, outbound, quality, and IT teams
Identify improvement opportunities and standardize processes
Requirements
Experience in an Area Manager, Supervisor, or similar leadership role in a warehouse environment
Experience working in a 3PL / contract logistics environment
Practical knowledge of inventory management and stock control processes
English proficiency at minimum B2 level
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills
Experience working with WMS and Excel
Leadership skills and strong work organization
Nice to have
Experience in e-commerce or high-volume warehouse operations
Knowledge of Lean or continuous improvement methodologies
Experience in an international work environment
Chinese language in speaking and writting
