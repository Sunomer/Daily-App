This is a key regional leadership role, acting as the bridge between local operations, Regional HQ, and Global HQ to ensure operational excellence, productivity improvement, digital transformation, and sustainable business growth across the East Cluster.
Key Responsibilities:
Leading and enhancing Air Freight product competitiveness across the East Cluster.
Driving process standardization, operational efficiency, and productivity improvement across multiple countries.
Managing the implementation of regional and global product strategies while adapting them to local market requirements.
Monitoring product and operational performance through KPIs, dashboards, and data-driven decision-making.
Collaborating closely with Quality, Digital Innovation, IT, Regional HQ, and Global HQ stakeholders.
Leading change management initiatives and supporting the adoption of new systems, tools, and processes across the region.
Driving continuous improvement through automation, CargoWise optimization, data quality enhancement, and process excellence initiatives.

Significant experience within Air Freight Forwarding and international logistics operations.
Strong understanding of Air Freight products, industry trends, digital solutions, and operational processes.
Experience in business process management, process optimization, continuous improvement, and change management.
Strong knowledge of CargoWise; experience with eFreight/eAWB processes is highly desirable.
Experience working with business intelligence and analytics tools such as Power BI is an advantage.

Strong stakeholder management, communication, and influencing skills.
