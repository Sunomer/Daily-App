Job Description
About the role
As AUTODOC continues to scale its operations across Europe, our business processes and People & Culture structures are continuously evolving. To keep pace with this dynamic environment, we need to adapt our systems, rethink our existing implementations, and design solutions that scale effectively across our global organization.
To achieve this goal, we are hiring an
HRIS Product Owner
to manage, configure, and maintain our current HRIS landscape. You will run and improve our current core HRIS (Workday: HCM, Time Tracking, Absence Management and Advanced Compensation) and shape the architecture, integrations and roadmap of our wider HR tech ecosystem.
In this role, you will analyze complex People & Culture requirements and determine whether solutions can be delivered directly within core platform other times it means integrating with external systems or bringing in other tools. Operating with high autonomy in a flexible Jira-driven environment, you will translate business needs into scalable technical architecture and drive continuous system improvements.
This is an exciting opportunity for a proactive problem solver to take end-to-end ownership of critical HR technology in a fast-growing e-commerce leader. You will directly influence how our people processes run, optimize workflows, and bring innovative solutions to life for thousands of AUTODOCers across Europe.
What you will do
Own the HR systems landscape and roadmap: the core HRIS, connected applications, integrations and data flows.
Define and maintain the HR data model (organizational structures, job architecture, compensation, time and absence) so it stays consistent, scalable and system-agnostic.
Analyze People & Culture business requirements, evaluate system feasibility, and design scalable technical solutions both inside and outside HRIS.
Manage business process security policies, domain security policies, and user/role-based access controls in accordance with company standards.
Partner with People & Culture stakeholders, Finance, and cross-functional teams to translate operational needs into clear system deliverables and Jira project roadmaps.
Ensure robust data integrity and quality control across systems through rigorous testing, audit routines, and workflow improvements.
Provide end-user support across Time Tracking and Absence Management, resolving tickets efficiently within agreed timelines.
Champion continuous improvement and engagement with HR tools, identifying opportunities to optimize functionality and enhance employee experience.
