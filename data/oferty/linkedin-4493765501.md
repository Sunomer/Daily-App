Turn data into business impact.
Are you passionate about transforming complex data into meaningful business insights? Do you enjoy building dashboards that drive decisions and help organizations perform better? If so, we would love to meet you.
As a
Business Analyst, BI & Analytics
, you will play a key role in shaping how data is used across the organization. Working closely with business stakeholders and technical teams, you will deliver impactful reporting solutions, improve data transparency, and support informed decision-making in a global environment.
What You'll Do
Design, develop, and maintain insightful Power BI dashboards and reports that drive business performance
Translate business needs into scalable data models, visualizations, and reporting solutions
Partner with stakeholders to understand reporting requirements and transform them into actionable insights
Identify trends, performance gaps, and opportunities for improvement through data analysis
Support the migration and standardization of reporting solutions across the organization
Improve reporting efficiency by reducing manual processes and increasing automation
Validate data quality, investigate discrepancies, and support root-cause analysis of data-related issues
Contribute to the development and monitoring of data pipelines and reporting processes
Help define and maintain KPI frameworks, reporting standards, and governance principles
Support user testing, implementation, and adoption of BI solutions
Provide ad-hoc analysis and recommendations to support business decisions
Collaborate with global teams to continuously improve data reliability, transparency, and reporting capabilities
What We're Looking For
2-5 years of experience in Business Intelligence, Data Analytics, Reporting, or a similar field
Hands-on experience with Power BI, including data modeling, DAX, and visualization design
Strong analytical skills with the ability to translate business requirements into practical solutions
Experience working with KPI frameworks, performance reporting, and business analytics
Good understanding of data structures and basic SQL
Advanced Excel and Microsoft Office skills
Ability to communicate clearly and effectively with both business and technical stakeholders
Strong ownership mindset and attention to detail
Ability to balance day-to-day execution with long-term data governance and standardization
Experience in a global or matrix organization is an advantage
Knowledge of Power Platform (e.g., Power Automate) is a plus
Experience in freight forwarding, logistics, supply chain, or transportation industries is a plus
Fluent English, both written and spoken
