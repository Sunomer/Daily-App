GBS Change Manager (m/f/d)
Temporary - Two years
The GBS Change Manager supports the implementation of change management plans within Global Business Services, ensuring that employees, customers and stakeholders are informed, engaged and supported throughout change initiatives. The role focuses on stakeholder management, employee engagement, communication and other change interventions that enable successful adoption.
Position
As the GBS Change Manager, you will
Develop and execute change management plans that support employee adoption
Perform stakeholder analyses, impact assessments and resistance management activities
Design and implement engagement plans that build stakeholder understanding and support
Apply the PROSCI framework to large projects
Propose stakeholder mapping and build strong relationships across assigned projects
Conduct stakeholder meetings to gather feedback, communicate updates and address concerns
Work with project teams and the Change Management Lead to maintain clear, consistent and timely stakeholder communication plans
Create content and communication assets that explain the vision, benefits and impact of change initiatives
Facilitate workshops and meetings that build understanding and acceptance of change
Coordinate the content and delivery of messaging plans across communication channels
Monitor communication effectiveness and adjust approaches when required
Integrate change management activities into project plans
Track, measure and report on the effectiveness of change management activities
Manage additional tasks and projects when required
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Business Administration, Communication, Change Management or a related field
At least five years of experience in change management, project management or a related role, ideally within a multinational organization
Experience using change management methodologies, preferably PROSCI
Experience in stakeholder management and engagement planning across all organizational levels, including senior stakeholders
Excellent written and verbal English at business proficiency level
Excellent communication, interpersonal and presentation skills
Strong project management expertise and the ability to manage complex change initiatives
Ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
Understanding of the Global Business Services environment would be beneficial
What To Expect
You can expect a collaborative role with broad stakeholder exposure and the opportunity to contribute to complex change initiatives within Global Business Services. You will work closely with project teams, the Change Management Lead and business stakeholders to turn change strategies into practical engagement, communication and adoption activities. The role encourages simple and efficient ways of working, timely decision-making, accountability and collaboration across diverse teams.
