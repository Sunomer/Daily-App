Make Every Experience Matter
Viseven is an AI-native Intelligent Experiences Enabler for life sciences, helping pharma and biotech organizations move from fragmented content operations to connected, compliant, and measurable omnichannel engagement with HCPs and patients. By combining proprietary technology — eWizard, eVa, Kheiron, and Lexi — with agentic AI and strategic consulting, we support the full content lifecycle: from creation and MLR validation to localization and omnichannel publishing. Our clients include Top 50 life sciences, pharma, biotech, and medtech companies around the globe.
Role Description:
Empower customers to drive transformation and achieve their business objectives by designing and delivering impactful learning experiences that develop critical competencies and support sustainable capability growth.
Responsibilities:
Lead Customer Education Initiatives - Analyze customer needs, transformation goals, and skills gaps to design targeted learning strategies that enable successful customer transformation, fostering the adoption of new processes, tools and ways of working
Design and Develop Learning Experiences - Develop and deliver customer-centric training programs, leveraging instructional design principles to ensure engagement and competence building
Collaborate for Impact - Work closely with internal teams, including change managers, subject matter experts, product, and customer success to align learning solutions with business objectives and customer needs
Curate & Develop Learning Content - Create and maintain diverse learning materials such as training scripts, presentations, interactive guides, video tutorials, and assessments
Facilitate Training & Workshops - Conduct interactive training sessions, both online and in-person, ensuring a dynamic and tailored learning experience for diverse audiences
Drive Continuous Learning - Collect and analyze feedback, iterating on learning content and delivery approaches to maximize impact
Enhance Customer Engagement - Maintain proactive communication with customers, guiding them through their learning journey and ensuring ongoing support
Measure & Optimize Success - Monitor the effectiveness of learning programs, track engagement rates, and analyze customer feedback to continuously refine and improve training initiatives
Requirements:
At least 2 years of commercial experience in customer education, learning and development, customer enablement, or a related field
At least Upper-Intermediate level of English (both written and spoken)
Experience leading customer education initiatives in a fast-paced environment, with a focus on change management and business transformation
Proven ability to develop and implement learning strategies aligned with business objectives
Good understanding of instructional design principles (e.g. ADDIE model, Learning Design Thinking) and the ability to apply them when developing learning and enablement programs
Experience working with SaaS solutions, customer onboarding, and/or omnichannel strategy and execution, preferably within the pharmaceutical industry
Self-driven and proactive approach, with the ability to bring innovative ideas to learning program development
Ability to simplify complex topics and translate them into engaging learning experiences
Strong communication, presentation, and facilitation skills
Excellent collaboration skills and the ability to work effectively with cross-functional and international teams
Experience with Veeva or Salesforce CRM is a plus
