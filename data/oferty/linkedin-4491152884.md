Too Good To Go is on a mission to inspire and empower everyone to fight food waste together. Our app connects people with surplus food from stores, restaurants and brands, so good food gets eaten instead of wasted.
Your mission:
Reporting to the CX Operations Director, you serve as the operational and strategic bridge between full-scale CX operations and cross-functional business teams. Your primary focus is translating new product features, service models, and business processes into scalable AI workflows and frontline operational procedures. You will be held accountable for measures of success, including implementation velocity, agent adoption rates, and overall process efficiency. This role is fully remote.
Your role:
Take full ownership of stakeholder management across all business teams, project leads, and cross-functional partners—including platform and parcel teams
Architect and pilot frontline operational procedures while defining clear technical and business requirements for AI and automation initiatives.
Manage end-to-end change management, leveraging frontline feedback loops to refine workflows, mitigate risks, and drive operational readiness.
Establish and maintain single-source-of-truth documentation, SOPs, Knowledge Base articles, and reference guides across all program phases.
Lead operational rollouts and performance tracking, facilitating seamless handoffs to AI and frontline leadership to ensure long-term adoption.
Enable CX Operations for new or changing workflows by providing timely communication, setting clear expectations, and ensuring seamless adoption.
Coordinate training resources where needed to support operational rollouts and maintain evergreen updates.
Requirements:
Experience & Background
Prior experience in CX operations and frontline workflows is strongly preferred, though adaptable candidates capable of quickly mastering new processes are highly encouraged to apply.
Proven experience coordinating operational implementations, managing cross-functional stakeholders, and supporting system-related projects.
Strong ability to translate operational needs into structured workflows and documentation.
Technical & Process Skills
Familiarity with CRMs systems(Zendesk tools ideally), workflows, and general platform workflows.
Understanding of B2B & B2C governance, reporting, data integrity, and process compliance principles.
Experience working with project management and change management principles and tools such as Asana.
Soft Skills & Personal Attributes
Synthesizes and delivers key messages effectively across all organizational tiers
Adept at translating complex operational data into actionable insights for leadership while maintaining approachable, clear guidance for customer-facing teams.
Detail-oriented and highly organized, ensuring clarity, consistency, and structured follow-up across multiple stakeholders.
Team player with a proactive, self-starting approach and a continuous learning mindset.
Calm, reliable, and solution-oriented under pressure, with a natural drive to improve processes and enable operational excellence
