About The Role
ICEYE is looking for a Freight & Customs Clearance Specialist to manage international freight forwarding, customs compliance, and logistics operations supporting our manufacturing activities in Warsaw, Poland. Given the significant workload involving dual-use items, this is a key position responsible for cooperating with customs agencies and carriers to ensure seamless import/export clearance and timely deliveries.
You will manage end-to-end inbound and outbound freight flows, execute international trade compliance (including ITAR, EAR, and dual-use export controls), coordinate customs brokerage services, and oversee shipping documentation. You will work closely with Procurement, Production Planning, Quality, and external logistics partners to ensure all shipments arrive on schedule while adhering to compliance and budget requirements.
What You Will Do
Oversee inbound and outbound logistics, including air, sea, and road freight forwarding, customs clearance, and carrier coordination.
Execute full compliance with international trade regulations, dual-use items, ITAR, and EAR export controls.
Manage relationships and operational workflows with external customs agencies (such as Customs Support Group/Rusak Business Services) and freight forwarders (such as Gebrüder Weiss).
Prepare, verify, and archive essential shipping and customs documentation (e.g., AWB, SAD/ZC429, commercial invoices, DSK declarations).
Ensure proper tariff classification (HS/CN codes), valuation, and origin determination for satellite components and specialized aerospace equipment.
Monitor freight costs, duty payments, and customs duty guarantees, driving cost optimization and efficiency in transport activities.
Coordinate specialized handling, shock-sensitive packaging, and transport requirements for high-value space hardware in accordance with AS9100 and ECSS standards.
Mitigate logistics risks, supply disruptions, and customs delays through proactive tracking and contingency planning.
Must Haves
What we're looking for
3–5 years of hands-on experience in international freight forwarding, customs clearance, and shipping operations.
Proven experience working in a structured production environment (Aerospace, Defense, Automotive, or Electronics).
Strong expertise in customs procedures, EORI, SAD/ZC429, tariff classification, and dual-use item regulations.
Experience handling international trade controls (ITAR, EAR, export licenses).
Proficiency with ERP systems and WMS workflows.
Strong command of MS Excel / Google Sheets for reporting and freight tracking.
Fluency in English and Polish (essential for daily communication with local customs authorities and international stakeholders).
Certification for dangerous goods transport by road, sea, and air (ADR, IMDG, IATA)
