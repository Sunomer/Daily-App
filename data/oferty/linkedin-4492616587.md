For our Client, an international automotive company specialising in the manufacturing of metal and hybrid components and structures for the automotive industry, we are looking for a
Resident Engineer – Hyundai & Kia (Korean Speaking
.)
The successful candidate will act as the main point of contact between the company and Korean representatives of Hyundai and Kia in Europe, ensuring effective communication, customer relationship management and coordination of technical and quality-related matters.
Key Responsibilities:
Acting as the primary point of contact for Korean representatives of Hyundai and Kia in Europe.
Building and maintaining strong relationships with customers through regular communication, meetings and on-site visits in Czechia and Slovakia.
Coordinating information flow between customers and internal departments, particularly Engineering, Quality, Program Management and Production.
Communicating and following up on technical concerns, quality issues, customer requirements and improvement requests.
Coordinating open issues, corrective actions, commitments and deadlines to ensure timely resolution.
Supporting product development, industrialisation and production launch activities.
Participating in technical reviews, project meetings and quality-related discussions.
Preparing reports, meeting summaries and monitoring the progress of agreed actions.
Representing the company's interests in customer interactions while ensuring that customer expectations are properly understood and addressed.
Requirements:
At least 3 years of professional experience in the automotive industry, preferably in quality, product development, project management, launch management or resident engineering.
Native or equivalent professional-level Korean language skills.
Very good command of English, both spoken and written.
Good understanding of automotive product development, manufacturing and quality processes.
Ability to understand technical documentation, engineering drawings, dimensional reports and tolerance-related topics.
Experience in customer communication and coordinating technical matters across different departments.
Strong communication, interpersonal and organisational skills.
Proactive and independent approach, with the ability to manage multiple topics and follow up on agreed actions.
Willingness to travel regularly, including at short notice.
Valid category B driving licence.
Nice to Have:
Previous experience working with Hyundai, Kia or other Korean automotive manufacturers.
Knowledge of Body-in-White (BIW) components and processes such as metal stamping, joining and assembly.
Experience working directly with automotive OEMs or Tier 1 suppliers.
Our Client Offers:
Employment with an international company operating in the automotive industry.
Hybrid working model.
Flexible approach to the candidate's place of residence and work organisation, subject to individual arrangements.
Company car for business travel.

Professional development in an international and technically advanced environment.
