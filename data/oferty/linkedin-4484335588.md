Role Overview:
We're looking for an IT Application Specialist to support and enhance the business applications that power our logistics operations across Europe.
In this role, you'll work at the intersection of business applications, systems integrations, automation, and logistics processes, ensuring the stability and continuous improvement of critical platforms used by our operational teams.
If you enjoy solving complex system challenges, collaborating with both business and technical stakeholders, and driving process improvements through technology, we'd love to hear from you.
key responsabilities:
Provide L2/L3 support for logistics and business applications.
Troubleshoot application, integration and data flow issues across multiple systems.
Coordinate incident resolution with internal teams and external vendors.
Support and maintain integrations using EDI, APIs and connected business platforms.
Gather and translate business requirements into practical system solutions.
Participate in application implementations, upgrades, testing and release activities.
Drive continuous improvement initiatives, automation opportunities and process optimization.
Maintain system documentation, support guides and process flows.
Support Microsoft Power Platform and SharePoint-based solutions.
Ensure compliance with security, access management and data protection standards.
Required Skills & Qualifications:
Experience supporting business applications in logistics, supply chain, transportation, warehousing, manufacturing or similar operational environments.
Understanding of system integrations, EDI/API concepts and data flows.
Strong troubleshooting, analytical and problem-solving skills.
Experience working with vendors and cross-functional stakeholders.
Ability to translate business requirements into effective technical solutions.
A proactive mindset with a passion for process improvement and innovation.
Experience with Power Platform and/or SharePoint is a plus.
Experience with logistics platforms, ERP solutions, WMS, TMS or customs systems is highly desirable.
Strong communication skills and confidence working with both technical and non-technical users.
