CBS Order To Invoice Analyst (F/M)
Salary Range: 7,700–10,000 PLN gross monthly
Join our dynamic team as a CBS Order To Invoice Analyst, where you will manage and optimize end-to-end order fulfillment while collaborating closely with commercial and logistics partners in a fast-paced, multinational setting.
MAIN RESPONSIBILITIES:
Manage end-to-end Order to Invoice workflows within company best practice standards, handling order validation, delivery creation, exception handling, and invoice follow-ups.
Utilize SAP and TMS order processing systems to execute fulfillment efficiently, minimize manual intervention, and consolidate shipments.
Partner with Customer Development Teams (CDTs) and Logistics (internal and 3PL) to investigate and resolve delivery disruptions, stock issues, returns, and refusals.
Enforce predefined commercial sales terms and identify supply chain inefficiencies to drive continuous cost and process optimizations.
Monitor, analyze, and report on key customer service KPIs and cost-to-serve metrics to drive corrective actions and support regional stakeholders.
REQUIREMENTS:
University degree.
1–2 years of experience in customer service or order-to-invoice operations.
Strong command of English (written and spoken) to support international stakeholders.
Proficiency in MS Excel and Google Workspace tools; SAP experience is a distinct advantage.
Analytical mindset with proven problem-solving abilities and a strong focus on team collaboration.
Strong communication and relationship-building skills across cross-functional teams.
