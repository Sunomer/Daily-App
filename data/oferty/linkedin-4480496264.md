Join our Supply Chain Technologies organization and take technical ownership of our 4PL / Lead Logistics solutions, with Blue Yonder Transportation Management at the core.
You will act as the end-to-end Technical Lead and Solution Architect, translating business and customer requirements into scalable, integrated solutions across transportation management, integrations, visibility, reporting and the wider supply-chain technology landscape.
Your responsibilities:
Own the end-to-end technical solution design for 4PL / Lead Logistics customer solutions
Act as design authority for Blue Yonder Transportation Management (BY TM) and its integration with the wider technology landscape
Translate business requirements into robust solution architectures aligned with DSV technology standards and enterprise architecture
Define application architecture, system boundaries, data flows, APIs/EDI integrations and key technical dependencies
Lead the technical workstream across customer implementations, migrations and major solution changes
Coordinate solution design across Blue Yonder TM, integration services, visibility platforms, ERP/WMS, reporting and other supply-chain applications
Work closely with Business Analysis & Product, development, integration, infrastructure/cloud and external technology partners
Provide technical leadership throughout the lifecycle, from solution definition and design through implementation, testing, go-live and transition to support
Drive standardization and reusable solution patterns, minimizing unnecessary customization and technical complexity
Assess solution gaps, technical risks and cross-application impacts and define appropriate solutions or enhancements
Maintain high-level solution and architecture documentation and provide technical guidance across the organization
Our requirements:
Strong functional and technical expertise in Blue Yonder / JDA Transportation Management
Strong understanding of 4PL, Lead Logistics, transportation management and supply-chain processes
Proven experience designing end-to-end solutions across multiple applications and technologies
Strong knowledge of EDI, APIs, system integrations and data flows
Experience integrating TMS with ERP, WMS, visibility, carrier, order-management and reporting solutions
Experience as a Solution Architect, Application Architect, Application Designer or Technical Lead in complex implementation environments
Ability to translate business requirements into pragmatic, scalable and supportable technical solutions
Strong analytical, problem-solving and stakeholder-management skills
Ability to lead technical discussions across geographically distributed teams and technology partners
Fluent English, written and spoken. Other languages are an advantage
Your role in the team:
You will be the technical counterpart to our Business Analysis & Product function, taking ownership of how business requirements are translated into an integrated technology solution.
This is more than a Blue Yonder TM specialist role. You will have a holistic view of the 4PL solution landscape, ensuring that applications, integrations and technology components operate as one coherent end-to-end solution.
Business requirements → Solution architecture → Application & integration design → Implementation → Operational solution
