Your main responsibilities:
Address and resolve operational issues or service disruptions with carriers, minimizing impact on Allegro's delivery and business goals.
Oversee key quality KPIs - in case of their decline, develop, implement and oversee improvement plans on the carriers' side.
Monitor and analyze carrier performance and cooperation efficiency.
Participate in cross-functional cooperation across Allegro to improve processes and service quality (e.g. Commerce, CX, Finance, Legal).
Provide feedback and insights supporting business decisions and process optimizations.
Represent Carrier Management in projects related to logistics platform changes and process improvements.
Prepare and share periodic reports, updates, and data analyses relevant to carrier performance and cost efficiency.
Support T&BC (Transport & Business Cooperation) initiatives and projects.
Manage cooperation and performance with international carriers, ensuring compliance with Allegro's delivery policies and standards.
Participate in negotiations and contract renewals with international and local partners.
Support expansion of cross-border delivery services and process improvements.
Cooperate with internal stakeholders on cross-border regulations, and service development.
Work on the settlements with the carriers related to the provided services
Handling specific platform T&Cs related to the changes/modifications connected with the particular carrier/market/service
Conduct logistics markets research to ensure an optimal blend of partners and delivery methods.
This is the right job for you if:
Proven experience: You have a minimum of 3 years of experience managing carrier relationships or working within a dynamic courier company. This experience has given you a deep understanding of carrier operations and management.
Industry insights: You possess a strong understanding of the transport services market, with a preference for expertise in the CEP (courier, express, parcel) and e-commerce logistics. area. You're familiar with industry trends and challenges.
Negotiation expertise: You have a track record of successfully negotiating contracts with carriers and other service providers, ensuring long-term, mutually beneficial agreements.
Data-driven decision-making: You demonstrate strong analytical skills and a passion for using data to inform your decisions and drive improvements in carrier performance.
Excellent communication skills: You are fluent in Polish and have a high level of proficiency in English (both spoken and written), enabling you to effectively communicate and collaborate with diverse stakeholders.
