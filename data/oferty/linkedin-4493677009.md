Role Overview
Our client is a fast-growing global digital marketplace for specialty green coffee, based in the United States. It connects coffee producers, exporters, importers, and roasters worldwide, with transparent pricing and secure, standardized trades.
As a
Junior Logistics Analyst
, you'll keep coffee shipments moving from the origin warehouse to the buyer's door. You'll track international shipments, check trade and shipping documents, and work closely with the client's operations team, exporters, freight forwarders, and importers to deliver on time.
Role Details
Schedule: Full-time
Working Hours: EU business hours
Location: Remote, based in Poland
Engagement: Independent Contractor
Compensation: USD 2,400 – 3,200 (depending on level of experience)
What You'll Do
Track every shipment from booking to delivery and update its status daily
Coordinate with exporters, freight forwarders, warehouses, and importers on bookings, sailings, ETAs, and delivery windows
Flag delays, rollovers, or quality holds early and help resolve them
Review trade documents (commercial invoices, bills of lading, packing lists, certificates of origin, phytosanitary certificates) against contract terms and Incoterms
Keep shipment, freight-cost, and lead-time data clean, and build weekly reports on on-time delivery, transit times, and costs
Answer logistics questions from buyers and sellers by email and chat
What We're Looking For
1-3 years of experience in logistics, freight forwarding, import/export, or supply chain analysis
A degree in Supply Chain, Logistics, Customs Administration, Industrial Engineering, Business, or a related field
Working knowledge of international shipping documents and Incoterms
Strong Excel / Google Sheets skills (lookups, pivot tables, basic dashboards)
Excellent written and spoken English
Detail-oriented, organized, and comfortable working fully remote and owning many shipments at once
Nice-to-Haves
Spanish or Portuguese
Experience with agricultural commodities, food or coffee exports
Exposure to ocean freight (FCL/LCL) and container tracking tools
Familiarity with SQL, Power BI, Looker Studio or similar tools
