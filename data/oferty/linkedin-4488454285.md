Johnson & Johnson Vision is recruiting for a Quality Assurance Supervisor, located in the J&J Distribution Centre, Warsaw, Poland.
In this role, you will be responsible for leading site quality engineering and inspection processes for Johnson & Johnson Vision products in the EMEA Supply Chain. You will drive key Quality Engineering initiatives related to sustaining and improving performance and lead a team of quality inspectors.
The Responsibilities And The Impact YOU Will Have
Lead the Quality Inspection team for the Distribution Centre in Warsaw, Poland.
Provide Quality support to contact lens distribution operations in the DC and into third party logistic partners.
Responsible for site CAPA and NC processes and support of records and associated metrics.
Minimize risk to the customer through risk analysis and risk management throughout the product lifecycle (including knowledge of Fishbone, 5Y and FMEA analysis)
Quality Lead for monthly and quarterly management reviews.
Train others in Quality systems, tools and objectives
Host notified body, and internal audits of the distribution centre and support audits of other sites across EMEA.
Delegate for Quality Manager as the need arises
