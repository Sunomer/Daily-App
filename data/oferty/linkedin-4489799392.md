Supply Planning Transformation Specialist – Manufacturing
We’re looking for an experienced production planning professional to support a global FMCG organisation with the deployment of new planning processes, tools and ways of working across its manufacturing sites.
You must have at least five years of hands-on experience working as a production planner
, with a strong understanding of manufacturing planning and the ability to train and support other planners through change.
The role
Lead site-level deployment of a global supply planning transformation programme.
Work with site leadership and planning teams to introduce a new Target Operating Model.
Assess site readiness, identify process and capability gaps, and develop practical deployment plans.
Train, coach and support production planners as they adopt new processes and planning tools.
Monitor deployment milestones, manage risks and resolve barriers to adoption.
Support sites through implementation and hypercare, ensuring teams can sustain the changes independently.
What we’re looking for
At least five years’ direct experience as a production planner within manufacturing.
Experience delivering or supporting supply chain process transformation, deployment or change management.
The ability to translate planning processes into practical training and guidance for site teams.
Hands-on working knowledge of SAP, Blue Yonder/JDA or equivalent planning tools.
Strong stakeholder management skills and confidence working across Manufacturing, Site Supply Chain and Global Supply Chain.
The ability to work independently and influence teams without direct line management responsibility.
A degree in Supply Chain, Business, Industrial Engineering or a related discipline.
Pharmaceutical or complex manufacturing experience strongly preferred.
