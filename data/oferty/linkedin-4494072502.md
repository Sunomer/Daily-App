We’re looking for a quality-focused professional to help ensure VIP customers receive a consistent, high-standard experience. In this role, you’ll review customer interactions and service processes, identify opportunities to improve quality, and work with teams to turn your findings into practical actions.
What You’ll Do
Monitor and assess VIP customer interactions across relevant communication channels, using agreed quality standards and scorecards.
Evaluate service quality, communication, accuracy, and adherence to internal procedures.
Identify recurring issues, service gaps, and opportunities to improve the VIP customer experience.
Document findings clearly and provide timely, constructive feedback to relevant team members and managers.
Prepare quality reports and share trends, insights, and recommendations with stakeholders.
Work with VIP, customer support, training, and compliance teams to support consistent service standards.
Contribute to the development and regular review of quality guidelines, evaluation criteria, and processes.
Follow up on agreed improvement actions and help assess their impact.
What You Bring
Experience in quality assurance, quality control, customer experience, or a similar role in a customer-facing environment.
Strong understanding of customer service standards and interaction evaluation.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to deliver clear, constructive feedback.
Strong analytical skills and attention to detail, including the ability to identify patterns in quality data.
Confidence preparing reports and presenting findings to different stakeholders.
A fair, consistent, and customer-focused approach to quality assessment.
Ability to manage priorities and work effectively with cross-functional teams.
Professional proficiency in English.
Nice to Have
Experience in iGaming, VIP services, online betting, or casino operations.
Familiarity with quality monitoring platforms, CRM systems, or customer support tools.
Experience creating quality scorecards, coaching materials, or service guidelines.
Knowledge of responsible gaming practices and customer service compliance requirements.
