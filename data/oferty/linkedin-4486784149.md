As an IT Specialist – Blue Yonder WMS Dispatcher, you will join our Contract Logistics IT team and work closely with DSV business teams, customers and regional and global IT functions.
Your main focus will be delivering changes and improvements within the Blue Yonder Dispatcher WMS environment, supporting warehouse operations and business requirements.
You will:
Deliver end-to-end Change Requests related to Blue Yonder Dispatcher, from requirement analysis through implementation, testing and release.
Analyse business requirements and translate them into effective WMS solutions.
Configure and implement changes within the WMS environment.
Design, modify and validate reports and database queries.
Work with SQL and PL/SQL, including troubleshooting and query optimization.
Prepare test plans and scenarios and perform testing in non-production environments.
Conduct regression testing to ensure existing warehouse processes continue to work correctly.
Review and validate solutions developed by other team members before production release, following the 4-eyes principle.
Ensure implemented changes follow ITSM, security, compliance and audit requirements.
Maintain technical documentation and development standards.
Cooperate with the Blue Yonder WMS Core team and other DSV IT and business teams.
Support continuous improvement of warehouse management solutions and processes.
Our Requirements
Minimum 3 years of experience in application or software development.
Good understanding of the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
Very good knowledge of SQL and relational databases.
Experience with PL/SQL, including query tuning and optimization.
Experience in analysing business requirements, designing solutions, testing and technical documentation.
Ability to take ownership of Change Requests from analysis through implementation.
Good knowledge of Windows Server and Windows shell scripting.
Experience with source/version control.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills.
Good communication skills and ability to cooperate with both technical and business stakeholders.
Ability to work independently in an international and multicultural environment.
Very good command of English.
Nice to have
Experience with Blue Yonder Dispatcher / JDA Dispatcher or other warehouse management systems.
Knowledge of Jasper Reports or SSRS.
Experience with EDI standards such as EDIFACT, XML, ODETTE or ANSI X12.
Previous experience within logistics, supply chain or the 3PL industry.

You are proactive, analytical and comfortable taking ownership of your work. You enjoy solving technical problems but can also communicate effectively with business users and understand their operational needs.
You can work independently, stay calm when priorities change and collaborate effectively with colleagues across different countries and functions.
