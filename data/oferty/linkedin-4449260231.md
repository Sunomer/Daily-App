What You’ll Do
We are looking for an experienced DELMIA Ortems (APS) Lead Analyst to drive the design, implementation, and optimisation of advanced production planning and scheduling solutions.
In this role, you will work closely with manufacturing, supply chain, and IT teams to configure and enhance Ortems solutions that improve production efficiency, capacity utilisation, and operational performance. You will lead the technical delivery of APS initiatives, from requirements gathering and solution design through implementation, testing, and user adoption.
Lead the implementation and enhancement of DELMIA Ortems (APS) solutions.
Design and configure production scheduling, capacity planning, and resource management processes.
Collaborate with business stakeholders to analyse requirements and translate them into effective planning solutions.
Develop and optimise scheduling models, business rules, and planning scenarios.
Support integration between APS, ERP, MES, and other enterprise systems.
Drive solution testing, validation, performance optimisation, and continuous improvement.
Provide technical leadership throughout the full solution lifecycle, from design to go-live and support.
Create and maintain functional and technical documentation.
Partner with global teams to deliver scalable, high-quality manufacturing planning solutions.
