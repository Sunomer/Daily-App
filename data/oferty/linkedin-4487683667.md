Tactical Procurement Manager (NTM - Non Tobacco Materials - And Capex) (m/f/d)
The Tactical Procurement Manager (NTM - Non Tobacco Materials - and Capex) strengthens commercial governance and supports the effective implementation of NTM and Capex procurement strategies. The role focuses on delivering cost transparency and savings, ensuring reliable sourcing execution, maintaining supplier and data integrity, and supporting informed procurement decision-making and business continuity.
Position
As the Tactical Procurement Manager (NTM - Non Tobacco Materials - and Capex), you will
Lead delegated NTM tail-spend negotiations in line with aligned strategies and key performance indicators, validate demand, prepare requests for quotation, support contract preparation and sign-off, and prepare sourcing plans
Monitor sourcing execution and contract fulfilment, working with relevant stakeholders to understand and address deviations
Analyse supplier cost structures and price lists, validate price changes, identify cost-reduction opportunities, monitor purchase price variance and assess supplier competitiveness
Support supplier performance and relationship management, including business reviews, performance actions and risk escalation
Gather procurement data, prepare required reports and maintain relevant systems
Monitor timely payments and proactively support resolution of overdue invoices
Follow up on assigned business continuity projects and supplier qualifications, coordinating with relevant stakeholders
Maintain source lists and provide ad hoc support for Spare Parts and Capital Equipment requirements
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Procurement, Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, Finance, Economics, Engineering or a related field
Minimum 3 years of experience in a similar environment and position in a multinational company
Fluent business English, with any additional language considered an asset
Strong commercial and financial acumen
Experience in procurement, sourcing, supplier management and negotiations
Strong analytical and data-management skills
Proficiency in Microsoft Excel, Microsoft PowerPoint and procurement or Enterprise Resource Planning (ERP) systems
Open-minded and adaptable approach, with a focus on quality, results and continuous improvement
Ability to communicate openly, encourage two-way communication and collaborate effectively with stakeholders
Strong execution mindset with a sense of urgency, accountability and commitment to delivering projects on time and to specification
