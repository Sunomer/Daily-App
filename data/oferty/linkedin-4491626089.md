Role Purpose
The Customer Journey Optimisation Coordinator supports the analysis and improvement of end-to-end customer journeys across Customer Sales and Service. Using data, insight, and customer feedback, the role identifies friction points, inconsistencies, and gaps that impact experience and operational efficiency.
Working closely with Digital, Operations, Systems, Knowledge, and Channel teams, the postholder coordinates journey enhancements to create smoother, more intuitive interactions for customers. The role supports the design of journey maps, improvement plans, and change activities, ensuring updates are aligned with policies, service standards, and business priorities, ultimately reducing customer effort and improving satisfaction.
Role Accountabilities
You will analyse customer journeys using data, feedback, and operational insight to identify friction points, inconsistencies, and improvement opportunities. The role develops journey maps, process diagrams, and supporting documentation to highlight gaps and coordinates improvements across systems, channels, and teams to ensure changes are aligned and coherent. It supports prioritisation of journey enhancements based on impact, feasibility, and customer value, working closely with Digital, Systems, and Knowledge teams to ensure effective implementation.
In addition, the role monitors the impact of journey changes through data, KPIs, and customer satisfaction indicators, maintaining documentation and governance standards for updated journeys. It contributes to incident and change readiness by mapping affected journeys, gathers and distils customer and frontline feedback into actionable insights, and supports continuous improvement initiatives across Customer Sales and Service.
Role Specific Knowledge and Experience
Essential:
Experience analysing customer journeys, service processes, or operational workflows
Ability to work with data, feedback, and qualitative insight to identify improvement opportunities
Experience collaborating with cross-functional teams on service or system changes
Understanding of customer service environments and multi-channel interactions
Strong communication and documentation skills, including producing journey maps or process diagrams
Familiarity with continuous improvement or service design principles
Ability to work independently and manage multiple priorities
Desirable:
Experience with journey mapping tools (e.g., Miro, Lucidchart)
Exposure to CRM, telephony, chat, or digital platform capabilities
