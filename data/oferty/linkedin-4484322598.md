TMS Application Specialist - Greenfield Project - Hybrid - Logistics Sector
Our client is seeking an experienced TMS Application Specialist to join a logistics and transport technology team. This role sits between Operations and IT, ensuring Transport Management Systems (TMS) are configured, supported and continuously improved to meet evolving business requirements.
Responsibilities
Configure, maintain and support Transport Management Systems in line with operational and business requirements.
Provide 2nd line application support, investigating and resolving system issues to minimise disruption to transport operations.
Analyse transport and logistics processes, translating business requirements into effective system solutions.
Support TMS implementations, upgrades, migrations and continuous improvement initiatives.
Perform testing of new functionality, system changes and enhancements, including support for User Acceptance Testing (UAT).
Develop reports and analyse transport data to support operational performance and decision-making.
Experience
Previous experience within transport, logistics, supply chain or freight forwarding environments.
Hands-on experience supporting or administering Transport Management Systems (TMS) or similar business-critical applications.
Strong analytical and problem-solving capabilities with the ability to diagnose and resolve application issues.
Experience configuring applications and supporting business users in a live operational environment.
Understanding of system integrations, databases and SQL would be advantageous.
