Solutions Design Project Manager – Contract Logistics
Poland
Can you build a compelling logistics solution, demonstrate the commercial case and lead it through to go-live?
I’m supporting an international logistics business with the appointment of a Solutions Design & Implementation Manager in Poland.
Combining hands-on solution engineering with project ownership, this position takes customer requirements through design, costing and presentation into implementation and operational launch.
What you’ll be doing
• Turn customer briefs, RFQ data and tender requirements into tailored logistics solutions.
• Analyse complex operational data to develop practical processes, capacity models and resource requirements.
• Produce warehouse layouts, process flows and infrastructure concepts.
• Develop cost calculations, pricing models, commercial assumptions and business cases.
• Present proposals to customers and internal stakeholders, working closely with commercial teams.
• Coordinate cross-functional teams from project planning through to go-live.
• Manage project risks, milestones and corrective actions, supporting ramp-up and stabilisation.
• Work with operations to improve processes and translate analysis into measurable action.
Who would suit the role?
You could be an established Solutions Design Manager, a logistics engineer with implementation experience, or a Project Manager with substantial hands-on design responsibility.
You’ll need experience within contract logistics or a comparable warehouse environment, strong analytical skills and confidence developing the financial case behind a solution.
We’re particularly interested in people who can explain what they have personally designed, costed and helped implement, and who are comfortable presenting their recommendations to customers.
Strong MS Office skills are important, with CAD and specialist calculation tools an advantage. A relevant degree in logistics, engineering, business or a related field is also sought.
Package on offer:
PLN 18,000–23,000 gross per month
This is an opportunity to combine technical depth, customer interaction and project delivery within an international logistics environment.
