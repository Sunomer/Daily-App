Are you an experienced supply chain leader passionate about driving operational excellence, shaping processes and influencing performance across international markets?
Imperial Brands, a global FMCG business supported by approximately 25,000 employees, has an exciting opportunity for a
Head of Supply Chain Planning & Delivery – Europe
to join our Global Supply Chain (GSC) function in Warsaw.
In this newly created leadership role, you will drive consistency, efficiency and continuous improvement across our European markets, overseeing regional supply chain governance, Integrated Business Planning (IBP), distribution and replenishment planning (DRP), and customer service.
Leading a team of three, you will work closely with senior stakeholders to align regional priorities, strengthen supply chain performance and drive transformation, ensuring a coordinated approach to planning and delivery.
Principle Accountabilities
Establish and maintain consistent supply chain processes, standards and governance frameworks across European markets, ensuring alignment with the global operating model.
Provide strategic oversight of regional supply chain planning, including IBP, distribution and replenishment planning (DRP) and customer service, ensuring alignment of priorities across a planning horizon of up to 36 months.
Define and monitor KPIs, identify opportunities for improvement and drive initiatives that enhance service levels, operational efficiency, cost-to-serve and supply chain resilience.
Partner with senior leaders across Commercial, Manufacturing, Logistics, Finance and Planning to manage competing priorities, facilitate decision-making and ensure alignment across European markets and clusters.
Drive the adoption of standardised processes, new technologies and global supply chain initiatives, ensuring effective change management and sustainable improvements.
Lead, coach and develop a team of three direct reports, fostering collaboration, accountability and continuous improvement across regional delivery activities.
Skills and Experience Required
Significant experience in supply chain management, planning, distribution or customer service within a complex, multinational organisation.
Strong expertise in one or more of the following areas: Integrated Business Planning (IBP), Sales & Operations Planning (S&OP), Distribution Requirements Planning (DRP), demand planning or supply chain project management.
Proven experience developing and implementing supply chain governance frameworks, standardised processes and continuous improvement initiatives across multiple markets or business units.
Strong stakeholder management and influencing skills, with the ability to engage senior leaders, navigate competing priorities and drive alignment across cross-functional teams.
Demonstrated leadership experience, with the ability to develop high-performing teams, lead transformation initiatives and deliver measurable business improvements.
Excellent analytical, problem-solving and communication skills, with fluency in English. Previous experience within FMCG is highly desirable, while tobacco industry experience would be an advantage.
