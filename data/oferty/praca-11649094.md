For our client, an international company creating a new business services center in Wroclaw, we are currently looking for a:
Procurement Specialist with German
responsible for efficient and compliant purchasing operations through the processing of procurement transactions, maintenance of procurement data, supplier-related administrative activities and support for requisitioners & business users.
Location:
new GBS Centre in Wroclaw, Poland
Office (initial mode), Hybrid (long-term solution)
Job description
The Senior Procurement Specialist is responsible for executing operational procurement activities within the Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process in accordance with established procedures, policies and controls.
The position also supports the resolution of complex operational issues, contributes to process improvement initiatives and knowledge sharing within the team
Responsibilities
Managing and executing purchase order activities to ensure continuity of supply and an efficient Procure-to-Pay (P2P) process.
Validating purchase requisitions and converting them into purchase orders.
Managing the three-way matching process for purchase orders, goods receipts and invoices, including resolving discrepancies with suppliers and requisitioners.
Maintaining supplier master data and supporting supplier onboarding activities.
Maintaining supplier catalogs, pricing information and material master data within procurement systems.
Supporting inventory and stock replenishment activities where applicable.
Providing first-line support to requisitioners regarding procurement-related processes and transactions.
Contributing to continuous improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing process efficiency and service quality.
Ensuring compliance with procurement policies, procedures and internal controls.
Identifying and escalating process issues, risks and service disruptions as appropriate.
Contributing to continuous improvement initiatives aimed at enhancing process efficiency and service quality.
Requirements
5+ years of experience in Procurement within P2P processes: for a senior level position.
1-5 years of experience in Procurement within P2P processes: for a regular level position.
Fluent German and English, both written and spoken.
Experience working with procurement tools, preferably Onventis.
Experience working with SAP MM.
Advanced knowledge of Microsoft Office applications, particularly MS Office 365.
Strong organizational skills and a structured way of working.
Commercial acumen and business understanding.
Strong communication and problem-solving skills.
Ability to work independently, manage priorities and work effectively in a dynamic environment.
Experience in a Shared Services Centre (SSC) or Global Business Services (GBS) environment would be an advantage.
