It's more than a job
Kuehne+Nagel is one of the world’s leading logistics providers. To strengthen our Integrated Logistics organization in Wroclaw, we are looking for a Quality Auditor who will support quality, compliance, and continuous improvement across complex international supply chain operations.In this role, you will help ensure adherence to internal and external quality standards, customer requirements, and, where applicable, GxP/GDP regulations. You will work closely with Operations, Excellence Management, Performance Management, Business Analytics, customers, and logistics service providers to maintain high quality standards throughout the supply chain.
‎
As Quality Auditor, you will combine auditing, supplier qualification, CAPA management, quality process improvement, and stakeholder coordination. You will act as a key quality contact for customers and logistics partners, supporting audits, investigations, documentation, training, and continuous improvement activities.The position has a strong international scope and may involve regular travel for on-site audits.
How you create impact
Plan, organize, and conduct internal, customer, external, and authority audits in line with applicable quality requirements
Manage audit findings, deviations, and CAPAs, including follow-up and effectiveness reviews
Support supplier qualification, due diligence, quality assessments, risk assessments, and ongoing supplier monitoring
Ensure quality processes, procedures, and documentation remain compliant with internal standards and customer requirements
Support GxP/GDP compliance, including Good Documentation Practices and applicable pharmaceutical and healthcare quality standards
Develop and deliver quality and GxP-related training and monitor training compliance and effectiveness
Act as a key quality contact for customers and logistics service providers and participate in regular quality review meetings
Drive continuous improvement initiatives and identify opportunities to strengthen quality performance across operations
What we would like you to bring
Higher education in logistics, transport technology, engineering, or a comparable field
Minimum 2 year of quality and/or regulatory experience within pharmaceutical, healthcare, distribution, or a similarly regulated environment
ISO 9001 Internal Auditor certification from a recognized body such as TÜV, Bureau Veritas, or DEKRA
Strong knowledge of GDP, and GxP requirements, combined with an analytical and customer-focused approach
Fluent English, strong stakeholder communication skills, and flexibility to travel internationally
What's in it for you
This role gives you the opportunity to work in an international quality environment where compliance has a direct impact on complex global supply chains. You will gain broad exposure to audits, supplier qualification, CAPA management, GxP/GDP compliance, and continuous improvement while collaborating with customers and stakeholders across multiple countries.
You will play an important role in ensuring that our logistics solutions remain compliant, reliable, and aligned with the quality expectations of highly regulated industries.
