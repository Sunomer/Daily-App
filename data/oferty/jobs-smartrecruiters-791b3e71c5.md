Job Description
We are seeking a Senior IT Project Manager for our Quality Lab to lead complex IT projects, ensuring high performance and compliance standards.
Plan, execute, and deliver IT projects in the quality lab environment, managing schedules, budgets, and resources
Collaborate with cross-functional teams to define project scope and implement quality assurance processes for IT systems
Conduct risk assessments and develop mitigation strategies while ensuring regulatory compliance
Lead project teams, fostering continuous improvement and driving adoption of best practices in IT project management
Prepare project status reports and manage vendor relationships related to IT quality lab projects
Experience in managing Document Management Systems (DMS)

Required Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or related field

7+ years of experience in IT project management, focusing on quality assurance
Expertise in project management methodologies (e.g., Agile, Scrum, Waterfall) and quality assurance processes
Strong knowledge of IT infrastructure, systems, and risk management
Proven track record of successful project delivery in complex IT environments
Excellent communication, leadership, and problem-solving skills
Experience with laboratory information management systems (LIMS)
Knowledge of GxP regulatory compliance
Experience with DMS solutions
