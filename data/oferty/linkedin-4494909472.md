About the Role
Regolith is looking for a reliable and detail-oriented Logistics Specialist Associate to support local procurement, equipment handling, and fulfilment activities.
In this role, you will coordinate with approved suppliers, collect and inspect purchased equipment, prepare items for shipment, and facilitate handovers to designated logistics partners. You will help ensure that every item is properly verified, documented, packaged, and ready for delivery.
This is a flexible, assignment-based field role, with tasks assigned according to operational requirements in your area.
Key ResponsibilitiesCollect purchased equipment and other items from approved suppliers.
Verify items against required specifications, quantities, and condition.
Safely handle, organise, and repackage items for shipment.
Coordinate with couriers, freight providers, and designated logistics partners.
Prepare and maintain procurement and shipping documents, including invoices, packing lists, and waybills.
Monitor shipments and provide timely tracking and delivery updates.
Report damaged items, discrepancies, missing documents, or other fulfilment issues.
Maintain accurate records of completed assignments.
Retrieve and temporarily store devices when required.
What We’re Looking ForPrevious experience in logistics, procurement, fulfilment, warehouse operations, or a related field is preferred.
Comfortable performing hands-on field activities, including collecting, transporting, packaging, and handing over physical items.
Has access to reliable local transportation for assignment-related travel.
Has a smartphone or computer with reliable internet access.
Strong attention to detail and ability to follow instructions.
Good communication skills in English and the local language.
Reliable, responsive, and able to work independently.
Compensation & EngagementThis is an Independent Contractor, Commission-Based opportunity. Compensation is based on completed fulfilment tasks, with assignments depending on operational requirements and order volume in your area.
Commission Structure (Per Unit):$100 – Large items, such as laptops.
$50 – Medium-sized items, such as monitors, smartphones, inverters, tables, and chairs.
$10 – Small items, such as headphones, cables, mice, and keyboards.
Important Engagement Details:Flexible, on-demand assignments; there is no guaranteed number of tasks or fixed monthly salary.
This is not a fully remote position. Assignments may require local travel to suppliers, collection points, and designated logistics partners.
Reliable local transportation is necessary to complete field assignments.
