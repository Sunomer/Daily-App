Job Description
We are seeking an experienced Business Management System (BMS) Manager to lead the development, governance, and continuous improvement of our European Business Management System. This is a strategic role focused on driving consistency, compliance, knowledge sharing, and operational excellence across multiple countries.
Key Responsibilities:
Manage and develop the European BMS, including tools, templates, guidance, training materials, and document control.
Ensure key business processes are clearly documented and accessible.
Deliver monthly compliance and BMS KPI reporting to regional leadership.
Manage ISO certifications, external audits, and certification body relationships.
Capture lessons learned from projects, client feedback, audits, and reviews, and translate them into practical guidance.
Partner with the Quality Assurance Manager to support reviews, audits, and continual improvement initiatives.
Develop training, promote best practice, and support adoption of company processes across Europe.
Drive knowledge sharing and rollout of digital and AI-enabled tools.
Deputise for the Head of Quality & Assurance when required.
Qualifications
Formal external qualification: IRCA Certified Lead Auditor (ISO 9001, ISO 14001, or ISO 45001) or an equivalent degree-level qualification in quality, business management, or a related discipline.
Background in construction, engineering, property, project management, or consultancy environments.
Proven experience managing Business Management Systems, including processes, document control, governance, reporting, and continuous improvement.
Strong understanding of ISO management systems and certification requirements.
Excellent communication, facilitation, and training skills, with the ability to engage stakeholders at all levels.
Proficient in digital technologies, knowledge management systems, and AI-enabled tools; experience with prompt engineering, AI agents, or ISO 42001 frameworks is advantageous.
Experience leading change initiatives and driving adoption of processes and standards across multiple countries and cultures.
Background in construction, property, engineering, project management, or professional consultancy environments.
Fluent in English; additional European languages are highly desirable.
Willingness to travel regularly across Europe.
