About The Role
As an Analyst II, Business Systems Analysis, you will be responsible for the accurate analysis, implementation, and verification of change requests within agreed service levels. You will investigate requests, identify appropriate solutions, and collaborate with stakeholders to ensure successful delivery of operational changes. This role plays a critical part in maintaining service quality, reducing operational risk, and supporting client outcomes. Success is measured through accuracy, timeliness, quality assurance, and continuous improvement contributions.
What You Will Be Doing
Analyse, implement, and validate change requests in accordance with established operational standards and procedures
Investigate issues, assess impacts, and determine effective solutions to support client and business requirements
Ensure all assigned requests are completed accurately within agreed service levels and quality expectations
Perform detailed verification activities to minimise operational risk and prevent processing errors
Collaborate with colleagues and stakeholders to support change implementation and resolution activities
Maintain accurate records, documentation, and audit trails for all changes and operational activities
Identify opportunities to improve processes, controls, documentation, and operational efficiency
Contribute to knowledge sharing, team capability development, and operational best practices
Support governance, compliance, and quality assurance activities to ensure controlled change delivery
Apply technical expertise and sound judgement to resolve complex operational requests and challenges
Required Qualifications
Demonstrated experience in analysing, implementing, and validating operational or system changes
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to investigate and resolve issues effectively
Excellent attention to detail and commitment to delivering high levels of accuracy
Experience assessing and mitigating operational risk through quality control and verification processes
Ability to manage multiple priorities and consistently meet established service level commitments
Strong communication and stakeholder management skills with the ability to work across teams
Experience creating and maintaining process documentation, procedures, and operational records
Degree in a relevant discipline or equivalent combination of education, training, and practical experience
Preferred Qualifications
Experience working within financial services, fintech, or highly regulated environments
Knowledge of change management, operational controls, and governance frameworks
Experience supporting process improvement and continuous improvement initiatives
Ability to coach, train, or support colleagues through knowledge sharing activities
Experience working across multiple products, platforms, or business applications
Familiarity with quality assurance methodologies and risk management practices
