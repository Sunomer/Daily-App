Overview
International pharmaceutical manufacturing organization is seeking an experienced Site Head to lead a strategically important manufacturing operation. The successful candidate will be responsible for the overall performance of the site, driving operational excellence, sustainable growth, regulatory compliance, and organizational development.
This is an opportunity to lead a highly regulated manufacturing business serving both internal and external stakeholders while playing a key role in shaping its future development and long-term competitiveness.
Key Responsibilities
Strategic Leadership
Lead and develop the site's long-term business strategy in alignment with corporate objectives.
Drive sustainable growth, operational performance, and profitability.
Identify and implement strategic initiatives that strengthen the site's competitive position and operational capabilities.
Foster a culture of accountability, continuous improvement, collaboration, and high performance.
Operational Management
Ensure reliable and efficient manufacturing, quality control, and supply chain operations.
Deliver high standards of customer service through consistent execution and supply reliability.
Oversee site-wide operational excellence programs focused on productivity, quality, efficiency, and cost optimization.
Support investments, capacity expansion projects, and technology implementation initiatives.
Financial Performance
Hold full responsibility for site financial performance.
Lead annual budgeting, forecasting, financial planning, and performance monitoring activities.
Drive profitability through operational improvements, effective resource allocation, and disciplined cost management.
Identify risks and opportunities and implement corrective actions where required.
Quality, Compliance & Governance
Ensure full compliance with applicable pharmaceutical, regulatory, quality, safety, and environmental requirements.
Maintain a strong quality culture and support continuous enhancement of quality systems and processes.
Represent the site during audits, inspections, and stakeholder interactions.
Stakeholder & Customer Management
Build and maintain strong relationships with customers, business partners, and corporate stakeholders.
Act as the senior representative of the site in internal and external interactions.
Support business development activities and long-term customer partnerships.
People Leadership
Lead, inspire, and develop a multidisciplinary leadership team.
Strengthen organizational capabilities through talent development, succession planning, and employee engagement.
Promote cross-functional collaboration and effective communication throughout the organization.
Candidate Profile
University degree in Pharmacy, Chemistry, Engineering, Life Sciences, Manufacturing, or a related discipline.
Significant leadership experience within the pharmaceutical, healthcare, life sciences, or another highly regulated manufacturing environment.
Proven track record of managing operational and financial performance within a manufacturing setting.
Experience leading complex organizations through growth, transformation, or operational improvement initiatives.
Strong understanding of pharmaceutical manufacturing and quality requirements.
Demonstrated success in implementing operational excellence, continuous improvement, Lean, Six Sigma, or equivalent methodologies.
Experience managing manufacturing, supply chain, quality, engineering, or related operational functions.
Solid commercial and financial acumen.
Strategic thinker with a hands-on leadership approach.
Professional proficiency in English is required.
