TD SYNNEX is accelerating its SAP EWM transformation across a network of European distribution centers. We are looking for an experienced SAP EWM Consultant to support upcoming rollouts, strengthen our internal EWM expertise, and improve logistics operations across Europe.
What You'll Do
Lead and support SAP EWM implementation and rollout projects across Europe.
Design, configure, and optimize SAP EWM solutions.
Partner with business stakeholders, IT teams, and implementation partners.
Support the full project lifecycle, from requirements gathering through go-live and hypercare.
Analyze and resolve complex warehouse and logistics process challenges.
Coach colleagues and help build internal SAP EWM expertise.
Contribute to continuous improvement and the warehouse technology roadmap.
What We're Looking For
5+ years of SAP consulting experience.
Strong hands-on SAP EWM configuration expertise.
Experience delivering multiple SAP EWM implementations or rollouts.
Good understanding of warehouse, logistics, and distribution processes.
Strong analytical, problem-solving, and communication skills.
Ability to work independently and collaborate in an international environment.
Fluent English. An additional European language is a plus.
Willingness to travel when required.
Nice to have
SAP WM knowledge, particularly in WM-to-EWM transition environments.
Experience in high-volume distribution or supply chain operations.
Project or workstream leadership experience.
Knowledge of AI capabilities within SAP.
Exposure Microsoft Copilot, AI agents, or automation technologies.
Why join us?
Be part of a major SAP EWM transformation across Europe.
Work on large-scale warehouse operations supporting millions of customer shipments.
Become a key SAP EWM expert within an international team.
Combine hands-on delivery, innovation, and knowledge sharing.
Make a direct impact on the future of logistics operations at TD SYNNEX.
