About the Role
The Specialist, Sales Support, drives seamless order management and sales operations across DKSH Poland. Based in
Warsaw
, this role enables commercial excellence through efficient and precise day-to-day business execution.
What You Will Deliver
Support the Sales Manager in day-to-day business operations, providing timely commercial and administrative coordination
Manage end-to-end order processing, including order confirmation, transmission, and supervision through to delivery
Follow up on purchase and sales orders to ensure accuracy, compliance, and on-time fulfillment
Obtain price offers from suppliers and calculate competitive customer proposals in consultation with the Sales Manager
Coordinate with forwarding companies and warehouses on logistics, pricing, and locations
Respond to customer and supplier queries in Polish and English, ensuring professional and timely resolution
Maintain SAP master data integrity and ensure legal conformity of Value Added Tax (VAT) transactions for both purchase and sales orders
Monitor stock reorder levels, coordinate sample dispatch, and maintain a customer complaints log with timely follow-up
What You Bring
Professional qualification in commercial or trading disciplines
8 to 10 years of experience in purchasing, sales administration, or customer service
Prior experience in distribution, trading, or export/import environments
Strong knowledge and understanding of supply chain operations and best practices
Proficiency in tools including Microsoft Excel, Word, and PowerPoint; familiarity with SAP and Salesforce preferred
Fluency in Polish and English, both written and spoken
Precise, organized, and customer-oriented with a proactive, reliable, and pragmatic working style
Why Join DKSH
Flexible time arrangement with partial work from home possibilities
Lunch vouchers and private health insurance
Centrally located office in Warsaw city centre
At DKSH, we help companies grow globally and enable people to perform at their best. You will be part of an organization that values accountability, collaboration, and long-term partnerships.
