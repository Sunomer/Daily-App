Company Description
IDEO (by ID Logistics) is a specialized 4PL provider established in 2014 in France, offering comprehensive supply chain management and orchestration services. Operating largely in Europe, IDEO focuses on strategic logistics planning, supply chain visibility, and managing multiple 3PL providers rather than physical transport. IDEO operates as part of the ID Logistics network, frequently utilizing a European Control Tower approach to manage transport.
Role Description
Project Manager (Productivity 4PL control tower) leads cross-functional productivity and continuous improvement projects by analyzing operational performance, intermodal opportunities and market know-how, identifying efficiency opportunities, managing stakeholders, driving business change, mitigating risks, and delivering measurable cost, service, and process improvements across the end-to-end transportation and control tower network.
