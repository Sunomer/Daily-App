About The Role
The S/4 PPDS Consultant plays a critical role in the SCM Deployment transformation stream, supporting Central & EMEA factory planning initiatives under the direction of the PP/DS Lead. This role is responsible for configuring and implementing Production Planning and Detailed Scheduling (PPDS) capabilities within S/4HANA, enabling manufacturing organizations to optimize planning runs, scheduling, and capacity management.
In this position, the consultant will work closely with cross-functional teams to translate business requirements into robust PPDS configurations, participate in fit-gap workshops, and ensure quality delivery through rigorous testing and documentation.
Essential Job Functions
Configure PPDS planning runs, heuristics, planning parameters, and scheduling profiles for deployment scope
Prepare functional specifications and PPDS configuration documentation
Support fit-gap workshops and document PPDS business requirements
Conduct unit testing, SIT support, and defect resolution for PPDS processes
Assist with PPDS integration testing with IBP and Manufacturing workstreams
Support PPDS data migration and cutover activities for planning master data
Qualifications & Experience
Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Engineering, or IT
SAP PPDS configuration experience with implementation delivery in manufacturing context
Knowledge of PPDS heuristics, capacity planning, and scheduling in S/4HANA
Experience with PPDS unit testing and integration testing support
Good documentation skills and ability to work in offshore/nearshore delivery model
Nice To Have
SAP PPDS certification
IBP-PPDS integration experience
