About The Role
As Associate Director for Planning Center of Excellence – Capability you will be responsible for the organization of Planning BPXs and Planning Masterdata Team. The organization has recently gone through ERP system change so you will be responsible for adoption of Integrated Business Planning by BY within planning community. You will be responsible for ensuring sufficient organizational capability and capacity exists to execute the work in the area of step changing the planning processes. You will be responsible for driving mastery and growth within the planning organization, to exploit existing solutions and create the capability foundation necessary to implement new technologies and deliver business strategies. You will be accountable for the ensuring the standard processes are defined, executed and continuously improved within the supply chain organization to deliver the KPIs.
You will be responsible for the roles of:
Planning Business Process Leads - BPX organization
Planning Masterdata Management Team ( 4 people)
Continuous Improvement Lead
Please be informed that this is
12-18 months fixed term contract

continuous improvement for planning processes and BY adoption

What We Want You To Bring Along
Minimum 10 years of professional experience in Supply Chain, Planning, Operations or related areas, ideally gained within a complex, international organization.
Strong end-to-end Supply Chain experience, with a solid understanding of the planning flow from suppliers and materials, through manufacturing, to distribution.
Very good understanding of key Supply Planning processes, particularly:
Distribution Requirements Planning (DRP)
Master Production Scheduling (MPS)
Material Requirements Planning (MRP)
production / external operations planning
initiative and customization planning.
Proven experience in leading major change or transformation, ideally connected with ERP implementation, planning-system transformation or redesign of Supply Chain processes.
Strong knowledge of ERP and advanced planning solutions. Experience with Blue Yonder (BY) / Integrated Business Planning would be a significant advantage.
Proven ability to drive system and process adoption across a large organization, rather than simply implementing a new tool.
Experience in establishing, standardizing and continuously improving planning processes, including defining standards, governance and KPIs.
Strong people leadership experience, including managing teams, developing capabilities, coaching employees, recruitment and performance management.
Ability to manage a diverse organization covering different areas of expertise, such as Business Process Experts/Leads, Master Data and Continuous Improvement.
Experience in working across functions and effectively partnering with Supply Chain, Manufacturing, IT and business stakeholders.
Strong project/program management capabilities, including managing readiness, testing, implementation and change management.
Experience in budget and headcount management.
Excellent analytical and strategic thinking combined with the ability to translate business requirements into practical processes and solutions.
Excellent communication, influencing and stakeholder-management skills, particularly in an environment with multiple and sometimes conflicting priorities.
Fluent English, allowing the candidate to operate effectively in a global organization.
