We are seeking an experienced Process Efficiency Lead to improve processes and ways of working across the region. The role is focused on increasing efficiency, reducing costs, improving quality, and supporting sustainable business improvements.
You will lead regional process improvement initiatives from analysis through implementation, working with cross-functional teams and stakeholders to identify opportunities and deliver practical solutions.
This role will be based in Warsaw and will be a hybrid position.
We Will Count On You To
Analyze processes and identify inefficiencies, risks, and improvement opportunities.
Lead regional process improvement projects from initiation to completion.
Design and implement solutions that improve efficiency, quality, and productivity.
Use data to identify root causes, support decisions, and measure results.
Work with cross-functional teams to map processes and develop recommendations.
Apply process improvement methodologies such as Lean, Six Sigma, or Agile.
Coordinate timelines, risks, stakeholders, and project communication.
Support the adoption and sustainability of improved processes.
What You Need To Have
Experience leading regional process improvement, optimization, or transformation projects.
Strong knowledge of process analysis, mapping, and continuous improvement.
Proven project management and stakeholder management skills.
Strong analytical, communication, and presentation skills.
Ability to manage multiple priorities and work independently.
Excellent spoken and written English.
What Makes You Stand Out
Experience with Lean, Six Sigma, Agile, or similar methodologies.
Exposure automation, RPA, AI, or digital process improvement initiatives.
Experience supporting IT-related projects, such as system implementations, workflow automation, data initiatives, or technology-enabled business change.
Consulting, financial services, or insurance experience.
Ability to influence without formal authority in an international environment.
