What's on the Menu:
We are seeking a Supply Planning Manager to optimize production processes and collaborate with stakeholders across the network.
Key ingredients:
Develop short- and long-term production plans for manufacturing site.
Optimize inventory levels across all receiving plants and manage production cycles.
Reduce non-performing inventory (NPI) by proactively identifying process gaps.
Work closely with production, quality, logistics and demand planning teams in multiple countries.
Deliver best-in-class service levels and product availability at all receiving locations.
Execute mid- and long-term supply strategies aligned with company goals and KPIs.
Manage production capacity.
Partner with raw and packaging material suppliers to optimize stock levels and secure production continuity.
Drive production cost optimization.
Proactively assess and manage risks, including mitigation plans.
Lead a high-performing team across multiple locations and foster excellent cross-functional collaboration.
Implement new digital solutions and make effective use of system data.
Knowledge of ERP/SAP systems is an advantage.
Recipe for Success: Apply if it sounds like you!
At least 2–3 years of supply chain experience, preferably in supply planning.
Analytical, data-driven approach with a focus on problem solving and KPI delivery.
Fluency in English and Polish.
Advanced Excel skills.
Experience working cross-functionally.
Confident decision-making and the ability to work under time pressure.
Experience in managing and coaching teams.
