Job Description
ID: JR0082228
YOUR MISSION
Operations are fundamental to Glovo business. We are currently building a strong team to re-invent how we want to create the most efficient solutions with our 3rd Party Logistics Partners - 3PLs.
Be a key role in developing the 3PL relationship in Poland and find ways to improve the quality and scale up the solutions. Analyse partners operational performance, profitability and growth. Have a real impact on seamingless operations to deliver the best possible quality for our end-users.
THE JOURNEY
Design and optimize the Third-Party Logistics (3PL) framework, defining the right next steps for each partner.
Support the Legal and Operations teams through diligent and meticulous management of 3PL and rider documentation, ensuring full compliance with labor regulations.
Develop processes and long-term projects to improve the efficiency of the 3PL setup, reporting directly to the Courier Ops & 3PL Manager and Head of Ops.
Identify industry trends and act proactively to outperform competitors, with a focus on rider experience.
Collaborate with the Operations team to guarantee the best possible user experience and position Glovo as the favorite delivery app for both riders and customers in Poland.
Oversee daily relationships with 3PL partners, managing their requests and ensuring an optimal rider supply.
Qualifications
WHAT YOU WILL BRING TO THE RIDE
Previous experience in a fast paced and demanding environment (e.g. consulting, tech industry).
Experience in the delivery or on-demand industry is considered a plus.
Strong stakeholder management skills - mixing good vibes with relentless focus to put Glovo objectives as priority.
Proficiency with Google Suite (or Microsoft Office).
Fluency in Polish and English.
Strong analytical skills with a results oriented, autonomous, and efficient mindset.
Ability to challenge and debate issues of importance to the organization.
Capacity to approach situations from different perspectives and propose creative solutions
Persuasive communicator, with attention to detail and evidence based reasoning.
Collaborative and adaptable, with a strong commitment to getting the job done.
