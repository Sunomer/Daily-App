Position Overview
The Director, Network Strategy & Transformation is the global leader responsible for shaping and executing the distribution network strategy supporting the establishment and future growth of DePuy Synthes across the US, EMEA, APAC, LATAM and Canada.
As a member of the extended Global Distribution & Customer Logistics (GDCL) Leadership Team, the Director leads a global portfolio of strategic network and transformation initiatives spanning distribution footprint, capacity, product flows, service and operating models. The role provides end-to-end leadership across program governance, stakeholder engagement, project financials, cross-functional dependencies, risk management and benefits realization.
Working across Commercial, Planning, Manufacturing, Logistics, Finance, Digital/IT and regional organizations, the Director translates business priorities into network scenarios, investment decisions and executable multi-year roadmaps. The role balances growth, service, resilience, working capital, capacity and cost-to-serve while ensuring quality and regulatory requirements are embedded throughout network strategy and execution.
A key focus of the role is establishing the future DePuy Synthes network and operating model while developing the capabilities, technology, analytics and talent required for a scalable and sustainable organization.
Tasks & Responsibilities
Lead the development and execution of the global distribution network strategy, translating business priorities into network scenarios, strategic recommendations and a multi-year roadmap across regions.
Lead the portfolio of strategic network and transformation programs across distribution footprint, capacity, product flows, service models and operating models, ensuring effective governance, prioritization, resource planning, risk management and benefits realization.
Identify and develop strategic opportunities and translate them into robust solutions, business cases, investment requirements and execution plans in partnership with Finance, business and functional leaders.
Lead network workstreams supporting the establishment of DePuy Synthes as a standalone organization, addressing critical dependencies, capability gaps and future-state requirements while shaping sustainable operating models, governance and decision rights.
Drive cross-functional execution and measurable improvements across service, cost-to-serve, resilience, working capital, capacity and scalability, ensuring appropriate visibility and escalation of portfolio performance and risks.
Advance technology-enabled network capabilities through AI, automation, advanced analytics, network modelling and scenario planning in partnership with Digital/IT and functional teams.
Provide the extended GDCL Leadership Team with a clear global view of project performance, financials, risks, interdependencies and functional engagement to support executive decision-making and delivery of the overall network strategy.
Lead, coach and develop a high-performing and flexible global team while strengthening organizational capabilities across network strategy, program leadership, analytics, technology-enabled decision-making and succession planning.
