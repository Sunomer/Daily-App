About The Role
Manages delivery of the SAP SCM Deployment for assigned region or workstream cluster (Central or EMEA). Responsible for project planning, workstream coordination, stakeholder management, and on-time delivery of SCM implementation scope.
Essential Job Functions
Lead project delivery for SAP SCM Deployment covering assigned region or workstream cluster
Develop and maintain project plan, RAID log, and schedule across EWM, Planning, Manufacturing, Finance, and technical workstreams
Manage client and business stakeholder relationships and facilitate regional steering committee reporting
Coordinate cross-stream interdependencies with Program Level, GTS, and Group Reporting streams
Oversee resource allocation and team performance across SCM workstreams
Drive go-live readiness, cutover coordination, and hypercare planning for SCM deployment
Qualifications & Experience
Bachelor's or Master's degree in IT, Business, Supply Chain, or Engineering
Extensive SAP project management experience with SCM or logistics implementation delivery
Proven delivery of SAP S/4HANA SCM (EWM, IBP, PP, Manufacturing) projects
Strong stakeholder management, RAID governance, and executive reporting skills
Experience managing distributed offshore/nearshore/onshore SCM delivery teams
Proficiency in project management tools and SAP Activate methodology
Nice To Have
PMP or PRINCE2 certification
SCM-focused SAP programme management experience in EMEA or manufacturing environments
