About The Role
The TransferGo Card is a Visa debit card, virtual and physical, and it works with Apple Pay and Google Pay. It's live in the UK, and more countries are coming.
You'll work between product, engineering, our card partners and the rest of the business, and pick up the work that falls between them. You'll agree priorities with our PM for cards and subscriptions, then run with them. One month you might be working out why cardholders don't activate and what would change that. The next you might be running a country launch with Engineering, Legal, Marketing, Compliance and our partners. When the same job comes round a second time, you'll build the tracker, dashboard or automation so it doesn't need a person the third time.
Cards also come with rules we don't write. Card schemes set deadlines, Apple and Google certify every wallet launch, and partners have their own requirements. You'll make sure none of it gets missed, and that as little of it as possible is done by hand.
Our PM for cards and subscriptions decides what we build and launch. Engineering owns how it gets built. You run card operations day to day: the projects you take on, from the first question to a shipped and measured result, and the systems that keep cards running without extra meetings.
What Success Looks Like After Six Months
You've shipped two or three projects that moved card activation, active cardholders or spend.
Scheme deadlines, partner requests and launch tasks all live in one system you built, and nobody hears about one late.
At least one weekly manual job in Cards is now done by an automation or tool you built.
What You’ll Be Doing
Own problems end to end
Take a problem from a vague question to a shipped, measured result. Scope it, bring in the right people, run it and close it.
Use SQL and Amplitude to find where cardholders drop off between ordering, activating and spending, and work out what a fix is worth. Come with a recommendation.
Read support contacts and partner reports, rank what's hurting customers, and make sure each item has an owner.
Build with AI
Build what the team needs yourself. For example, a tool that turns each new scheme bulletin into a short summary with draft tasks and deadlines, or a weekly card dashboard leadership reads instead of asking for updates.
Prototype product ideas before engineering commits to them, so the PM can decide with something real in front of them.
Keep cards running
Run card launches and wallet certifications with Engineering, Legal, Marketing, Compliance and our partners, from one tracker with one readiness date and an owner for every item.
Keep our scheme mandate tracker up to date, and turn each bulletin or partner notice into tasks with owners and dates.
Work through operational issues with our card partners. When something needs escalating, write it in three lines: what we need, by when, and what it blocks.
What You Might Work On
Finding out why some customers order a card and never use it, and testing what changes that.
Using AI to turn our card partner's monthly reports into a one-page summary of what changed and what needs attention.
Writing the launch playbook for the next country, and automating half of it.
Making the card easier to start using in the first week.
