Job Title
Customer Service Desk Specialist (All genders)
Job Description
As a Customer Service Desk Specialist, you will support the day-to-day management of product availability and order allocation for Poland and the Baltic countries. You will work closely with Demand Planning, Sales and warehouse teams to help ensure that customer orders are released on time and in full.
Your Role
Coordinating the daily order allocation process for Poland and the Baltic countries.
Monitoring warehouse entries and product availability to support timely order fulfilment.
Working closely with the Demand Planning team and acting as a point of contact for Sales regarding product availability and order status.
Preparing and maintaining daily operational reports and providing updates during Operations stand-up meetings.
Identifying potential supply or order issues and supporting the team in finding solutions.
Partnering with different stakeholders to ensure smooth information flow and timely execution of operational activities.
You're The Right Fit If
You have completed a Bachelor’s or Master’s degree, ideally in Supply Chain, Logistics, Business Administration, Economics or a related field.
You have some first professional experience ideally in operations, supply chain, logistics, order management, customer service or a similar environment.
You are comfortable working with data and have a good knowledge of MS Excel.
Experience with SAP or another ERP system would be an advantage
You are well organized, detail-oriented and comfortable managing multiple priorities.
You enjoy working with different teams and have strong communication and problem-solving skills.
You are fluent in Polish and English.
