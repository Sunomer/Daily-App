About the role
We are looking for an experienced and proactive
EMO Planner (External Manufacturing Operations Planner)
to manage supply and production planning activities with our external manufacturing partners.
In this role, you will be responsible for ensuring products are manufactured and delivered on time, in full, and in line with business demand and inventory targets. Acting as the key operational link between external manufacturers and internal stakeholders, you will coordinate production plans, monitor inventory performance, manage supply risks and drive operational excellence across the supply chain.
You will work closely with Supply Planning, Demand Planning, Procurement, Logistics, Quality, Customer Service and external manufacturing partners to ensure uninterrupted product supply while supporting business growth and customer satisfaction.
Your responsibilities
Manage supply and production plans with external manufacturing partners to ensure product availability.
Balance production requirements against demand forecasts, inventory targets and manufacturing capacity.
Monitor inventory levels, safety stock and Days of Supply (DOS) performance.
Create and release purchase orders while ensuring timely supplier confirmations.
Identify, escalate and mitigate supply risks, capacity constraints and service issues.
Coordinate logistics activities and shipment execution to ensure on-time product deliveries.
Lead regular operational reviews and planning discussions with external manufacturers.
Support new product launches, product transfers and supply chain change initiatives.
Resolve supply disruptions, material shortages, delivery delays and operational challenges.
Maintain planning parameters, master data and relevant system information within SAP and planning tools.
Analyze supply chain performance and recommend improvements to enhance service, efficiency and inventory management.
Collaborate effectively with internal and external stakeholders to deliver supply chain objectives.
The experience we're looking for
Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain, Business, Engineering, Operations Management or a related field.2+ years of experience in Supply Planning, Production Planning, Materials Planning or Supply Chain Operations.
Experience working with suppliers, contract manufacturers or external business partners.
Strong understanding of supply chain planning processes, inventory management and production planning principles.
Experience managing supply risks, capacity constraints and operational issue resolution.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills with the ability to make data-driven decisions.
Experience working with SAP and advanced Excel.Experience with planning systems such as Kinaxis RapidResponse is highly desirable.
Excellent communication and stakeholder management skills.
Ability to work in a fast-paced, international environment and manage multiple priorities simultaneously.
Proactive mindset, strong ownership and a continuous improvement mentality.
