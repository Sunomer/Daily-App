Job Summary
The Senior Customer Support Process Analyst serves as the accountable Process Owner for Technical Support processes associated with Equinix interconnection and virtual network services, as distinct from physical colocation and data center services.
In scope are software-defined services delivered on Platform Equinix, such as Equinix Fabric, Fabric Cloud Router, Network Edge virtual devices and Equinix Internet Access. The role governs how support for these services is designed, documented, measured and adopted across tiers, and owns the domain improvement agenda. Applying process, impact and root cause analysis, it drives meaningful improvements within Technical Support and initiates changes with cross-functional teams in Product, Engineering and systems, making support more scalable, adaptable and efficient. The result is fewer avoidable contacts, faster resolution, fewer repeat issues and a better customer and engineer experience.
Scope of ownership
Own end-to-end Technical Support processes for assigned interconnection and virtual network services, from intake and triage through troubleshooting, escalation and closure
Provide the process overlay across Support tiers, while tier leaders remain accountable for staffing and daily delivery
Cover journeys such as provisioning and turn-up, virtual device configuration, routing and BGP, cloud and provider connectivity, access, Portal and API workflows, and complex incidents
Exclude physical colocation and data center services (cage, cabinet, power, cross connects), while defining clean handoffs where virtual services depend on physical elements such as remote ports and LOA/CFA
Own the improvement agenda, using process, impact and root cause analysis to make support more scalable, adaptable and efficient across Customer Support, Product and systems
Be accountable for outcome KPIs: case deflection and avoidable contacts, MTTR, reopen and repeat-contact rate, and proactive problem prevention, balanced with CSAT and first-contact resolution
Act as the Customer Support lead for these services, without replacing Product, Engineering, incident management or case handling
Responsibilities
Core responsibilities
Process ownership and governance
Define the vision, guardrails and target operating model for interconnection and virtual network services support processes, aligned with Customer Support strategy
Maintain clear decision rights, RACI and interfaces across Technical Support, Product, Engineering, GTM, IT and Customer Support Operations
Approve material process changes and production readiness, ensuring each has an owner, success measures, risk controls and post-implementation review; escalate trade-offs as needed
End-to-end process design and documentation
Map current and future-state workflows using methods such as SIPOC, value-stream mapping and swimlanes
Define tier ownership, routing and handoff criteria, escalation paths, exception handling and compliance boundaries
Own standards for SOPs, knowledge articles, troubleshooting guides and escalation playbooks, validating with SMEs that they reflect real product behavior
Performance, quality and support intelligence
Establish a balanced measurement system covering customer outcomes, process health, quality, demand and adoption
Evaluate QA findings, customer feedback, case drivers, resolution times, escalations and knowledge gaps to identify recurring pain points
Turn evidence into prioritized improvements and business cases, and use post-change reviews to confirm benefits and decide whether to scale, adapt or stop
Continuous product and support improvement
Create and manage a domain improvement roadmap covering process, product, system, automation, knowledge and enablement
Engage early in Product roadmap and release planning, channeling validated support requirements, acceptance criteria and supportability needs to Product and Engineering
Drive shift-left through self-service, knowledge automation, AI-assisted support and better diagnostics, and lead cross-functional improvement initiatives
Change readiness, UAT and adoption
Assess the impact of product and system changes on workflows, case volumes, skills, tooling, knowledge and customer communications
Co-develop acceptance criteria and UAT scenarios, and confirm readiness with WFM, QA, Enablement, tier leaders and delivery teams before go-live
Coordinate rollout, communication, training and knowledge updates, then monitor early-life performance and adoption
Stakeholder leadership and influence
Serve as the primary Customer Support process contact for assigned interconnection and virtual network services across global teams
Present evidence, risks and recommendations to leadership and governance forums, influencing without direct authority to secure commitments
Mentor Customer Support Process Analysts and domain contributors to strengthen process, analytics and improvement capability
Key deliverables
Approved end-to-end process maps, RACI and operating standards for interconnection and virtual network services support
Current SOPs, troubleshooting guides, escalation playbooks and governed knowledge content
KPI and control framework with regular reporting on case deflection, MTTR, reopen and repeat-contact rate, and recurring problem prevention
Prioritized improvement roadmap in which each initiative is based on process, impact and root cause analysis and linked to a domain objective, baseline and measurable target
Delivered improvements across Technical Support, Product and systems that measurably reduce avoidable contacts, resolution time and repeat issues
Operational requirements, impact assessments, UAT sign-offs and go-live readiness decisions
Post-implementation reviews confirming benefits against objectives, with corrective actions where needed
Qualifications
Bachelor’s degree or equivalent practical experience in business improvement, customer support, service design, operations, engineering or a related field
Proven years of experience in customer support, technical support, process ownership, process improvement, service design or operational transformation in a global environment
Demonstrated experience governing or redesigning complex cross-functional support processes and delivering measurable improvements
Working knowledge of interconnection and virtual networking environments (for example Layer 2/Layer 3 connectivity, BGP routing, virtual network functions and cloud connectivity), technical troubleshooting, escalation models and product or engineering interfaces
Experience translating operational evidence into process, product and system requirements, acceptance criteria and business cases
Proficiency in process mapping, root cause analysis, performance measurement and change management. Lean Six Sigma or comparable certification is preferred
Experience with CRM, case management, knowledge management, analytics and automation platforms; Salesforce, Power BI or comparable tools are advantageous
Core capabilities
End-to-end systems thinking and strong customer orientation
Process governance, decision-making and accountability
Technical curiosity and ability to work credibly with Product and Engineering teams
Advanced analytical reasoning and root cause analysis
Clear written documentation and executive-level presentation
Influence, negotiation and stakeholder alignment without direct authority
Structured project delivery, prioritization and benefit realization
Ability to operate independently while building an effective network of SMEs and contributors
