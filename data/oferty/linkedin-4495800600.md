The Impact You'll Make
What does it take to ensure that products manufactured by external partners meet the quality standards our customers expect? It takes technical expertise, strong collaboration and people who are not afraid to challenge the status quo.
At
Danfoss Climate Solutions
, we're looking for a
Senior Engineer, EMP Customer/Supplier Quality
to join our team in Grodzisk Mazowiecki on a
fixed-term contract until June 2028
.
In this role, you will play a vital part in ensuring the quality and reliability of our Externally Manufactured Products (EMP), supporting both Danfoss customers and finished product suppliers. Your technical expertise and proactive approach will help maintain high product and process quality standards, strengthen supplier performance and enhance customer satisfaction.
You'll work closely with international stakeholders, leading problem-solving activities, investigating quality issues, driving supplier improvements and supporting product development and industrialization processes. From resolving customer complaints to ensuring quality requirements are met before serial production launch, your work will have a direct impact on the quality of our heating and cooling solutions.
What You’ll Be Doing
Ensure the quality and reliability of finished products manufactured by external suppliers, driving compliance with Danfoss quality requirements and customer expectations.
Lead customer and supplier complaint investigations, using structured problem-solving methods such as 8D to identify root causes and implement effective corrective and preventive actions.
Collaborate with external manufacturing partners to improve product and process quality, conduct supplier audits and follow up on improvement initiatives.
Support new product development and industrialization projects, ensuring quality requirements are fulfilled from the design phase through supplier PPAP approval and serial production launch.
Work closely with international teams, including Engineering, Sales and Application Engineers, to resolve technical quality challenges, strengthen quality processes and continuously improve customer satisfaction.
What We're Looking For
A degree in Engineering, preferably in Manufacturing, Electronics, Mechanical Engineering, Quality or a related technical field.
Approximately 5 years of experience in manufacturing, supplier or customer quality engineering, ideally in an international manufacturing environment.
Good knowledge of quality management systems and standards, particularly ISO 9001 and/or IATF 16949.
Practical experience with structured problem-solving and quality methodologies, such as 8D, APQP, PPAP and Quality Core Tools.
Experience with supplier or internal audits, new product development or quality improvement projects. Knowledge of electronic or HVAC products would be an advantage.
Strong communication and collaboration skills, fluency in English and confidence working with international stakeholders, along with proficiency in MS Office.
Nice to have:
ISO 9001 or VDA 6.3 auditor certification and project management experience.
Don't meet every single requirement? We'd still love to hear from you!

What You'll Get From Us
Hybrid working model: 3 days per week in our Grodzisk Mazowiecki office and 2 days working remotely.
Meaningful technical challenges: the opportunity to take ownership of quality improvement activities, collaborate with international suppliers and engineering teams, and influence product quality throughout its lifecycle.
Professional development: access to training, mentoring and opportunities to expand your technical expertise within a global organization.
Supportive and inclusive environment: a workplace where your ideas are valued, collaboration matters and your well-being is important.
Attractive benefits package: annual bonus, private medical care with Lux Med, life insurance, employee pension program (PPE), co-financing of sports cards and subsidized meals in our company canteen.
