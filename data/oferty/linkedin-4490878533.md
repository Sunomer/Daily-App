What You’ll Do
In the Supplier Development Engineer position, you will:
Be responsible for developing supplier manufacturing capability, capacity, scalability, and operational readiness to support current and future business requirements.
Lead structured supplier development activities to identify and close systemic manufacturing, process, capacity, and operational gaps.
Provide proactive supplier development support for business growth, new product launches, supplier transfers, capacity expansion, and strategic supplier initiatives.
Partner with Supplier Quality, Supply Chain, Engineering, Operations, COE, and suppliers to drive measurable and sustainable improvement in supplier capability and performance.
The positions requires availability for up-to 60% travel.
Qualifications
Bachelor’s degree in Engineering, Manufacturing, Quality, Supply Chain, or related technical discipline
Minimum 5 years of experience in Manufacturing Engineering, Supplier Development, Supplier Quality, Operations, or Supply Chain including experience with wire harness, cable assembly, connector assembly, or electro-mechanical manufacturing processes. Demonstrated knowledge of wire processing, crimping, soldering, testing, process validation, error-proofing, traceability systems, and manufacturing process controls
Strong knowledge of manufacturing processes and supplier-development methodologies
Demonstrated experience assessing manufacturing capability and driving supplier improvement
Working knowledge of APQP, PPAP, PFMEA, Control Plans, MSA, SPC, and structured problem solving
Experience with capacity analysis and production-rate readiness
Knowledge of Lean / Continuous Improvement methodologies
Strong analytical and data-driven decision-making skills
Ability to influence and lead supplier improvement without direct authority
Ability to lead cross-functional activities across multiple sites and functions
Additional Requirements
Lead Auditor qualification
Lean / Six Sigma certification
Experience with supplier transfers, NPI, capacity expansion, and industrialization
Experience working across multiple sites, regions, and cultures
Advanced technical or business degree
