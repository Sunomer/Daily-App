The Planning & Fulfillment Manager will focus on supply planning, demand–supply balancing, and reconciliation to drive and support S&OP from an operations perspective. Using demand plan inputs, the role will deliver robust supply planning, providing feedback and input for subsequent S&OP steps.
In addition to creating and facilitating the monthly supply planning drumbeat, the role requires engaging stakeholders and providing a ready-to-use, deployable design for supply planning and reconciliation to improve customer on-time delivery, inventory turns, and lead time. A strong implementation focus, effective change management, and clear process definitions will be key to success. The Planning & Fulfillment Manager will be accountable for inventory and supply plan adherence.
The Work Model For The Role Is
You will be mainly accountable for :
Monitoring inventory levels, work with procurement to ensure timely delivery, and coordinate with cross-functional teams to deliver finished goods efficiently.
Developing and maintaining relationships with key suppliers and vendors, and lead projects to improve planning and fulfillment metrics such as On Time Delivery, Inventory Turns, Lead Time, and Plan Accuracy.
Managing stakeholders and defining roles and responsibilities within the S&OP process, including governance of the monthly supply planning drumbeat and related KPIs and reporting.
Performing standard and ad hoc analyses related to S&OP and S&OE metrics, including identifying key root causes for non-performance and driving related improvement initiatives.
Acting as a subject matter expert for S&OP and S&OE, establish and share best practices.
Qualifications For The Role
Bachelor's degree in Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business Administration or equivalent and at least 10 years of experience in Planning & Fulfillment.
Proven track record of improving Planning & Fulfillment, particularly in Sales & Operations planning implementation and improvement in a global organization.
Strong expertise in Planning & Fulfillment domains, including Planning and Disposition, Sales & Operations Planning, and Execution.
Experience in inventory management and procurement processes with knowledge of SAP. (Modules MM, PP, WM).
Strong communication skills in English, as well as solid consulting and facilitating skills.
