About the department
The position is based in Coloplast Business Centre in Szczecin, Poland. Our P&C & Admin Department consists of specialized teams covering P&C Operations, Talent Acquisition, Employer Branding, Learning & Development, HR IT, Facility and Front Desk Coordination. Together, we provide comprehensive support to employees and leaders, continuously developing our services through standardization, digitalization and process improvement.
Main responsibilities:
Provide day-to-day P&C Operations support for our German entities, ensuring high-quality and timely service to employees and managers.
Act as a point of contact for employees and managers regarding P&C operational matters, employment documentation, employee data and HR processes.
Manage and coordinate selected employee lifecycle processes, including hiring, contractual changes, employee data changes and terminations.
Prepare, issue and coordinate employment-related documents, ensuring accuracy, timely completion and compliance with established processes.
Enter, update and ensure the accuracy of employee data in SuccessFactors.
Cooperate closely with local German P&C stakeholders and other internal teams to ensure smooth execution of operational processes.
Take ownership of assigned cases and processes, ensuring effective follow-up and end-to-end resolution.
Support the transition and knowledge transfer of German P&C processes, depending on the stage of the transition at the time of joining.
Contribute to creating and maintaining process documentation, procedures and work instructions.
Identify opportunities to simplify, standardize, digitalize and automate P&C processes.
Proactively propose and support process improvements and automation initiatives using available digital tools.
Prepare P&C data, reports and other operational information when required.
Ensure appropriate handling of confidential employee data and compliance with internal policies and applicable data protection requirements.
Support the team with other P&C Operations activities and projects as the scope of services develops.
Requirements:
Fluent German (minimum B2+/C1), both written and spoken, allowing confident communication with employees, managers and P&C stakeholders.
Very good command of English (minimum B2), both written and spoken.
Previous experience in P&C / HR Operations, HR Administration or a similar role.
Good understanding of employee lifecycle processes and HR administration.
Experience in a Shared Services / international environment would be a strong advantage.
Very good knowledge of MS Office, particularly Excel; experience with SuccessFactors or other HR systems would be an advantage.
Strong digital and automation mindset, with genuine interest in using technology, AI and automation to improve everyday work.
Continuous improvement mindset with the ability to identify opportunities to simplify, standardize and automate processes.
Strong sense of ownership and accountability, with the ability to independently manage tasks and follow them through to completion.
Proactive approach and willingness to challenge existing ways of working and propose better solutions.
Strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with attention to detail and data quality.
Ability to quickly learn new processes and work effectively in a changing and developing environment.
Very good organizational and prioritization skills, with the ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines while maintaining high quality.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with confidence in working with different stakeholders.
Open-minded, curious and not afraid of new challenges, with a willingness to take on new responsibilities as the scope of P&C Operations develops.
