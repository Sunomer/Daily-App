Job Summary
The Process Engineer designs, develops, and optimizes water, wastewater, and organic waste stream treatment processes across the full project lifecycle. The role delivers reliable, compliant, and sustainable process solutions through strong technical expertise, digital engineering tools, and close collaboration with multidisciplinary teams.
Your Main Responsabilities
Design and develop water and wastewater treatment systems by developing mass balances, flow diagrams (PFDs), layout diagrams and piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&ID), define design criteria, select suitable technologies (clarification, filtration, reverse osmosis, etc.), write functional descriptions and perform the necessary design and hydraulic calculations for the design and hydraulic calculations required for the design and hydraulic design of the system. process units and water mains.
Contribute to the planning, budget monitoring and coordination of projects by ensuring consistency between technical deliverables and financial objectives, model and optimize processes using advanced digital tools, ensure compliance with environmental, health and safety regulations, provide technical support to multidisciplinary teams throughout the project lifecycle, and integrate technological innovations as well as feedback from pilot projects into engineering standards to improve the performance and sustainability of the proposed solutions.
Your Profile
Academic / vocational education : Bachelor's or Master's degree in chemical engineering, environmental engineering or process engineering.
Relevant work experience: 3 to 8 years of experience in process engineering for water, wastewater, or industrial utilities, strong knowledge of water treatment technologies and process safety principles. Experience with process modelling and digital engineering tools (e.g. AutoCAD P&ID, Plant 3D). Familiarity with regulatory frameworks, water quality standards, and chemical handling
