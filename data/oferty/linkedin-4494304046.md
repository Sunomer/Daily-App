1. Job Purpose
Support the fast-growing Nordic MDA and Consumer Electronics business by managing the end-to-end order-to-delivery process, controlling abnormal costs, and ensuring smooth communication between customers, the sales team and internal supply chain functions.
2. Key Responsibilities
Slot management:
Manage and monitor customer delivery slots; coordinate with customers, factories and logistics partners to secure slots and avoid penalties.
ETA monitoring:
Track shipment ETAs (ocean, air and inland), proactively update customers on delivery schedules, and escalate delays in a timely manner.
Abnormal-cost control:
Identify, track and regularly review abnormal/exceptional costs (e.g. storage, demurrage, re-delivery, Sweden chemical-tax-related); drive root-cause analysis and reduction actions.
Order-to-delivery follow-up:
Follow up on overdue, current-month and current-quarter orders; send regular ETA updates to customers; arrange stock borrowing and pull-in for out-of-stock models to ensure supply-demand matching and customer satisfaction.
KPI tracking:
Monitor and report the achievement of monthly internal supply chain KPIs for the Nordic region; prepare data for regular business reviews.
Cross-functional collaboration:
Work closely with the sales team, warehouse & logistics team and HQ to continuously improve delivery performance and process efficiency.
3. Requirements (must-have)
Bachelor’s degree or above; major in supply chain, logistics, international trade or business-related fields preferred.
Minimum 3 years of relevant work experience in supply chain, logistics or order management.
Mandarin Chinese speaker with fluent English
Proficient in Microsoft Office (especially Excel).
Detail-oriented with a strong sense of ownership; good cross-cultural communication skills; able to work independently; valid work eligibility in Poland.
4. Preferred Qualifications
Work experience in Poland:
3+ years of work experience in Poland; Chinese-speaking candidates familiar with the European logistics, customs and market environment are preferred.
Supply chain background:
preferably in home appliances or consumer electronics, with exposure to order management or customer-facing supply chain.
Others:
Experience with ERP/SAP systems and with direct-delivery (drop-shipment) models or customer-facing supply chain roles.
