Job Purpose
Poszukujemy Brand Promotora odpowiedzialnego za południową Polskę, który będzie aktywnie współpracował z klientami w celu promowania naszych produktów i wzmacniania rozpoznawalności marki. Osoba na tym stanowisku będzie pełnić rolę ambasadora marki w regionie, realizując działania promocyjne oraz ściśle współpracując z zespołem sprzedaży w celu opracowywania i wdrażania strategii zwiększających rozpoznawalność marki, zaangażowanie klientów oraz ich długoterminową lojalność.
We are seeking a Brand Promotor responsible for Southern Poland, who will actively engage with customers to promote our products and enhance the visibility of our brand. Working closely with the sales team, the successful candidate will develop and implement regional strategies to grow market presence, strengthen customer relationships, increase brand recognition, and drive customer loyalty.

Key responsibilities
Drive sales growth, market development and profitability of PHINIA products and solutions within the assigned territory.
Strengthen brand awareness and customer loyalty through targeted marketing activities, product promotion and customer engagement.
Serve as the PHINIA brand and product expert, delivering impactful presentations, product demonstrations and technical training to customers and workshops.
Conduct market research and competitor analysis to identify trends, customer needs and new business opportunities.
Develop and implement local sales and marketing initiatives aligned with regional and company strategies.
Proactively identify, develop and convert new business opportunities to support revenue growth.
Provide commercial and technical support to customers, delivering solution-oriented assistance across multiple channels.
Develop and support annual sales plans, budgets and customer action plans to achieve business objectives.
Collaborate closely with regional sales, marketing and technical teams to maximise market penetration and customer satisfaction.
Represent PHINIA at national and international trade shows, exhibitions and industry events.
