Job Description
As
Global Service Owner of End-User Device Services
, you are accountable for the strategic direction, performance, and continuous improvement of Bosch’s global end-user device portfolio, including computers, mobile devices, and printers.
The role combines strategic product and service leadership with end-to-end responsibility for service quality, lifecycle excellence, supplier performance, cost competitiveness, and customer value at Bosch scale.
Key Responsibilities:
Define and execute the long-term strategy for Bosch’s global end-user device portfolio.
Translate business needs, technology trends, market benchmarks, and customer feedback into a clear portfolio roadmap
Own the commercial success of the portfolio
Own the end-to-end service perspective across the full device lifecycle, including planning, demand management, procurement, provisioning, operation, support, refresh and return.
Drive continuous improvement along the full value chain, balancing local business requirements, global standardization, user experience, cost efficiency, security, and compliance.
Steer strategic suppliers, service providers, and technology partners, ensuring reliable service delivery, performance transparency and contractual alignment.
Build and develop a high-performing product and service management organization
Ensure that local requirements and operational feedback are systematically captured, prioritized, and integrated into global portfolio development and service optimization.
