Role Overview
We are looking for a driven and ambitious General Ledger Account Specialist to perform country-level R2R activities for Poland in accordance with cluster accounting standards, including journal processing, balance-sheet reconciliations, month-end closing, statutory-reporting support, accounting-data quality and D365-based process execution.
Key Responsibilities
Entering financial data and transactions into the ledger.
Reviewing financial documents for accuracy and reconciling accounts.
Analyzing financial data and providing financial recommendations.
Ensuring financial records comply with regulatory standards.
Generating financial reports.
Collaborating with other departments to complete accurate records and provide accounting support.
Accurately prepare quarterly and annual tax reports.
Supporting the Auditor, Controller and other financial professionals in their duties as needed.
Supports implementation of standardized cluster processes and D365.
Maintenance of process documentation and controls.
Execution of cluster-defined KPIs and service standards.
What You Will Bring To The Role
General ledger maintenance, GAAP compliance, financial reporting, and account reconciliation.
Proficiency in tools like D365 or advanced Microsoft Excel.
Strong analytical thinking, attention to detail, and cross-functional communication.
Fluent English, both spoken and written.

Career growth opportunities within a global organization.
An initial 12-month employment contract, with a strong intention to establish a long-term cooperation and transition to an indefinite-term employment contract based on business needs and individual performance.
