Your Role:
The Master Data Operations Manager is responsible for leading global master data operations, ensuring the accurate, timely, and compliant creation, maintenance, and governance of master data across SAP and related enterprise systems. The role leads a global team of Master Data Specialists and SMEs, delivers operational excellence across core master data domains, drives continuous improvement and automation, and acts as a trusted business partner to functions including Supply Chain, Procurement, Manufacturing, Product Development, Finance, Retail, and Digital & Technology. The position plays a critical role in supporting business operations, ERP transformation initiatives, data quality improvements, and the transition of strategic programs into sustainable business-as-usual operations.
