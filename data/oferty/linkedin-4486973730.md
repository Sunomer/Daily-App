As our
Logistics Engineer
, you will ensure the daily support of the warehouse teams so that they can carry out their activities while respecting safety, quality, deadlines, stock accuracy and productivity. You will propose process and/or tool improvements and qualify significant developments and new processes.
What you will do:
Define tools, processes & layout in the warehouse
Propose investment and participate to the budget construction • Identify suppliers for equipment requirements (with support of the sourcing team)
Create the warehouse instructions and train the team (new process/tools)
Lead the qualification for the new process/equipment
Apply HSE rules and instructions when defining processes
Support the warehouse team for the daily operations, identify the gaps and propose corrective actions (suppliers claim, inventory discrepancy)
Lead continuous improvement action plans with warehouse team and other departments
SLE is the interface with WH manager / PE / Facility maintenance / HSE / MDM / procurement / Logistics Specialist /Production
What you need to have:
Proficiency in an ERP with a WMS (SAP is a plus)
Operational knowledge of International Logistics, Warehouse Operations, Automatic Storage Systems, Kanban
Continuous improvement and problem-solving methods (LDM, PDCA, 8DO, 5S, ...)
Practical knowledge in managing projects
Proficient in office tools (outlook, word, excel, powerpoint)
Polish fluent, English (at least B2 level)
