Regional Project Lead
The Opportunity
A global industrial technology organisation is seeking an experienced
Regional Project Lead
to lead the delivery of complex manufacturing and turnkey engineering projects.
This is a high-impact technical leadership position focused on transforming complex customer manufacturing challenges into practical, measurable solutions. The role combines advanced manufacturing expertise, project leadership, customer engagement, and cross-functional coordination to deliver improvements across productivity, quality, cost, capacity, and process performance.
The successful individual will bring a strong machining or manufacturing engineering background alongside the commercial awareness and leadership capability required to take ownership of customer projects from initial concept through implementation, validation, and final handover.
The Role
The Regional Project Lead will take end-to-end ownership of complex customer manufacturing projects, translating technical and operational requirements into complete manufacturing solutions.
The position will work across process design, tooling, fixturing, workholding, CAM strategy, cycle-time optimisation, validation, and implementation, coordinating internal specialists, customers, suppliers, and external partners throughout delivery.
Operating as an experienced individual contributor, the role will provide project leadership without direct line-management responsibility and will act as a trusted technical partner to both operational and senior customer stakeholders.
Key Responsibilities
Manufacturing Solution Development
Analyse customer components, drawings, manufacturing processes, and production requirements to define effective manufacturing strategies.
Develop complete process concepts covering operation sequencing, tooling, workholding, fixturing, and machining strategy.
Lead cycle-time studies, cost-per-part modelling, tooling selection, and process optimisation.
Support CNC and CAM strategies across multi-axis and complex machining environments.
Lead machining trials, process validation, production run-offs, and first-article approval.
Resolve technical challenges relating to tool life, tolerances, surface finish, productivity, and process stability.
End-to-End Project Leadership
Lead complex manufacturing projects from technical concept through execution, verification, and customer handover.
Establish clear project scope, objectives, milestones, responsibilities, risks, and performance measures.
Ensure projects are delivered against agreed timelines, quality standards, technical requirements, and customer expectations.
Manage project risks, escalations, stakeholder communication, and issue resolution.
Deliver measurable improvements across productivity, cost per part, quality, capacity, and long-term process stability.
Customer Engagement & Value Creation
Partner with customers to understand manufacturing challenges, operational constraints, and business priorities.
Translate technical recommendations into measurable operational and commercial value.
Lead customer reviews, technical presentations, and project steering discussions.
Develop trusted relationships with engineering, operational, and senior business stakeholders.
Identify opportunities for continuous improvement and further strategic collaboration.
Cross-Functional Collaboration
Coordinate multidisciplinary teams of application engineers, machining specialists, technical experts, suppliers, and strategic partners.
Provide technical direction and maintain alignment throughout project delivery.
Support local technical and commercial teams in developing sustainable customer capability.
Share technical knowledge, lessons learned, and project best practices across regional and international teams.
