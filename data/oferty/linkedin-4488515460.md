KPMG Global Trade & Customs is a specialized group in customs compliance. The team focuses on helping clients navigate the complex landscape of international trade regulations, identify cost-saving opportunities and manage compliance risks associated with supply chains and global trade operations. The team consist of experienced customs experts mainly in the EMEA market. When you become part of our team, we are committed to fostering your growth by offering a diverse array of training opportunities tailored to our employees. These training programs encompass various areas of expertise, including customs classification, free trade agreements, preferential origin as well as other pertinent fields that contribute to the advancement of your personal career trajectory.
Location: Warszawa
Region: mazowieckie
Job Description
supporting supplier solicitation for given Free Trade Agreement (FTA), including request preparation, supplier follow-up, response tracking, and escalation of overdue or incomplete submissions.
reviewing/preparing certificates of origin and proofs of origin, with particular emphasis on Long-Term Supplier Declarations (LTSDs).
analyzing bills of materials, product structures, supplier data, material origins, tariff classifications, values, and production information to assess preferential origin.
analyzing product-specific rules of origin, specific processing requirements, and combinations of rules.
determining and documenting whether products qualify for preferential origin under relevant Free Trade Agreement (FTA) programs and preferential trade arrangements.
identifying missing, inconsistent, or insufficient origin evidence and preparing clear clarification questions for suppliers, clients, or internal stakeholders.
maintaining solicitation and qualification trackers, monitoring deadlines and response rates, and providing regular status reporting.
meeting agreed productivity, quality, and timeliness targets while managing multiple countries, suppliers, products, and deadlines.
Requirements
at least 1 year of experience in customs and trade, with practical exposure to preferential origin, FTA qualification, supplier solicitation, or origin-document management
hands-on experience in supplier solicitation processes, including supplier communication, follow-up, document tracking, validation, and escalation
experience preparing or reviewing certificates and proofs of origin, particularly Long-Term Supplier Declarations (LTSDs)
a good understanding of preferential and non-preferential origin concepts and the supporting evidence required for origin claims
the ability to interpret and apply product-specific rules of origin under relevant FTA programs and preferential trade arrangements
strong attention to detail and the ability to identify gaps, contradictions, expired documents, incorrect product coverage, and unsupported origin claims
strong analytical and problem-solving skills, with the ability to document conclusions and maintain a clear audit trail
good written and verbal communication skills, with English at B1 level or above
good working knowledge of Microsoft Excel, including the ability to work efficiently with large datasets, apply formulas, filters, lookups, and perform basic data analysis
a proactive, organized, and collaborative approach, with the ability to manage time-sensitive tasks and communicate risks early
experience working directly with clients or suppliers is an advantage
experience with EU preferential origin frameworks is an advantage
