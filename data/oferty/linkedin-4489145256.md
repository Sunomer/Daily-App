We are looking for a Sourcing Specialist to join our new GE Aerospace manufacturing site in Poland, focused on production of turbine components for aircraft engines using precision machining and electrical discharge machining (EDM) technologies.
This role will report directly to the Sourcing Leader.
In the start-up phase of the site, the position will combine responsibilities across Sourcing and Logistics to support the launch of operations and ensure material availability, warehouse control, and smooth inbound/outbound flows. Over time, as the organization grows, the role is expected to transition into a position focused primarily on Sourcing.
The successful candidate will play a key role in ensuring continuity of production by securing direct and indirect materials, consumables, external processes, and other items required for both manufacturing operations and overall site readiness. This person will work with both internal and external suppliers, support supplier identification and selection, and help build a reliable supply base for a new aerospace production facility.
Job Description
Key Responsibilities:
Sourcing
Manage sourcing and purchasing activities for direct and indirect materials, including raw materials, consumables, external processing, tools, and other items required for production and site operations.
Ensure timely availability of all materials and services needed to support manufacturing schedules, operational continuity, and site readiness.
Cooperate with internal suppliers and external vendors to place orders, follow up on deliveries, and resolve supply-related issues.
Identify, evaluate, and select new suppliers in line with business needs, quality expectations, and delivery requirements.
Support the process of onboarding new external suppliers, including creation or extension of supplier records in relevant systems and databases.
Monitor supplier performance in terms of quality, delivery, responsiveness, and cost, and escalate risks when needed.
Partner with internal stakeholders such as production, planning, quality, engineering, and finance to align sourcing activities with operational priorities.
Logistics
Coordinate and execute inbound and outbound shipments, ensuring timely and accurate material flow to and from the site.
Manage day-to-day warehouse operations, including receipt, storage, issue, and movement of materials.
Perform goods receipt and material issuing transactions in the warehouse / ERP system with accuracy and timeliness.
Monitor inventory levels and warehouse stock status, identify shortages or replenishment needs, and raise purchase or supply requests as needed.
Maintain proper material identification, storage conditions, and stock accuracy in line with site standards and operational requirements.
Support logistics documentation and shipping activities, including packing, dispatch preparation, and coordination with transport providers.
Contribute to establishing and improving logistics and warehouse processes suitable for a newly launched manufacturing site.
Requirements
Experience in sourcing, purchasing, supply chain, logistics, or warehouse operations within a manufacturing environment.
Good understanding of procurement processes for both direct and indirect materials, as well as supplier coordination and order follow-up.
Practical experience in warehouse and logistics operations, including stock control, material transactions, and shipment handling.
Ability to work effectively in a dynamic, fast-changing environment typical of a new plant launch or growing operation.
Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and ability to manage multiple priorities across sourcing and logistics activities.
Good communication and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to cooperate across functions and with internal and external suppliers.
Working knowledge of ERP / warehouse management systems and good command of English.
