Role Purpose:
As a Service Delivery Manager, you will be the primary point of accountability for delivering exceptional service outcomes and building trusted customer relationships. You will ensure operational excellence, drive continuous improvement, and lead cross-functional teams to achieve contractual commitments while identifying opportunities for long-term business growth.
How You´ll Make an Impact:
Drive, manage and control performance and improvements in service delivery towards a customer;
Help and supervise internal support units and external service suppliers in achieving SLAs and KPIs required by contracts;
Lead and coach cross-functional teams in application of process improvements, project and change management industry standards and best practices;
Manage and maintain customer interactions on a regular basis – lead and participate in customer meetings, monitor performance reports and communications, provide responses to customer requests, etc.;
Interface with other cross-functional units such as governance, finance, sales and application centers providing the appropriate level of management and collaboration activities in order to achieve business goals;
Articulate the objectives of an assigned customer delivery project as they apply to the fulfillment of organization and individual objectives.
What Makes You a Great Fit:

Leadership skills - lead an account management team
Negotiation skills - explore new business opportunities with existing customers, conduct additional contractual negotiations
Financial management skills - manage budgets and profitability
Conflict-resolution skills
Ability to clearly formulate goals and objectives and communicate them to account team members
Ability to clearly prioritize, coordinate and streamline activities across different support functions
Ability to understand technical topics and general technology concepts on a high level
Motivation and self-drive
