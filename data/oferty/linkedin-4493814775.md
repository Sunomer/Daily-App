Vestas Technology & Operations > Procurement > Procurement Logistics
The Logistics Procurement Department is a vital part of the Global Procurement team, responsible for establishing and managing strategic partnerships with logistics suppliers across the globe. The department plays a critical role in ensuring that logistics capacity is secured and available to support business needs while maintaining the highest standards of safety, quality, service, and cost competitiveness.
As a global function, the Logistics Procurement team manages a broad range of logistics categories that enable the efficient movement of materials, components, and equipment throughout the Vestas value chain. These categories include Project Transport, covering vessel chartering, port operations, and road and rail transportation of oversized components, as well as General Freight & Logistics Services, including ocean freight, air freight, overland transportation, express and courier services, warehousing, and customs-related activities.
Responsibilities
Serve as the global subject matter expert for warehousing operations, driving best practices and ensuring alignment of warehouse solutions with business and regional requirements
Lead the global Warehousing & Customs category with a high degree of autonomy, taking ownership of sourcing decisions, supplier strategies, and operational improvements
Manage supplier relationships, including sourcing, tendering, contract negotiations, and performance management of warehouses, 3PLs, and customs brokers

Establish and track key performance indicators (KPIs), analytics, and reporting to measure supplier, cost, service, and customs performance
Drive continuous improvement, digitalization, and cross-functional collaboration to support efficient end-to-end supply chain and logistics operations
