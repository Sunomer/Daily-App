As a
Coding & Tracking (C&T) Manufacturing Process Owner,
you will lead the design, governance, continuous improvement, and global deployment of critical Coding & Tracking manufacturing processes. In this role, you will ensure regulatory compliance while driving process standardisation, operational excellence, and business value across a global manufacturing network.
As the global Process Owner, you will be accountable for end-to-end Coding & Tracking manufacturing processes, including process definition, governance, change management, continuous improvement, and solution development. You will work closely with Manufacturing, Engineering, IT, Quality, Supply Chain, Regulatory Affairs, and external partners to ensure compliant and sustainable solutions that support business objectives worldwide.
You will act as the central subject matter expert for Coding & Tracking manufacturing processes, driving standardisation, supporting implementation projects, managing process changes, and ensuring alignment with evolving regulatory requirements and industry standards.
Principle Accountabilities
Own and govern global Coding & Tracking manufacturing processes, ensuring they are standardized, documented, compliant, and effectively adopted across markets.
Drive process changes, continuous improvements, and knowledge transfer activities, ensuring sustainable process governance and successful organizational change management.
Act as the subject matter expert and 3^rd level support for Coding & Tracking manufacturing processes, providing advanced support, assessing change requests, and guiding implementation projects.
Own key process areas including Pack Coding, Product Aggregation, Coding & Labelling, Rework & Refeed, Repacking, Code Ordering, Anti-Tampering, Tier 2 Solutions, and OTP processes.
Lead or support implementation, development and continuous improvement projects, using structured project management methodologies to meet scope, budget, timeline, and quality standards
Manage business requirements, suppliers, risks, timelines, costs, compliance, and stakeholder communication.
Represent Imperial Brands in industry working groups, driving alignment with external standards, regulatory requirements, and best practices while identifying opportunities for innovation and process optimization.
Skills and Experience Required
Sound knowledge of Coding & Tracking Technology and Manufacturing Processes
At least 5 years of experience within Manufacturing functions, preferably in FMCG industry
Proven project management expertise, successfully leading cross-functional projects from concept to execution
Ability to work in international, cross-functional and cultural teams
Strong written and verbal communication skills (towards Senior Management); English fluent, other languages are considered as an advantage
Strong analytical, conceptual, and strategic thinking as well as planning skills
