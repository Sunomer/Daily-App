The Engineering Services department implements advanced projects in the field of industrial automation, and due to the dynamic development of new initiatives, we are looking for experienced automation engineers for projects in the intralogistics industry.
This is an opportunity for those who want to work with modern systems, have a real impact on project implementation, and develop in an international environment. The work is hybrid and involves business travel (about 60% of the time over the year) across Europe, the USA, and Canada — a great opportunity to gain global experience and work directly with clients.
We are looking for specialists with solid experience in programming PLC controllers and commissioning systems and production lines, who have carried out projects independently or as part of larger project teams. Intralogistics projects are an attractive and promising development direction, especially for engineers with experience in the automotive industry who want to expand their competencies and work with modern solutions that automate material flow.
Your tasks
Programming industrial controllers (Beckhoff, Siemens)
Commissioning industrial automation and robotics systems for clients in Poland and abroad
Participation in FAT/SAT tests and production support
Preparation of software, technical and project documentation with particular emphasis on safety requirements and industrial standards
Parameterization, commissioning, and optimization of industrial automation components – presses, vision systems, drives, etc.
Resolving current production line failures (if necessary)
Requirements
Experience working with PLC controllers (Siemens, Beckhoff, or Rockwell)
Willingness to travel for business approximately 60% of the time during the year, including to Hungary, Germany, the Czech Republic, the United Kingdom, Italy, the USA, and Canada
Higher technical education: Automation, Robotics, Mechatronics, or related fields
Minimum 3 years of experience in a similar position, preferably in the intralogistics or automotive industry
Knowledge of English at B2 level
Fluent knowledge of Polish is required
Must reside in Poland
Possession of a valid passport
Nice to have
Knowledge of additional controllers, e.g., Allen-Bradley, Omron, Mitsubishi
