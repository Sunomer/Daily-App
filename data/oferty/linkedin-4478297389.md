Senior Software Engineer — Build the AI-native platform modernising America's industrial supply chain. On-site, Warsaw.
Our client is a YC W25 company (top 5% growth in its batch) that has raised $10M+ from top-tier investors. Its platform makes aerospace, electronics, industrial and defense supply-chain operations nearly autonomous, and already runs at 7-figure ARR with ~$50M a week transacting through the system.
You'll join the Warsaw engineering team, working with the platform team and forward deployed engineers to build the systems customers run on, much of it integrating with legacy ERPs and email-driven workflows that move real orders every day.
What You'll Do
→ Build backend-leaning full-stack features end to end, from schema to UI (Svelte, TypeScript, Postgres)
→ Integrate with legacy ERPs and email-driven order workflows
→ Run several projects in parallel and get each one shipped
→ Travel up to one week per quarter to SF HQ or customer sites
What We're Looking For
→ 5+ years of genuine full-stack experience
→ A shipped full-stack product you can show (GitHub, live app or Loom)
→ Experience designing systems on your own, not just building to spec
→ Strong TypeScript, a frontend framework, and real SQL/Postgres depth beyond the ORM
→ Daily use of AI coding tools and a feel for where the frontier is
→ Fluent English, comfortable with ambiguity and working for young founders
What's on offer:
$100K–$120K base salary plus 0.01%–0.015% equity, full-time on-site in Warsaw.
