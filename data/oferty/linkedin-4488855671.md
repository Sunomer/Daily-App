Job Description
We are looking for an experienced
SAP Logistics Project Manager
to lead SAP logistics and supply chain transformation initiatives within a complex pharmaceutical environment. The role focuses on managing SAP implementation, integration, and optimisation projects across warehouse management, inventory management, procurement, and distribution processes.
You will be responsible for end-to-end project delivery, coordinating cross-functional teams, managing stakeholders, and ensuring SAP logistics solutions align with business requirements, operational objectives, and pharmaceutical regulatory standards.
Key Responsibilities
Lead end-to-end SAP logistics projects, including implementation, migration, integration, and optimisation initiatives.
Manage project scope, timelines, budgets, resources, risks, and deliverables to ensure successful execution.
Oversee SAP logistics workstreams involving SAP EWM, MM, WM, and related supply chain processes.
Coordinate cross-functional teams, including SAP functional consultants, technical specialists, business stakeholders, and external vendors.
Ensure alignment between SAP solutions and business requirements across warehouse management, procurement, inventory management, and distribution.
Manage integration dependencies between SAP logistics modules and other enterprise systems.
Lead project governance activities, including stakeholder reporting, risk management, and escalation processes.
Oversee testing, data migration, deployment, and go-live readiness activities.
Ensure project delivery complies with pharmaceutical industry regulations, including GxP and GDP requirements.
Support change management, business process improvements, and user adoption during SAP transformations.
Qualifications
Minimum 8 years of experience in IT or SAP project management, with a strong focus on logistics and supply chain projects.
Proven experience managing e
nd-to-end SAP logistics implementations
, rollouts, or transformation projects.
Strong understanding of SAP logistics modules, particularly SAP EWM, MM, WM, and their integration with other SAP solutions.
Experience managing SAP S/4HANA and/or SAP ECC projects.
Solid understanding of warehouse management, inventory management, procurement, and distribution processes.
Experience working within pharmaceutical or other regulated environments, with knowledge of GxP and GDP requirements.
Strong project management capabilities, including budget management, resource planning, risk mitigation, and vendor coordination.
Experience leading cross-functional teams and managing senior business and IT stakeholders.
Knowledge of project management methodologies, including Waterfall, Agile, and hybrid approaches.
Excellent communication, leadership, and problem-solving skills.

Preferred Qualifications
Experience with SAP S/4HANA logistics transformation programs.
Familiarity with SAP SD, PP, QM, or TM and their integration with logistics processes.
Experience with warehouse automation, supply chain integration, or distribution systems.
