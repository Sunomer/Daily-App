Role Summary
The Global Travel, Fleet & Workplace Performance Manager is a performance, governance, and analytics-focused role supporting the company's global Travel, Fleet, and Workplace programs.
Reporting to the Global Head of Real Estate & Corporate Services, the role is responsible for providing performance oversight, supplier management, reporting, compliance monitoring, and continuous improvement support across a global organization of approximately 11,000 employees.
The position serves as the primary coordinator for the global travel program, working closely with Global Shared Services, which manages travel approvals and ticketing activities, as well as with travel suppliers, HR Total Rewards, facility managers, and business stakeholders worldwide.
A core objective of the role is to transform data into actionable insights, improve visibility and governance, identify optimization opportunities, and support informed decision-making across Corporate Services. The successful candidate will combine strong analytical capabilities, business acumen, stakeholder management skills, and a continuous improvement mindset to enhance program performance, employee experience, and cost effectiveness.
Key Focus Areas
Travel program governance, supplier management, reporting, and performance oversight.
Fleet reporting and performance management in partnership with HR Total Rewards.
Workplace and Real Estate analytics, reporting, and portfolio insights.
Data-driven decision-making, compliance monitoring, and continuous improvement initiatives.
Global Travel Program Performance & Governance (Primary Scope)
Support the Global Head of Real Estate & Corporate Services in overseeing the global travel program, including governance, policy management, compliance monitoring, supplier performance, and continuous improvement initiatives.
Act as the primary business coordinator between Global Shared Services, travel suppliers, and internal stakeholders, ensuring effective service delivery, clear accountability, and a high-quality traveler experience.
Manage key travel suppliers and contracts, monitor service levels and performance indicators, and support sourcing, negotiation, and renewal activities.
Produce executive reporting, dashboards, and analytical insights to monitor travel spend, compliance, traveler experience, sustainability metrics, and optimization opportunities, supporting informed business decisions and cost management.
Fleet Performance Management
Support the management and monitoring of the global fleet program in partnership with HR Total Rewards and Procurement, ensuring accurate and up-to-date fleet data, reporting, and key performance indicators.
Collect and consolidate fleet data covering costs, vehicle utilization, sustainability performance, and renewal cycles, and support the maintenance and review of fleet catalogues.
Prepare regular reports and analyses to support fleet monitoring and provide relevant information to stakeholders.
Real Estate & Workplace Analytics
Maintain and enhance workplace and real estate data, ensuring consistency, quality, and visibility across the global portfolio.
Deliver reporting and analytical insights covering occupancy, utilization, lease obligations, operating costs, and portfolio performance.
Support workplace optimization initiatives, real estate projects, lease management activities, budgeting, and strategic decision-making through robust data analysis and performance reporting.
Global Coordination & Continuous Improvement
Coordinate a global network of facility managers and local stakeholders, promoting consistent processes, governance standards, reporting methodologies, and best practices.
Drive automation, simplification, data governance, and continuous improvement initiatives across Travel, Fleet, Workplace, and Corporate Services activities.
Support the implementation and enhancement of systems, tools, and reporting capabilities that improve efficiency, visibility, and decision-making.
We are looking for a highly analytical and adaptable professional who is comfortable operating in a dynamic international environment and collaborating with a wide range of stakeholders across Corporate Services, HR, Finance, IT, Procurement, Shared Services, and local operations. The successful candidate will be fluent in English, with strong communication and influencing skills and the ability to engage effectively with both operational teams and senior management.
The ideal candidate combines strong business acumen with excellent data management and reporting capabilities. They are highly proficient in Excel, comfortable working with large datasets from multiple systems, and able to transform complex information into clear, actionable insights and meaningful management reporting. A structured, proactive, and continuous improvement mindset, together with the ability to identify trends, challenge assumptions, and support data-driven decision-making, will be key to success in this role.
