We are looking for a skilled System Analyst to join our team and work on exciting mobile application projects. You will be responsible for conducting business and system analysis, creating analytical documentation, and supporting development and testing processes. This role involves collaborating with various stakeholders to translate business needs into technical requirements for development teams. The position is hybrid (one day per week in the office), allowing for flexibility between remote work and office presence in Warsaw. If you are passionate about mobile apps and have a strong analytical mindset, this opportunity may be the right fit for you.
Your tasks
Conducting business and system analysis for mobile applications
Creating analytical documentation to support development and testing
Translating business needs into requirements for development, QA, UX/UI, and backend teams
Preparing documentation for mobile application screens and API integrations
Modeling processes, flow diagrams, and sequence diagrams
Utilizing tools such as Jira, Confluence, and Bitbucket for project management
Applying Agile/Scrum methodologies in project execution
Analyzing logs and diagnosing errors using tools like Kibana
Defining and analyzing analytical events in mobile applications, including GA4
Collaborating with UX/UI teams and analyzing projects in Figma
Requirements
Minimum 4 years of experience as a Business/System Analyst in mobile application projects
Experience in mobile projects (preferably native applications for iOS and Android)
Ability to gather, analyze, clarify, and document business and system requirements
Experience in preparing documentation for mobile application screens
Previous work with API documentation and system integration analysis, including REST and SOAP
Familiarity with process modeling, flow diagrams, and sequence diagrams
Knowledge of Agile/Scrum methodologies
Practical experience with log analysis and error diagnosis tools
Understanding of Firebase and its application in mobile development
Strong communication skills and experience collaborating with various stakeholders
Fluent Polish
Residency in Poland
Nice to have
Previous work in the financial, insurance, or banking sector
Familiarity with investment products, policies, or self-service offerings
Experience analyzing production deployments, incidents, and user behavior post-release
