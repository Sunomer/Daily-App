Job Summary:
The Demand Planner is responsible for creating and maintaining an accurate demand plan that aligns customer requirements, commercial objectives, and supply chain capabilities. The role drives forecast accuracy, supports the S&OP process, minimizes inventory risks, and enables efficient decision-making through data analysis and collaboration with Sales, Marketing, Supply Chain, and Operations.
Key Responsibilities:
Develop short-term, medium-term, and long-term demand forecasts by analyzing historical data, market trends, seasonality, and customer behavior.
Maintain forecast accuracy and identify forecast risks and opportunities.
Manage the demand planning process and forecasting tools.
Coordinate forecast updates with stakeholders.
Work closely with Sales, Marketing, Supply Planning, Production to gather information about product launches, customer projects, and market changes.
Prepare demand planning reports for sales review.
Support S&OP (Sales and Operations Planning) processes by attending monthly Pre-S& OP calls.
Monitor stock levels, excess inventory, slow-moving items, and shortages.
Recommend actions to reduce inventory risks.
Manage demand forecasting for new product introductions.
Plan phase-ins, phase-outs, and product substitutions.
Monitor demand during product transitions.
Ensure forecast-related master data is accurate and maintained.
Identify and resolve data quality issues.
