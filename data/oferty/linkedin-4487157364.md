Overview
The role provides senior technical leadership and leads a team of subject matter experts being accountable for Operational Technology (OT), Control & Information Systems (C&IS), CyberSecurity and Digital Manufacturing capabilities impacting the Food & Bev capital investments (growth, productivity, automation, compliance) executed across EMEA, spanning +100 Food/Bev plants and Warehouse operations.
The role leads the implementation of Control System Architecture across all capital projects enabling integration and standardisation as per Pep Operational Technology (OT) global strategy while covering a portfolio of different Food process platforms (PC, Stax, TC, Extruded, Oats & RBS etc.) along with Primary/Secondary Packaging, and Bev process platforms (PET, Can, RGB across CSD and NCB)
