Job Description
Key Responsibilities
Health & Safety System & Governance
Support the development, implementation, and continuous improvement of the Health & Safety management system across multiple AUTODOC locations.
Coordinate and harmonize H&S standards internationally, ensuring alignment between corporate requirements and local legal frameworks.
Monitor, analyze, and report H&S performance indicators and compliance status.
Maintain and further develop H&S documentation, processes, and reporting structures.
Organize and participate in the mandatory quarterly safety committee meetings (ASA-Sitzungen / Arbeitsschutzausschuss) for the German sites, as required by ASiG § 11.
International Compliance & Service Provider Coordination
Act as the primary coordination point between AUTODOC and external H&S service providers in different countries.
Review and assess local compliance status, identify gaps, and initiate corrective measures where required.
Ensure that local legal requirements are properly implemented and documented.
Support audits, inspections, and regulatory interactions in cooperation with local stakeholders.
Operational & Strategic Support
Participate in incident and accident investigations, root cause analyses, and risk assessments.
Support the implementation of training programs and awareness initiatives for employees and management.
Contribute to strategic H&S initiatives at global level.
Provide expert guidance to internal stakeholders regarding occupational safety, health management, and environmental protection topics.
Qualifications
What We Expect
Minimum 3 years of professional experience in Health & Safety, ideally within logistics, automotive, warehouse operations, or comparable industrial environments.
Proven experience in coordinating or managing H&S topics across multiple sites or countries.
Official certification as a Fachkraft für Arbeitssicherheit (FaSi) or Sicherheitsingenieur, as legally required under German law (ASiG § 7 and DGUV V2) to support the German warehouses.
Strong understanding of legal H&S compliance frameworks and practical implementation.
Experience working with external service providers and regulatory authorities.
Analytical mindset with the ability to identify compliance gaps and implement structured solutions.
Strong communication skills and the ability to operate confidently in an international environment.
Fluency in English and German (additional languages are a plus), as this role actively supports our Warehouse in Berlin.
Willingness to travel occasionally.
Nice to Have
Additional qualifications as a Fire Protection Officer (Brandschutzbeauftragter) and/or Dangerous Goods Officer (Gefahrgutbeauftragter).
Knowledge of, or auditor certification in, occupational health and safety management systems, particularly ISO 45001.
Familiarity with related international standards such as ISO 14001 (Environmental Management) and ISO 9001 (Quality Management).
