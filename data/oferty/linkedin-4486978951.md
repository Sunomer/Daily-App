Job Responsibilities / 岗位职责
1. Warehouse Operations Management / 仓库运营管理
Oversee daily warehouse operations, including receiving, put-away, picking, packing, dispatching, and returns processing.
负责仓库日常运营管理，包括货物收货、上架、拣货、打包、出库及退货处理等工作。
2. Team Management / 团队管理
Supervise and coordinate warehouse staff, allocate daily tasks, monitor performance, and ensure efficient execution of warehouse activities.
负责仓库团队的日常管理与工作安排，合理分配任务，监督员工工作表现，确保各项仓库作业高效执行。
3. Inventory Control / 库存管理
Ensure inventory accuracy through regular stock checks, cycle counts, and inventory reconciliation. Investigate and resolve stock discrepancies.
负责库存管理及准确性维护，定期组织盘点和库存核对，及时调查并处理库存差异。
4. Process & Performance Improvement / 流程与效率优化
Monitor warehouse productivity and operational performance, identify inefficiencies, and implement process improvements to enhance operational efficiency.
监控仓库运营效率及各项绩效指标，识别运营问题并推动流程优化，持续提升仓库作业效率。
5. Safety & Quality Management / 安全与质量管理
Ensure compliance with warehouse safety regulations, operational procedures, and quality standards. Maintain a clean, organized, and safe working environment.
确保仓库作业符合安全规范、操作流程及质量标准，维护整洁、有序、安全的仓库工作环境。
6. Coordination & Reporting / 沟通协调与工作汇报
Coordinate with transportation, customer service, and other departments to ensure smooth inbound and outbound operations. Prepare regular operational reports and escalate issues when necessary.
协调运输、客户服务及其他相关部门，确保货物进出库流程顺畅，并定期汇报仓库运营情况，及时反馈和处理相关问题。
Requirements / 任职要求
Previous experience in warehouse operations or warehouse supervision; team management experience is preferred.
具备仓库运营或仓库管理相关工作经验，有团队管理经验者优先。
Good understanding of warehouse processes, inventory management, and logistics operations.
熟悉仓库操作流程、库存管理及物流运营相关工作。
Strong leadership, communication, coordination, and problem-solving skills.
具备良好的团队领导能力、沟通协调能力及问题解决能力。
Proficient in Microsoft Office; experience with WMS, ERP, or other warehouse management systems is preferred.
熟练使用办公软件，有 WMS、ERP 或其他仓库管理系统操作经验者优先。
Responsible, detail-oriented, and capable of working in a fast-paced operational environment.
工作认真负责，注重细节，能够适应快节奏的仓库运营环境。
