As a Senior Business Architect in Strategic Customers Integration Team, you will work closely with business stakeholders, customers, and technical teams to design and implement integration solutions. In this role, you will own projects end-to-end, from requirements gathering and solution design through implementation, testing, deployment and hypercare.
Your responsibilities:
Lead complex EDI integration projects from initial analysis through implementation and hypercare
Analyze DSV customers’ requirements and prepare technical specifications
Analyze and estimate the work effort to implement new solutions
Creation or modification of complex interfaces based on existing standard solutions, ensuring high quality using test scenarios
Design, create, and modify complex integration interfaces based on existing standards and best practices
Maintain timely, efficient and proactive stakeholder communication regarding the daily tasks and projects
Support business and the organization to identify any possible cooperation with the EDI department
Collaborate with colleagues and share knowledge to support team development and success
Professional skills development by participation in training and constant self-education
Our requirements:
Minimum 3 years of experience in EDI integration projects
Extensive expertise in EDI standards, particularly UN/EDIFACT and ANSI-X12, demonstrating the ability to navigate complex EDI landscapes
Solid understanding of EDI transactions and integration processes within transport and logistics environments
Advanced knowledge of XML and XPATH
Vast experience implementing IT projects with Agile methods
Knowledge of communication protocols such as FTP, SFTP, AS2 or HTTP
Strong analytical thinking, effective problem-solving and stakeholder collaboration
Ability to multi-task, plan, prioritize and manage time effectively in order to meet goals and achieve results
Excellent stakeholder management and communication skills
In-depth knowledge of communication protocols critical to EDI processes, including API, FTP, SFTP, and AS2, with proven hands-on implementation experience
Professional proficiency in English, both written and spoken
