Business Transformation Expert
About the Role
As a Business Transformation Expert, you will be the execution backbone of the Business Transformation Office (BTO). You will support Transformation Leads across the full project lifecycle — from discovery and analysis, through design, to delivery and post-go-live stabilisation. Your focus will be on coordination, documentation, data gathering, tracking and reporting, ensuring that transformation initiatives run smoothly, on time, and with high-quality artefacts. This is a hands-on role for someone who is highly organised, detail-oriented, and eager to develop into a future Transformation Lead.
What You'll Do
1. Project Coordination
Coordinate day-to-day activities across assigned transformation workstreams, tracking tasks, owners, deadlines, dependencies, and risks.
Maintain project plans, RAID logs (Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies), action trackers, and decision logs.
Organise and prepare workshops, working sessions, and stakeholder meetings — including agendas, pre-reads, logistics, and follow-ups.
Capture accurate meeting minutes, decisions, and action items; follow up with owners to ensure timely closure.
Support onboarding of new team members and external consultants to the project environment (tools, templates, ways of working).
2. Documentation and Process Artefacts
Draft, format, and maintain transformation documentation: process maps, SOPs, RACI matrices, playbooks, business requirement documents, and knowledge transfer materials.
Support process discovery by shadowing SMEs, taking structured notes, and translating them into first-draft process maps (BPMN) or narrative descriptions.
Maintain the BTO template library, ensuring standards, version control, and consistency across projects.
Prepare and format executive presentations, status decks, and steering committee materials under the guidance of Transformation Leads.
Ensure all project artefacts are stored, tagged, and accessible in the agreed repositories (SharePoint, Teams, project tools).
3. Data, Tracking and Reporting
Collect, clean, and consolidate operational and financial data required for baselining, business cases, and benefit tracking.
Support the preparation of business cases by gathering inputs, populating financial models, and running basic sensitivity analyses under supervision.
Produce weekly and monthly status reports, dashboards, and KPI trackers for individual projects and the BTO portfolio.
Track realised savings, efficiency gains, and automation benefits versus baseline; flag deviations early.
Maintain the BTO project pipeline and portfolio tracker, keeping status, RAG, milestones, and financials up to date.
What You'll Need
2–4 years of professional experience in a PMO, business analyst, process improvement, transformation support.
Strong organisational skills with the ability to manage multiple parallel workstreams, deadlines, and stakeholders.
High attention to detail and a genuine care for the quality of documentation and data.
Solid proficiency in MS Office and comfortable using GenAI tools (e.g. Copilot, Claude) to accelerate research, drafting, and analysis.
Strong written and spoken English — able to draft clear meeting notes, status updates, and first-draft executive materials.
Foundational understanding of Lean / Six Sigma concepts (Yellow or Green Belt is a plus, not required).
