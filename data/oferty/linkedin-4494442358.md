The Impact You'll Make
Are you an experienced leader who enjoys combining people management with logistics process optimization? Would you like to make a real impact on warehouse performance and contribute to the development of modern logistics operations?
As our
Warehouse Manager
, you’ll play a key role in managing and developing warehouse operations at our Grodzisk Mazowiecki site. You’ll be responsible for ensuring efficient material flow, high team productivity, and compliance with the highest safety and quality standards.
Your impact? Through your leadership, analytical skills, and continuous improvement mindset, you’ll help us optimize warehouse processes, manage operational costs, and ensure timely and reliable deliveries.
You’ll collaborate with various functions across the organization, develop your team’s capabilities, and implement solutions that support the ongoing improvement of our logistics operations.
What You’ll Be Doing
Lead and develop the warehouse team: Manage and develop warehouse employees, coordinate work schedules and resource allocation, maintain the competence matrix, and support employee motivation and performance evaluations, working closely with shift coordinators.
Oversee warehouse operations: Ensure smooth and efficient inbound, internal supply, and outbound processes, monitor inventory accuracy, and maintain proper warehouse documentation and transactions within the Warehouse Management System (WMS).
Drive team productivity and performance: Monitor key operational KPIs, analyze results, ensure achievement of organizational targets, and initiate corrective actions and improvement plans whenever performance deviates from expectations.
Lead continuous improvement initiatives: Identify opportunities to improve logistics processes, implement Lean Management practices, and optimize operational costs, productivity, and delivery performance.
Ensure operational standards and support technological development: Maintain high standards of health and safety, 5S, ISO 9001, and ISO 14001 compliance, oversee the proper functioning of WMS, manage expenses within budget, and support the implementation of new technologies, including warehouse automation and robotics.
What We're Looking For
A university degree, preferably in Logistics, Supply Chain Management, Business Administration, Engineering, or a related field.
At least 5 years of experience in a similar warehouse or logistics management position, with a proven track record of leading teams.
Strong knowledge of warehouse operations, Warehouse Management Systems (WMS), and SAP, particularly warehouse-related functionalities; experience with SAP EWM will be an advantage.
Good understanding of Lean Management, continuous improvement methodologies, and quality and safety standards, including 5S, ISO 9001, and ISO 14001.
Very good knowledge of Microsoft Office, strong analytical skills with the ability to interpret operational data and KPIs, and intermediate English proficiency
Hands-on experience with warehouse automation and digital transformation is a strong advantage
What You'll Get From Us
Opportunities to grow: We support your professional development through training, mentoring, and opportunities to advance your career within our global organization.
Ownership and real impact: You’ll have the autonomy to make decisions, bring your ideas to life, and drive improvements that directly contribute to warehouse efficiency and business performance.
A supportive and inclusive environment: We create a workplace where your ideas are valued, collaboration matters, and your well-being is important to us.
An attractive benefits package: Enjoy an annual bonus, private medical care with Lux Med, life insurance, an Employee Pension Programme (PPE), co-financing of sports cards, and subsidized meals in our company canteen.
Global opportunities: Become part of an international organization where you can collaborate with experts across different countries, share knowledge, and learn from diverse experiences.
