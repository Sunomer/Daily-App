We are looking for a Senior Process Engineer to join a project focused on process mapping and material flow analysis at a manufacturing site in Szczecin. In this role, you will assess current production and logistics processes, identify automation opportunities, and define operational requirements for autonomous material transport solutions. You will collaborate closely with production, logistics, and engineering teams to drive improvements and support the implementation of AGV/AMR technologies. This is a short-term assignment and requires you to be based at the office in Szczecin for approximately three weeks. If you are passionate about optimizing processes and enhancing operational efficiency, this opportunity may be the right fit for you.
Your tasks
Mapping current production, logistics, and material flow processes on the shop floor
Collecting and analyzing data such as transport routes, material volumes, cycle times, frequencies, and handling methods
Identifying suitable processes and use cases for AGV/AMR automation
Defining process and operational requirements for the AGV/AMR solution
Working closely with production, logistics, and engineering teams during on-site workshops and observations
Supporting the preparation of the future-state process and material flow concept
Requirements
Minimum 5 years of experience in process engineering
Strong expertise in process mapping and optimization
Practical experience in analyzing production and logistics processes
Willingness to work full-time from the office in Szczecin for short period of time (around 3 weeks)
Ability to define operational requirements for automation solutions
Excellent analytical skills and attention to detail
Fluent Polish and residency in Poland
Nice to have
Familiarity with AGV and AMR technologies
Experience with Digital Twin concepts
