About us
bolttech is an international insurtech with a mission to build the world’s leading, technology-enabled ecosystem for protection and insurance. With a full suite of digital and data-driven capabilities, bolttech powers connections between insurers, distributors, and customers to make it easier and more efficient to buy and sell insurance and protection products.
A part of Pacific Century Group, bolttech serves customers in multiple markets across North America, Asia and Europe.
In this position you will…
...be commercially minded Retail Marketing Specialist to implement retail marketing and point-of-sale activities across the iMad and Luxtrade stores network. In this role, you will turn marketing campaigns into consistent, high-quality in-store experiences, optimize product displays and merchandising, and deliver activations that increase store traffic and support sales. Working closely with store managers, sales teams, Marketing and external partners, you will monitor execution, analyze results and recommend practical improvements to the customer experience.
You will be responsible for:
Planning store visits and coordinating marketing activities across the retail network.
Implementing, monitoring, and continuously improving merchandising standards, product displays, and POS materials.
Planning and executing retail marketing activities for the
iMad (Apple Premium Reseller)
and
LuxTrade (Refurbished Electronics Retailer)
brands to support sales, increase store traffic, and enhance customer experience.
Coordinating the distribution and implementation of marketing materials across retail locations while ensuring consistent brand communication.
Collaborating with the Marketing team to create and optimize campaign materials, POS assets, and social media content.
Organizing events, product launches, and marketing activations in cooperation with stores and external partners.
Supporting store managers and sales teams in the rollout of new products, campaigns, and communication standards.
Conducting audits and analyzing the quality of product displays, campaign execution, and the availability of marketing materials.
Analyzing the effectiveness of marketing initiatives and recommending improvements to support sales performance and increase store traffic.
To be successful you will require the following competencies & skills:
2-3 years of experience in retail marketing, point-of-sale marketing, merchandising or a related field.
Experience working with a store network and physical retail locations.
Practical knowledge of point-of-sale materials, merchandising principles, customer journeys and product display standards.
Ability to plan retail marketing activities and evaluate their effectiveness.
Strong analytical skills, with the ability to interpret sales and marketing data and translate findings into improvements.
Excellent organizational skills, independence and the ability to manage field activities effectively.
Strong relationship-building and stakeholder management skills, with the ability to collaborate with store teams, internal functions and external partners.
A strong eye for visual detail and the ability to maintain consistent brand and display standards.
Category B driving licence and willingness to travel regularly to stores.
English at B1 level or above.
Experience in consumer electronics, home appliances or telecommunications, and experience organizing events, would be an advantage.
What we offer:
A high level of autonomy and freedom in a role responsible for marketing and communication across the retail network.
The opportunity to bring your own ideas to life and have a real impact on store appearance, marketing activities, and customer experience.
Opportunities for professional growth in retail marketing, merchandising, and event management through close collaboration with Sales and Marketing teams.
A hybrid work model (3 days in the office and 2 days remote for Warsaw-based employees) and flexible working hours (start between 7:00 AM and 9:00 AM).
Additional paid time off (10 days of care leave plus a day off for your birthday).
Shortened working hours on Fridays (until 1:45 PM).
