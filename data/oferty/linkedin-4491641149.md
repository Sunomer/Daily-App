The
Supplier Performance Lead
is responsible for ensuring there are no customer disruptions due to the „Raw Material (consumables or components)" supply base. Activities include, ongoing risk management and mitigation, supplier collaboration and capacity review. The key metric ownership being supplier delivery performance and all associated activities.
Collaborate with the sourcing team in development and execution of the sourcing strategy to secure expected performance.
What You’ll Do
Manage and ensure the accuracy of the ERP data related with assigned categories/sub-categories
Conduct the RfI/RfP/RfQ of sourcing projects, collect the commercial data for sourcing decision making. Preliminary analyze the offers and provide the proposal on total cost ownership
Execute and optimize the document management and control, such as contracts/ agreements, RfI/RfP/RfQ package, offers, pricing records, sample orders, and other purchasing commercial documents/records
Follow up the implementation activities of sourcing projects with suppliers and SDE. Report the discrepancies of execution to GCM/GCL
Monitor the data/documents in public or share folders for assigned categories/sub-categories. Ensure the completion and accuracy of project management documents following the sourcing process
Lead - Capacity and Forecast reviews with key strategic suppliers and verify capability
Lead - Ongoing supply risk reviews as part of weekly planning process, working with (PD, Sourcing, GDM)
Develop and Manage ongoing Supplier Relationship Management
Additional projects or activities as assigned by GDM management team.
Direct Responsibility for Delivery Performance by technology and root cause review and corrective actions
Management of invoicing and pricing queries
Local Negotiation Pricing of "Non-Strategic" suppliers
