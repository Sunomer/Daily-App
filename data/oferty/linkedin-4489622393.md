Job Description
Are You Ready to Make It Happen at Mondelēz International?
Join our Mission to Lead the Future of Snacking. Make It Uniquely Yours.
As Global Digital Smart Factory Operations Technical Lead you will lead the end-to-end Service Delivery for the IoT Platform and Applications. Act as the single point of contact for all support related activities bridging end users, plant-level IT, Engineering, IoT Center of Excellence, Deployment teams, and third-party integrators. The role is part of the MDS Digital Smart Factory organization.
How You Will Contribute
You will:
Lead the end-to-end Service Delivery ensuring compliance to SLA’s, high availability and reliability of IoT Platform and Applications
Own the continuous improvement backlog and ensure timely development and deployment of CIs into the production environment.
Own the end-to-end release management lifecycle (Dev, Test, Stage, Prod) & process using DevOps for deploying new features/functionalities into the live environment.
Define & establish standardized ways of working, identify and implement improvement opportunities to strengthen the IoT Ops organization & processes.
Knowledge Management: Own the knowledge management and capability building of the IoT Ops organization
What You Will Bring
A desire to drive your future and accelerate your career. You will bring experience and knowledge in:
Education:
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science/Information Technology or related field
Experience:
7+ years in IT Service Delivery Management preferably in manufacturing (CPG preferred) processes and systems.
Understanding of digital manufacturing applications, Industry 4.0 solutions would be an added advantage (CPG preferred)
Demonstrated experience leading a support organization in a large-scale global digital manufacturing program
Technical:
Knowledge in AWS/Azure platform integration services with applications, other platforms and automation required.
Experience with AWS, Kubernetes, and container-based edge computing architecture
Experience with DevOps (GitHub pipelines, Helmchart, repos)
SAP ECC/S4H Manufacturing modules (PP/PI, QM, WM, IM, PM) exposure or knowledge required.
Others:
Strong leadership, communication and problem-solving skills
Ability to coordinate and supervise the work of across multiple workstream (incidents, continuous improvements) running in parallel.
Strong communication and interpersonal skills; ability to work effectively with global teams
Work in an international environment with some travel.
Language Requirement: English Advanced (Fluent in written and spoken English)
No Relocation support available
