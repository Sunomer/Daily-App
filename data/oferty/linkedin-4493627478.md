About Franke
We, the Franke company with its three divisions, are a world's leading supplier of products and services for domestic kitchens, the professional foodservice systems and the convenience store sector as well as for professional coffee making. With around 60 companies, we are at home on five continents. Over 8,000 employees constantly take on new perspectives to identify opportunities and turn them into inspiring, innovative solutions for tomorrow’s demands.
About The Job
As an SAP Supply Chain Consultant, you will support Franke's supply chain transformation journey with a focus on planning and logistics processes. You will contribute to global projects, rollouts, continuous improvement initiatives, and process optimization across Demand Planning, Supply Planning, Production Planning, Warehouse Management, Quality Management, and Transportation Management. You will play an active role in shaping future supply chain capabilities and driving process excellence across the organization.
Your tasks
Support the implementation and global rollout of SAP Supply Chain solutions.
Analyze business requirements and translate them into scalable SAP solutions.
Contribute to solution design, configuration, testing, and go-live activities.
Drive process improvements across planning, logistics, warehousing, and transportation.
Partner with Key Users and Global Process Owners to understand business needs and provide consulting support.
Act as a trusted link between business and IT, supporting transformation and harmonization initiatives.
Support incident resolution, maintain process documentation, and ensure stable operations through continuous improvement.
Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Information Technology, Supply Chain Management, Logistics, Business Information Systems, etc.
Experience with SAP Supply Chain solutions.
Hands-on experience in SAP implementation projects.
Knowledge of planning and logistics processes.
Understanding of end-to-end supply chain processes and their integration points.
Fluent in English; German and/or Italian are an advantage..
What we offer you
Stable employment based on an employment contract (umowa o pracę).
Home office options (2 days per week in Warsaw office), flexible working hours.
Remote work from anywhere (up to 20 days per year).
Social benefits package: Medicover medical care, MultiSport card, life insurance, co-financing of glasses, holiday fund, birth or adoption allowance, discount for Franke products and more options available on out benefits' platform.
Essential introductory trainings and access to additional development trainings.
