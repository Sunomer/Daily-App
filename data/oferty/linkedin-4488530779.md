Purpose of position
As the Modern Workplace Champion, you will help Awin achieve more by increasing awareness, confidence and effective use of our productivity and AI tools, mainly Microsoft 365, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and Asana. You will translate technology into practical ways of working, lead adoption activity, create learning experiences, and help colleagues use AI safely and responsibly in a secure hybrid environment.
As a trusted adviser on AI-enabled productivity, you will work across technical and non-technical teams to identify valuable use cases, remove barriers to adoption and turn new capabilities into measurable business outcomes.
Key tasks
Promote Awin’s approved productivity and AI tools, showing how they work together to improve everyday work, with a primary focus on Microsoft 365, Microsoft Copilot, Claude and Asana.
Develop and maintain an adoption and training strategy that addresses barriers to productivity, communication, collaboration and knowledge-sharing.
Own and coordinate adoption campaigns for new capabilities, including communications, demonstrations, workshops, office hours and role-based learning.
Identify and prioritise high-value AI use cases with business teams, support pilots, and help successful practices become repeatable ways of working.
Stay informed about developments across Microsoft 365, Copilot, Claude, Asana and the wider approved AI landscape; assess relevance and communicate meaningful changes to the business.
Create practical learning content for Copilot and Claude, including prompt guidance, playbooks, FAQs and scenario-based training for different functions and levels of AI confidence.
Coach colleagues and champions on effective prompting, critical review of AI output, human oversight and safe handling of company information.
Build and support a network of workplace and AI champions who share good practice and reinforce responsible adoption across teams and territories.
Partner with Information Security, Privacy and Legal so that AI adoption follows approved governance, data protection and responsible-use requirements.
Track developments in the core tools and the wider AI landscape, working with tool owners to communicate relevant changes to the business.
Define and report adoption measures, such as participation, active and repeat use, user confidence and use-case value, and use them to guide priorities.
Create and maintain end-user documentation for core productivity and AI tools.
Work with the IT Support and System Administrator teams to capture recurring issues and improve self-service content.
